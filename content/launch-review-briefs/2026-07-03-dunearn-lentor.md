## CONTENT ANGLES

1. **"First-mover vs later-mover" framing** — Dunearn House at $2,900–$3,100 psf vs Plot 2 at an estimated $3,200–$3,300 psf (2H 2027). Buyers who enter now get the same CRL/masterplan tailwind at a $150–$250 psf discount = $180,000–$300,000 on a 3BR. Strong investor-mindset angle.

2. **"The 33-year supply gap"** — Last non-landed launch in this subzone was in the early 1990s. This is a genuinely scarce supply event. Strong emotional hook for Bukit Timah families.

3. **"99-year LH at near-$3,000 psf: the honest conversation"** — Most agents will avoid this. Winfred can own the contrarian, honest take: this is the #1 objection buyers will have; here's how to think through it. Positions him as trustworthy over promotional.

4. **"School zone deep dive"** — MGS Primary proximity is a major marketing pillar across all sites. Winfred can do a data-led post clarifying the straight-line vs road distance nuance, what the MOE ballot process actually involves, and why "within 1km" isn't a guarantee. High-value content for family buyers.

5. **"What the Turf City masterplan actually means in timeline terms"** — 20–30 years is a long hold. Most marketing sites breeze past this. A frank breakdown of what will be built by 2030, 2035, 2040 would be distinctive.

6. **Stack selection matters** — PropertyNet.sg and New Launches Review both flag this. A short post or carousel on how to pick a stack in a 19-storey block at Turf City (views, construction dust, road noise) would be genuinely useful and shareable.
