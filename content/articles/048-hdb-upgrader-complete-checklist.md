# HDB Upgrader's Checklist: Sell First, Buy Second

You own a 5-room HDB. You want to upgrade to a private condo. One wrong move—buying before selling, missing MSR windows, or CPF miscalculation—costs $50-100k in lost opportunity or forced fire sales. This checklist keeps you on track.

## The Sequence (Critical)

**Phase 1: Prepare to sell (2-3 months before)**
- [ ] Get HDB valuation (portal.hdb.gov.sg or agent estimate)
- [ ] Calculate net proceeds: sale price − agent fee (2-2.5%) − legal ($1.5-2k) − ABSD (if <5 years) − mortgage payoff = **net cash**
- [ ] Confirm CPF refund amount (CPF Board statement)
- [ ] List HDB; target 4-8 week sale

**Phase 2: Parallel preparation (while HDB listed)**
- [ ] Pre-approve for private property mortgage (LTV limits apply)
- [ ] Identify 3-5 private properties in price range
- [ ] Check MSR/TDSR qualification (your income vs total debt)

**Phase 3: Trigger sale (when HDB offer accepted)**
- [ ] Schedule HDB closing in 4-6 weeks
- [ ] Make offer on private property *contingent on HDB closing* (agent includes this clause)
- [ ] **Never** close private before HDB closes (you'll carry both mortgages)

**Phase 4: Close HDB, immediately close private (1-2 weeks apart)**
- [ ] HDB settlement: receive net proceeds + CPF refund
- [ ] Use proceeds as down payment for private property
- [ ] Private closing: new mortgage kicks in

## The Money: HDB Proceeds → Private Down Payment

**Example: 5-room HDB to 4-room condo**

HDB sale: $550k
- Agent (2.25%): −$12,375
- Legal: −$1,800
- ABSD (if <5y): −$0 (HDB exempt)
- Mortgage payoff: −$300,000
- **Net: $235,825**

CPF refund: +$180,000 (if you used CPF to buy)

**Total down payment available: $415,825**

Target condo: $800k
- Down payment (52%): $415,825 ✓
- Mortgage needed: $384,175 (48%)
- MSR on $384,175: $1,230/month (3.8% rate)

Your monthly income needed: **$3,075** (assuming 40% TDSR cap)

## The Timing Trap: MSR Windows

**Mistake 1: Buying private before HDB sells**

You close private (take $800k mortgage) while HDB still pending. Your TDSR instantly jumps:
- Private mortgage: $384,175 → $1,230/month
- HDB mortgage: $300,000 → $960/month
- **Total: $2,190/month** (vs. $1,230 if you'd waited)

Your TDSR: 73% (exceeds 60% cap). **Mortgage gets denied or reduced.**

**Mistake 2: Time gap (selling HDB, then hunting for condo)**

You sell HDB in June, close in August. But condo prices moved; your budget is now 10% below market. You settle for inferior unit or pay 5% premium.

**Solution:** Parallel hunt. Find condo *before* HDB closes. Negotiate contingency clause with seller. Close HDB → private in 1-2 week sequence.

## CPF Rules (The Hidden $180-250k)

**How much CPF comes back?**

When you sell HDB, CPF Board refunds your original contribution + accrued interest, minus:
- Amount still owed on HDB mortgage (if any)
- Minimum $20k "retirement account" retention (mandatory)

**Example:**
- Original CPF used to buy: $120,000
- Accrued interest: +$60,000
- Mortgage still owed: −$0
- **Refund: $180,000** (less $20k retention = $160k liquid)

**Can you use CPF to buy private property?**

No. CPF is for HDB only. But you can:
1. Withdraw CPF refund (→ cash down payment)
2. Use same CPF account to top up your retirement savings (not for property)

**Pro move:** Use CPF refund for private down payment, then rebuild retirement savings over 5-10 years via employment contributions.

## The Financing Trap: LTV + TDSR

**Private property financing is stricter than HDB:**

| Metric | HDB | Private |
|--------|-----|---------|
| LTV cap | 90% | 75% (Condo); 70% (Landed) |
| TDSR cap | 60% | 60% (same) |
| Tenure | 99-year | Freehold / 99-year |

**LTV example:**

4-room condo: $800k
- 75% LTV max = $600k mortgage available
- You need $200k down payment minimum (not 10%)
- If you only have $150k CPF refund → **You need more cash, or buy cheaper unit**

**Solution:** Sell HDB, get CPF refund, *then* hunt for condo in price range where (down payment + CPF) = 25%+ of price.

## The Checklist: Pre-Sale (What To Confirm)

**Mortgage status:**
- [ ] What's remaining balance on HDB mortgage? (Call bank)
- [ ] Any outstanding HDB loans (renovation, etc.)? (CPF Board statement)
- [ ] What's the early-termination penalty? (Usually $0 if sale proceeds go to CPF Board)

**CPF refund estimate:**
- [ ] Call CPF Board: "I'm selling HDB, how much will I get back?"
- [ ] Account number: ______
- [ ] Estimated refund: $________

**Private property pre-approval:**
- [ ] Get pre-approval letter from bank (valid 3 months)
- [ ] Pre-approved amount: $________
- [ ] Lender name: ________

**Market timing:**
- [ ] When is condo market good? (Interest rates, supply)
- [ ] When should I list HDB? (Seasonal demand)
- [ ] Can I carry both mortgages for 2-4 weeks? (If yes, more flexibility)

## The Real Example: Uptown Tampines to Parc Riviera

HDB upgrader, $750k budget, selling Tampines 5-room.

**HDB metrics:**
- Sale price: $560k (realistic)
- Net after costs: $245k
- CPF refund: $165k
- **Total liquidity: $410k**

**Condo search:**
- Range: $700-750k (LTV = 45-55% needed, 25%+ down)
- Parc Riviera 4-bed: $725k
- Down payment target: $180k (25%)
- Mortgage needed: $545k (75%)
- MSR: $1,745/month (3.8% rate)

**Sequence:**
- Month 1-2: List HDB (target $560k)
- Month 2: Pre-approve for private; view Parc Riviera units
- Month 3: HDB offer accepted at $560k; make offer on Parc unit *contingent on HDB close*
- Month 4: HDB closes (Aug 15) → receive $410k
- Month 4: Private closes (Aug 28) → down payment ($180k) + mortgage ($545k)

**Result:** Smooth transition, no mortgage overlap, optimal TDSR (58%).

**Alternative (Wrong):**
- Month 2: Fall in love with Parc Riviera; close early (buyer wants fast close)
- Month 3: Carry both mortgages ($1,745 private + $960 HDB = $2,705/month)
- TDSR jumps to 72% (exceeds limit; lender notices)
- Month 4: HDB finally sells; refinance private (costs $2-3k in legal + new appraisal)

## The Checklist: Closing (Final 4 Weeks)

- [ ] HDB lawyer sends draft S&P; review within 5 days
- [ ] Confirm HDB closing date (target 4-6 weeks out)
- [ ] Private pre-approval is live (not expired)
- [ ] Schedule private property inspection (week before close)
- [ ] Get private property valuation (bank-ordered appraisal)
- [ ] Confirm down payment source (CPF refund + cash)
- [ ] Schedule private closing 1-2 weeks after HDB
- [ ] Arrange moving logistics (staggered, not same day)
- [ ] Notify CPF Board of sale (they get notified automatically, but confirm)
- [ ] Update insurance (HDB → private property)

## Real-World Gotchas

**Gotcha 1: HDB doesn't close on time**

Buyer's bank delays appraisal or employment verification. HDB closing slips from Aug 15 to Sep 10. You're already obligated to close private (Aug 28). **Carry both mortgages or lose private deposit.**

**Prevention:** Build 2-week buffer. Schedule HDB close 2 weeks *before* private close, not 1 week.

**Gotcha 2: CPF refund takes 4-6 weeks**

HDB closes. Lawyer says "CPF refund will hit your account in 4-6 weeks." But private closes in 2 weeks. **You need bridge financing ($150-200k) or down payment delay (not allowed).**

**Prevention:** Ask lawyer upfront: "Can we accelerate CPF refund?" (Sometimes yes, sometimes no. Plan accordingly.)

**Gotcha 3: Private property appraisal is low**

Condo valued at $725k, but bank appraisal says $695k. Your 75% LTV now gives $521k mortgage (not $545k). **Down payment shortfall: $24k.**

**Prevention:** Get independent appraisal before making offer. Price offer based on appraisal, not listing price.

## Quick Wins

- **Parallel hunt:** Find private before HDB closes (saves 2-3 months of limbo)
- **Contingency clause:** "Offer contingent on HDB closing by [date]" (lets you close both in 1-2 weeks)
- **CPF timing:** Ask lawyer for expedited refund (sometimes 2-3 weeks vs 4-6)
- **Bridge financing:** Pre-arrange with bank if timing is tight (better than emergency last-minute)
