# Short Sale: When You Owe More Than It's Worth

You owe $750k mortgage. Property is worth $700k. If you sell, you're $50k short.

Short sale = selling for less than mortgage balance (lender approval required).

---

## Lender Approval Process

1. List property at market ($700k)
2. Get offer ($695k)
3. Submit to lender with hardship letter ("I've lost job, can't afford mortgage")
4. Lender approves (forgives $50k shortfall)
5. Sale closes; lender eats loss

---

## Deficiency Liability

After short sale, does lender chase you for $50k?

**In Singapore:** Rare (lender typically forgives as part of short-sale approval)
**In US:** Common (deficiency judgment; lender sues for balance)

Confirm with lender before proceeding.

---

## Timeline

Short sale takes 90-120 days (lender approval adds 30-60 days vs normal sale).
