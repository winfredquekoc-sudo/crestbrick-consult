# Second Property: The ABSD & LTV Reality Check

Your first property appreciated $150k. You want to buy a second condo as investment. But suddenly you need $350k down payment instead of $150k. Why?

Two reasons: ABSD (20% tax) and LTV cap (45% max, not 75%).

## The Math Shock

**First property: $600k condo**
- LTV: 75% → borrow $450k
- Down: $150k
- ABSD: 0%
- Total cost: $150k

**Second property: $600k condo**
- LTV: 45% (capped) → borrow $270k
- Down: $330k
- ABSD: $120k (20% on $600k)
- Total cost: $450k

**Difference: $300k more cash needed.**

## Why LTV Capped at 45%?

Banks restrict leverage on second properties because:
- Default rates higher (investors abandon property if market drops)
- Tenant risk (investment property = occupancy uncertainty)
- Debt service risk (investor may not cover mortgage if rent drops)

---

## ABSD: 20% Tax on 2nd Property

$600k purchase → $120k tax (on top of down payment). Government disincentive for investors.
