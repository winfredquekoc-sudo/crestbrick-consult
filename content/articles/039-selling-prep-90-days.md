# 90 Days to Selling: Finalize Pricing & Calculate Your Take-Home

You're 90 days from listing. This is your final pricing window, net proceeds calculation, and staging sprint. Upgraders and investors especially need clarity on cash landing (net proceeds) to know if the sale unlocks your next move. First-time sellers are often shocked to discover how much disappears between sale price and take-home.

## Money: Net Proceeds Calculation (Weeks 1-4)

**Sell price: $700,000 (example)**

**Subtract:**
- Agent commission (2% = $14,000)
- Legal fees ($1,500-2,000)
- Mortgage payoff ($445,000; varies by paydown pace)
- Seller's stamp duty (0% for HDB resale after 5 years, 1-4% for private depending on hold)
- Property tax ($300-600 prorated)
- Outstanding property manager fees, if any

**Example math ($700k sale, 8-year-old property):**
- Sale price: $700,000
- Agent commission: -$14,000
- Legal: -$2,000
- Mortgage payoff: -$445,000
- SSD: $0 (5-year+ HDB)
- **Net proceeds: $239,000**

The gap between $700k (sale price) and $239k (take-home) shocks most sellers. This number drives your next move: Can you upgrade? Invest? Or do you need a second property to access that equity?

**Calculate YOUR net proceeds now.** Know the number before you list. It's the difference between "I can upgrade" and "I'm stuck."

## Timing: Strategic Listing (Week 2)

**Launch on your optimal day of week:**
- Monday-Tuesday: Weekday workers browse during lunch; moderate competition for agent time
- Wednesday: Mid-week is dead (avoid)
- Thursday-Friday: Weekend approaching; serious buyers; peak agent traffic
- Sunday: Launches get lost; avoid

**Optimal launch timing by property type:**
- HDB 3BR: February, May-June (upgraders active)
- Private condo mature: September (post-summer softness)
- Investment property: 30 days before tenant moves out (empty possession)

**Expect these timelines post-listing:**
- Week 1-2: Initial viewing spike (motivated buyers who've been searching 3+ months)
- Week 3-6: Sustained viewing (market-level interest)
- Week 7-12: Declining interest; repricing needed if no offers

## Safety: Staging & Legal Prep (Weeks 2-8)

**Stage for 3 buyer types simultaneously:**
1. **Owner-occupiers (first-time, upgraders):** Clean, neutral, lifestyle-focused (young family energy, professional ambiance)
2. **Investors:** Highlight rental potential (furniture layout for tenant, square-footage clarity)
3. **Developers/land bankers:** Clean canvas (minimal furniture, access to every corner for measurement)

**Staging ROI: $2-5k spend yields 3-8% sale price premium.** Professional stager costs $1.5-3k; furniture rental $500-1.5k. Avoid beige-box generics; subtle personality (art, plants, lighting) outperforms sterile.

**Prepare your legal documents:**
- Title deed (certified copy ready)
- Outstanding mortgage statement (payoff amount as of sale date)
- Disclosure statement (water damage history, repairs, known issues)
- Building insurance (hand to buyer at completion)
- COV certificate if applicable (HDB)

**Contingency prep:**
- What if inspection finds $20k in work? Accept it or renegotiate discount upfront
- What if buyer can't get financing approved? Have backup buyers lined up
- What if buyer's sale (their funding source) falls through? Know your walk-away point

## Audience-Specific 90-Day Actions

**Upgraders:** Confirm your next property is under active offer or in advanced viewings. Your sale closing is 90+ days away; you need purchase timing aligned. If closing sale on Sep 15, you need new property secured by Aug 15 (30-day overlap minimum).

**Investors:** Empty possession timeline locked? If tenant is month-to-month, issue formal notice now (60 days pre-listing). If tenant is fixed-term, calculate lease-end and coordinate exit 30 days before sale closing.

**First-time sellers:** This is your stress test. Mentally walk through: offer comes in, inspection reveals issues, buyer drags on appraisal. Can you handle 120-day sale cycle? If not, factor 3-6 month holding cost into your net proceeds calculation and adjust expectations.

## The 90-Day Checklist

- [ ] Net proceeds calculated and confirmed
- [ ] Pricing locked (within ±2% of market)
- [ ] Staging plan set and contractors contacted
- [ ] Legal docs (title, mortgage payoff, disclosure) prepared
- [ ] Optimal listing date and day-of-week chosen
- [ ] Buyer-type profile defined (owner vs. investor vs. developer)
- [ ] Contingency scenarios planned (inspection issues, financing delays)
- [ ] Backup timing confirmed (if upgrading/investing next)
