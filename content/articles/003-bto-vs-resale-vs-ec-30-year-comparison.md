# BTO vs Resale vs EC: 30-Year Ownership Cost Comparison

Most first-time buyers face a choice: **BTO (Build-to-Order), Resale, or EC (Executive Condominium)**. Each has different price points, subsidies, maintenance costs, and future appreciation profiles. This comparison shows the real 30-year cost using a $400,000 budget.

## The Three Options

**BTO (Build-to-Order):** New HDB flat, high subsidy, 3-5 year wait, heavy demand.
**Resale:** Existing HDB flat, immediate occupancy, moderate subsidy, lower demand.
**EC (Executive Condominium):** Hybrid HDB+condo, less subsidy than BTO, condominium facilities, 5-year minimum holding period.

## Scenario: $400,000 Budget, 4-Room Family Flat, 30-Year Hold

### BTO Scenario

**Purchase Price:** $300,000 (subsidized price for a BTO 4-room in non-central location)
**Down Payment (10%):** $30,000
**Mortgage:** $270,000 at 2.6% over 30 years = $1,050/month

**Maintenance & Costs Over 30 Years:**
- HDB annual maintenance: ~$200/year = $6,000 total
- Property tax: ~$5/month = $1,800 total
- Upgrading/repairs at year 15: ~$30,000 (common for aging flats)
- **Total 30-year costs: $37,800**

**Mortgage Payments Over 30 Years:** 1,050 × 360 months = $378,000

**Appreciation Assumption:** 2.5% annual (conservative for HDB)
- Initial price: $300,000
- After 30 years at 2.5%/year: ~$630,000

**Sale Proceeds at Year 30:**
- Sale price: $630,000
- Less: Agent commission (2.5%): -$15,750
- Less: Maintenance costs paid earlier: Already accounted
- **Net proceeds: $614,250**

**Total Cost of Ownership:**
- Down payment: $30,000
- Mortgage: $378,000
- Maintenance/costs: $37,800
- **Total invested: $445,800**

**Net Gain at Sale:** $614,250 - $445,800 = $168,450

**ROI Over 30 years:** 37.7% cumulative, or ~1.2% annually

### Resale Scenario

**Purchase Price:** $380,000 (current resale price for similar 4-room flat, less subsidy than BTO)
**Down Payment (15%, more due to less subsidy):** $57,000
**Mortgage:** $323,000 at 2.6% over 25 years (shorter loan) = $1,445/month

**Maintenance & Costs Over 30 Years:**
- HDB annual maintenance: ~$250/year = $7,500 total
- Property tax: ~$5/month = $1,800 total
- Major repairs at year 10: $40,000 (older flat needs earlier intervention)
- Major repairs at year 20: $35,000
- **Total 30-year costs: $84,300**

**Mortgage Payments Over 25 Years:** $1,445 × 300 months = $433,500
(Mortgage fully paid by year 25; remaining 5 years mortgage-free)

**Appreciation Assumption:** 2% annual (lower than BTO—resale is already appreciating slower)
- Initial price: $380,000
- After 30 years at 2%/year: ~$686,000

**Sale Proceeds at Year 30:**
- Sale price: $686,000
- Less: Agent commission (2.5%): -$17,150
- **Net proceeds: $668,850**

**Total Cost of Ownership:**
- Down payment: $57,000
- Mortgage: $433,500
- Maintenance/costs: $84,300
- **Total invested: $574,800**

**Net Gain at Sale:** $668,850 - $574,800 = $94,050

**ROI Over 30 years:** 16.4% cumulative, or ~0.5% annually

### EC Scenario

**Purchase Price:** $420,000 (EC price for similar-sized unit, higher than HDB, lower than private condo)
**Down Payment (15%, financing constraint):** $63,000
**Mortgage:** $357,000 at 3.0% (EC rates higher than HDB) over 25 years = $1,680/month

**Maintenance & Costs Over 30 Years:**
- EC annual maintenance: ~$400/year = $12,000 total
- Property tax: ~$8/month = $2,880 total
- Upgrading/repairs at year 15: $35,000 (EC common areas well-maintained, personal unit less need)
- Condominium sinking fund: ~$100/month = $36,000 total
- **Total 30-year costs: $85,880**

**Mortgage Payments Over 25 Years:** $1,680 × 300 months = $504,000
(Mortgage fully paid by year 25; remaining 5 years mortgage-free)

**Appreciation Assumption:** 3% annual (higher than HDB—EC has better location and facilities)
- Initial price: $420,000
- After 30 years at 3%/year: ~$1,024,000

**Sale Proceeds at Year 30:**
- Sale price: $1,024,000
- Less: Agent commission (2.5%): -$25,600
- **Net proceeds: $998,400**

**Total Cost of Ownership:**
- Down payment: $63,000
- Mortgage: $504,000
- Maintenance/costs: $85,880
- **Total invested: $652,880**

**Net Gain at Sale:** $998,400 - $652,880 = $345,520

**ROI Over 30 years:** 52.9% cumulative, or ~1.6% annually

## Qualitative Factors

**BTO Advantage:** Lowest purchase price and monthly payment. High subsidy makes it unbeatable for budget-conscious buyers. Trade-off: 3-5 year wait and no choice (balloted allocation).

**Resale Advantage:** Immediate occupancy and choice. You can inspect the actual unit. Trade-off: Higher price and lower future appreciation (already appreciated).

**EC Advantage:** Strongest long-term appreciation (1.6% annual). Condo facilities and newer building. Trade-off: Highest monthly payment and maintenance costs.

## The Affordability Reality

Using the $400,000 budget as the constraint:
- **BTO:** You can afford it at $30k down, $1,050/month mortgage.
- **Resale:** You can afford it at $57k down, $1,445/month mortgage (24% higher monthly).
- **EC:** You can barely afford it at $63k down, $1,680/month mortgage (60% higher than BTO).

If your budget is tight, BTO wins. If you can comfortably afford the higher payments, EC wins on long-term wealth.

## Decision Checklist

- [ ] Do you have 3-5 years to wait? (If yes, BTO. If no, Resale or EC.)
- [ ] What's your monthly cash flow budget? (BTO cheapest, EC most expensive)
- [ ] Do you want to inspect the actual unit? (Resale yes, BTO no)
- [ ] Are you planning to hold 20+ years? (EC best ROI)
- [ ] Do you prioritize low cost or long-term appreciation? (BTO = low cost, EC = appreciation)
