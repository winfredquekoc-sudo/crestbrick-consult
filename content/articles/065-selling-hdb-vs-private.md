# HDB vs Private: Why HDB Sells Faster

HDB: Average 30-50 days to close
Private: Average 60-90 days to close

Why the gap?

---

## HDB Advantages

**Buyer pool larger:** Every first-time buyer must buy HDB
**Price lower:** $450-550k (more affordable)
**Financing easier:** 90% LTV available
**Decision faster:** Buyers decide quicker on lower prices

---

## Private Disadvantages

**Buyer pool smaller:** Only upgraders + investors
**Price higher:** $700k-2M (more consideration needed)
**Financing stricter:** 75% LTV cap
**Decision slower:** Buyers take more time on big decision

---

## Selling Strategy

**HDB:** List at market; expect 4-8 week sale
**Private:** List 5-8% above market; expect 8-12 week negotiation
