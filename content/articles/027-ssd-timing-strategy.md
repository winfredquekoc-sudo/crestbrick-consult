# SSD Timing Strategy: Minimizing Costs on Your Property Exit

The decision to sell your property isn't just about price—it's about minimizing SSD taxes. Holding 5+ years guarantees zero SSD, but sometimes selling earlier with SSD is better if your property has appreciated dramatically.

## Decision Framework

**Ask yourself:**
1. Has the property appreciated enough to offset SSD?
2. How much longer until I hit the 5-year SSD-free threshold?
3. What else can I do with the capital if I sell now vs. later?

## Scenario 1: Should You Sell Before Year 5?

**Property:** $400,000 HDB flat

**Scenario A: Sell at Year 3**
- Current value: $480,000 (20% appreciation over 3 years)
- SSD (5%): -$24,000
- Agent commission (2.5%): -$12,000
- **Net proceeds: $444,000**
- Net gain: $44,000 (after costs)

**Scenario B: Hold to Year 5**
- Projected value: $510,000 (assuming 3.5% annual appreciation)
- SSD (0%): $0
- Agent commission (2.5%): -$12,750
- **Net proceeds: $497,250**
- Net gain: $97,250
- But: Holding costs ($4,000), forgone rent on $444k (if invested elsewhere at 3%), opportunity cost

**Math:**
- Holding 2 more years costs $4,000 + ~$12,000 opportunity cost (conservative estimate) = $16,000
- But you save $24,000 in SSD and gain ~$30,000 from appreciation
- **Net benefit of waiting: $38,000**

In this scenario, **waiting for zero SSD wins decisively.**

## Scenario 2: Property Isn't Appreciating—Sell Anyway?

**Property:** $400,000 EC (Executive Condo)

**Market stall:** EC market flat for 3 years, no appreciation expected

**Scenario A: Sell at Year 3**
- Sale price: $400,000 (no appreciation)
- SSD (5%): -$20,000
- Agent commission (2.5%): -$10,000
- **Net proceeds: $370,000**
- Net loss: -$30,000 (SSD + commission exceed gains)

**Scenario B: Hold to Year 5**
- Sale price: $400,000 (still stalled)
- SSD (0%): $0
- Agent commission (2.5%): -$10,000
- **Net proceeds: $390,000**
- Net loss: -$10,000 (just commission)
- Holding costs: -$4,000 over 2 years
- **Effective proceeds: $386,000**

**Math:**
- Scenario A: Net $370,000
- Scenario B: Net $386,000
- **Waiting saves $16,000 even with no appreciation**

Even in a stalled market, waiting for zero SSD beats selling early.

## Scenario 3: Forced Sale (Job Relocation, Divorce, etc.)

**Property:** $350,000 resale HDB

**Situation:** Job relocation to London in 2 years; must sell

**Scenario A: Sell now (Year 2)**
- SSD (8%): -$28,000
- Net proceeds: ~$300,000

**Scenario B: Can't wait**
- Reality check: You have no choice. Waiting costs you the relocation opportunity or costs of maintaining two homes overseas
- **Action: Sell now. Accept the SSD as the cost of circumstance**

Not every sale can be optimized for tax timing.

## SSD + ABSD Coordination (Multi-Property Strategy)

If you own multiple properties:

**Scenario: Sell HDB in Year 3, buy 2nd property**
- HDB sale: $450,000 @ 5% SSD = $22,500 cost
- Proceeds net: $416,250
- Buy private condo (citizen, 2nd property): 20% ABSD on $800,000 = $160,000 cost
- **Total tax: $182,500**

**Alternative: Hold HDB to Year 5, THEN buy 2nd property**
- HDB sale: $480,000 @ 0% SSD = $0 cost
- Proceeds net: $468,750
- Buy private condo: 20% ABSD on $800,000 = $160,000 cost
- **Total tax: $160,000**
- Savings: $22,500 (just the SSD saving alone)

**Key insight:** Coordinating multiple sales can compound tax savings.

## Quick Decision Tree: Should You Sell Now or Wait?

**START:** You're at Year ___ of ownership, property worth $___.

1. **Is this a forced sale** (job relocation, divorce, cash emergency)? 
   → **YES:** Sell now. Accept SSD as cost of circumstance.
   → **NO:** Continue.

2. **Average annual appreciation: > 3%?**
   → **YES:** Likely worth waiting for zero SSD (appreciation may offset holding costs).
   → **NO:** Continue.

3. **Years until zero SSD: < 3 years left?**
   → **YES:** Waiting is manageable. Consider holding for zero SSD.
   → **NO:** Continue.

4. **Do you have a compelling use for the capital** (upgrade, invest in 2nd property)?
   → **YES:** Model both scenarios (sell now vs. wait); compare total wealth in 5 years.
   → **NO:** Default = wait for zero SSD.

**OUTPUT:**
- Forced sale, low appreciation, or <2 years to zero SSD → **SELL NOW**
- Stable appreciation (>3%), 2-3 years to zero SSD → **WAIT FOR ZERO SSD**
- Other scenarios → **NEED CUSTOM MODELING** (see calculator below)
