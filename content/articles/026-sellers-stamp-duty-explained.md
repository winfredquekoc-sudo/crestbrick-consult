# Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD) Explained: Rates, Holding Periods, and Exemptions

Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD) is a tax you pay when you SELL a residential property, based on how long you've held it. Shorter holds face 12% SSD; longer holds pay 0%. Understanding SSD windows can save you $20,000–100,000+ on your sale.

## SSD Rates (2026 Singapore)

**HDB flats:**
- Held < 1 year: 12% of sale price
- Held 1–3 years: 8%
- Held 3–5 years: 5%
- Held 5+ years: 0% (exempt)

**Private residential property (condos, landed houses):**
- Held < 1 year: 16%
- Held 1–3 years: 8%
- Held 3–5 years: 5%
- Held 5+ years: 0%

**Key insight:** Holding 5+ years eliminates SSD entirely. This is the "magic threshold."

## Example: $450,000 HDB Flat Sale

**Held 3 years (5% SSD):**
- Sale price: $450,000
- SSD: 5% = $22,500
- Agent commission (2.5%): $11,250
- **Net proceeds: $416,250**

**Held 5+ years (0% SSD):**
- Sale price: $450,000 (assume same price after 2 more years)
- SSD: 0%
- Agent commission (2.5%): $11,250
- **Net proceeds: $438,750**

**Difference:** $22,500 saved by waiting 2 years. If the flat appreciates to $480,000 by year 5, your net is even higher.

## HDB First Sale Exemption

Many Singaporeans qualify for an **SSD exemption** on their FIRST HDB flat sale if:

1. You bought the HDB as your first property
2. You owner-occupied it (not rented it out)
3. You're selling to upgrade to another HDB or private property
4. The sale is for your primary residence

**If you qualify:** You owe **0% SSD regardless of holding period**. This is huge—it means you can sell your HDB after 2 years with zero SSD penalty.

**Catch:** This applies only if you're "upgrading." If you sell your HDB and move overseas or rent indefinitely, the exemption may not apply.

**Check with HDB:** Contact HDB directly or your lawyer to confirm eligibility before selling.

## When to Sell: SSD Windows

**Window 1: Year 1-3 (high SSD)**
- SSD: 12% (year 1) or 8% (years 1–3)
- Only sell if: Urgent (job relocation, divorce), property has appreciated dramatically (20%+), or you qualify for first-sale exemption

**Window 2: Year 3-5 (moderate SSD)**
- SSD: 5%
- Reasonable if: Solid appreciation (8–10%+) or life event forces the sale
- Example: Buy at $400k, sell at $450k after 4 years → 5% SSD = $22.5k

**Window 3: Year 5+ (zero SSD)**
- SSD: 0%
- **Optimal:** Hold 5+ years, then sell with zero SSD penalty
- Most investors plan for this window

## Holding Periods and Holding Costs

Don't just look at SSD—factor in holding costs:

**Costs of holding 5 years instead of 3:**
- Property tax: ~$500/year × 2 = $1,000
- Maintenance and repairs: ~$2,000/year × 2 = $4,000
- Opportunity cost: Could have deployed capital elsewhere

**Total holding cost for 2 extra years: ~$5,000**

**But SSD saved: $22,500** (on a $450k sale)

**Net benefit: $17,500** (still a win even after holding costs)

## SSD + ABSD Coordination (2nd Property Strategy)

If you're selling a property to buy a 2nd property:

**Timing matters:**
- **Sell first, then buy:** You pay SSD on the sale, but lower ABSD rate may apply on the new purchase (depends on property type)
- **Buy first, then sell:** You may owe ABSD on the new purchase immediately, creating cash-flow pressure

**Example:**
- Selling HDB at 4 years: 5% SSD = $22,500
- Buying private condo (2nd residential, citizen): 20% ABSD
- Coordinate sales 30+ days apart to minimize overlap in cash obligations

## Tax on SSD

Is SSD itself taxable income in Singapore?

**No.** SSD is a tax on the property sale (stamp duty), not income tax. You don't pay income tax on the SSD amount—only the SSD itself.

**However:** If you're a property trader/investor (selling properties as a business), IRAS may treat gains as trading income and tax you on the profit (not the SSD).
