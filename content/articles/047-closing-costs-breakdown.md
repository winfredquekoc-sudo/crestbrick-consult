# Singapore Closing Costs Breakdown: Seller vs Buyer

Most sellers think closing costs are minor. Then settlement day arrives: $15-30k in costs appear, net proceeds drop suddenly, regret sets in. Understanding what you'll pay—and why—prevents sticker shock.

## Seller Costs (Your Responsibility)

**Agent Commission: 2-2.5% of sale price**
- Example: $700k sale = $14,000-$17,500
- Negotiable (hotly; offer 1.5-2% in strong market)
- Non-negotiable in soft market (agents won't list at <2%)
- Paid at closing (from proceeds)

**Legal Fees: $1,500-$3,000**
- Varies by law firm and complexity
- Includes: draft Sale & Purchase agreement, title search, stamp duty calculation, closing coordination
- Lower end ($1.5k) if straightforward
- Higher end ($3k) if complications (mortgage payoff delays, title issues, co-owner disputes)

**Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD): 1-4% (HDB resale, if <5 years owned)**
- 1-year hold: 4%
- 2-year hold: 3%
- 3-4 year hold: 2%
- 5+ years: 0% (HDB exemption)
- Private property: SSD applies year 1-3 after purchase (rates vary by jurisdiction)
- Example: $700k sale, 2-year hold = $14,000 SSD

**Property Tax (Prorated): $100-$500**
- Split between seller (pre-sale portion) and buyer (post-sale portion)
- Example: Annual tax $1,200; if you sell mid-year, you owe ~$600

**CPF Refund (If Applicable): Variable**
- If you used CPF to buy and still have CPF loan, your CPF is refunded from proceeds before you get yours
- Example: CPF still owes $100k; proceeds reduced by $100k before your payout

**Mortgage Payoff: $XXX**
- Remaining mortgage balance
- Paid off from proceeds at closing
- Example: $700k sale, $450k mortgage = $450k from proceeds goes to bank

**Miscellaneous: $200-$500**
- Title insurance (optional; recommended $600-$800)
- Survey (if needed; $500-$1,000)
- Inspection contingency (if offered)

## Buyer Costs (NOT Your Problem, But Context)

**Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD): Variable by status**
- Singapore citizen, 1st property: 1%
- Singapore citizen, 2nd+ property: 15% (plus 5-15% ABSD depending on property type)
- Foreign buyer: 5-20% ABSD

**Financing Costs:**
- Mortgage application fee: $500-$1,000
- Valuation fee (bank-ordered appraisal): $500-$1,000
- Mortgage insurance: 0-3% of loan (varies by lender)

**Legal Fees (Buyer's side): $1,500-$2,500**

**Inspection (Buyer's optional): $1,500-$2,500**

**Total buyer costs: 5-40% of purchase price** (varies wildly by buyer status and financing)

**Why mention buyer costs?** Because they influence buyer behavior. A buyer facing 20% total costs ($140k on $700k) is more cautious; they're less likely to bid aggressively or close fast.

## The Math: Example $700k Sale

**Gross proceeds: $700,000**

**Seller costs:**
- Agent commission (2%): -$14,000
- Legal: -$2,000
- SSD (2-year hold, 2%): -$14,000
- Property tax (prorated): -$600
- Mortgage payoff: -$450,000
- Title insurance: -$700

**Net proceeds to you: $218,700**

**Insight:** Gross and net are $481,300 apart. Many sellers don't account for this until closing.

## Negotiation Points (What You Can Control)

**Agent Commission:**
- In hot markets (buyer frenzy): Negotiate 1.5-1.75%
- In soft markets (inventory glut): Accept 2-2.5%
- Offer to split commission (you pay 1%, buyer's agent pays 1%)

**Legal Fees:**
- Shop 2-3 law firms; negotiate $1.5-$2k range
- Complex title = higher fee justified
- Simple transaction = $1.5k is fair

**Timing SSD (Strategic):**
- If selling in year 2-3 of ownership: Consider waiting until year 5 (SSD drops to 0%)
- If forcing sale in year <3: Accept SSD as cost of exit

**Mortgage Payoff:**
- Refinance before sale to lower balance? (Only if strategic, not reactive)
- Confirm exact payoff amount 1 month before closing (rates change)

## What You CANNOT Control

- Property tax (government-set)
- CPF refunds (mandatory law)
- Buyer's stamp duty (buyer's problem, but affects their offers)
- Title insurance (optional, but recommended)

## The Checklist: Closing Cost Prep

- [ ] Ask agent: what's realistic commission in current market?
- [ ] Get 2-3 legal fee quotes; pick middle option
- [ ] Calculate SSD liability (check if 5-year exemption applies)
- [ ] Confirm mortgage payoff amount (call lender)
- [ ] Estimate prorated property tax (annual ÷ 12 × months remaining)
- [ ] Account for CPF refund (if applicable)
- [ ] Calculate total costs; subtract from sale price
- [ ] Net proceeds = (sale price - all costs - mortgage payoff)
- [ ] Confirm this number with lawyer 1 week before closing

## Real Example: The Surprise

Seller thought: "$700k sale = $700k in my bank account"

Reality:
- Sale price: $700,000
- Costs (commission + legal + SSD + tax): -$30,500
- Mortgage payoff: -$450,000
- Net: $219,500

Shock: "I expected $400k! Where did $180k go?"

Lesson: Understand math before you list.
