# Private Financing & Developer Loans: When to Use Them

When CPF and bank mortgages aren't enough—or you don't qualify—developer loans and private financing fill the gap. They're expensive, but sometimes necessary.

## Developer Loans (New Launch Condos)

**What it is:** The condo developer offers financing for 2–3 years while you arrange permanent financing from a bank or refinance.

**Rate:** 2.5–3.5% (attractive at launch)

**Term:** 24–36 months (very short)

**LTV:** Up to 80% (sometimes 85% for qualified buyers)

**How it works:**
1. You buy condo at launch price (e.g., $500k)
2. Developer offers 80% loan = $400k @ 2.8% for 30 months
3. You pay $1,500/month interest-only for 30 months
4. At month 30, condo completes; you refinance with a bank for permanent 25-year mortgage
5. Bank loan repays developer loan in full

**Benefit:** During construction (2–3 years), you pay interest-only. When building completes, you get a permanent mortgage at lower rates (hopefully).

**Risk:** When refinancing time comes, what if:
- Your income dropped?
- Banks are offering 5%+ rates (much higher than your developer loan)?
- Property hasn't appreciated (harder to refinance)?

## Cost Example: Developer Loan vs. Bank Mortgage

**Property:** $600,000 new condo

**Scenario A: Developer Loan, then bank refinance**
- Developer loan: $480,000 @ 2.8% for 30 months
- Monthly interest-only payment: $1,120
- Total interest paid during construction: $33,600
- After completion: Refinance $480,000 @ 3.2% (bank rate at time of completion) for 25 years
- New monthly payment: $2,270
- Total interest over 25 years (from month 31): $197,000
- **Total cost: $33,600 + $197,000 = $230,600**

**Scenario B: Bank Mortgage from Day 1**
- Bank mortgage: $480,000 @ 3.2% for 25 years
- Monthly payment: $2,270 (starting immediately)
- Total interest: $197,000
- But: You have 30 months of duplicate payments during construction (~$68,000) because you're paying rent elsewhere while building
- **Total cost + housing burden: $197,000 + $68,000 = $265,000**

**Developer loan wins:** Saves ~$34,000 (the interest-only vs. full amortization, minus the construction-period rent)

## Private Mortgages (Non-bank Lenders)

**What it is:** Money lent by private individuals, companies, or investment funds (not banks) at higher rates.

**Rate:** 4–8% annually (significantly higher than banks)

**Term:** 3–10 years

**LTV:** 50–70% (conservative; high-risk lending)

**Security:** Registered mortgage on the property

**When used:**
- Self-employed or freelancers with irregular income (banks reject them)
- Properties in contested areas or odd specifications (banks won't finance)
- Fast cash needed (private lenders approve in days vs. weeks for banks)
- Recent immigrant with limited Singapore credit history

## Private Mortgage Cost Example

**Loan:** $200,000 at 6% for 5 years

**Option A: Interest-only**
- Monthly payment: $1,000
- Total paid over 5 years: $60,000 (all interest)
- Principal due at end: $200,000 (lump sum)

**Option B: Full amortization (5-year term)**
- Monthly payment: $3,865
- Total interest over 5 years: $31,900
- Principal fully repaid

**Compare to bank mortgage (same $200k @ 2.8% for 25 years):**
- Monthly payment: $950
- Total interest over 25 years: $85,000

**Private mortgage cost premium:** $31,900 (5-year private) vs. $85,000 (25-year bank) = Private is actually cheaper on a 5-year horizon, but forces refinancing every 5 years (risky).

## When Private Financing Makes Sense

**Scenario 1: Freelancer/self-employed, good credit, short-term bridge**
- Stable freelance income ($10k+/month) but 2 years of P&L makes banks nervous
- Found perfect property; willing to pay 5% private mortgage rate for 3 years
- Plan: Refinance to bank after 3 years when income history is longer
- **Cost:** 3 years at 5% = ~$30,000 extra interest vs. bank rate
- **Benefit:** Buy now; avoid losing the property; refinance later

**Scenario 2: Development investment, high-risk property**
- Want to buy a shophouse or landed property in emerging area (e.g., new MRT station)
- Banks won't finance because area is "unproven"
- Private lender will finance at 6.5% LTV 60%
- **Cost:** Pay 6.5% for 5–7 years until area develops; then refinance to bank at lower rate
- **Benefit:** Capture appreciation from early development cycle

**Scenario 3: Construction period, delayed bank approval**
- Buying new launch condo; developer loan expires in 36 months
- Bank mortgage approval delayed due to portfolio review
- Private bridge for 6–12 months buys time
- **Cost:** $15,000–25,000 extra interest for short period
- **Benefit:** Avoid forced sale or penalty for missing permanent financing deadline

## When NOT to Use Private Financing

**Avoid if:**
- You qualify for bank financing (always cheaper)
- You have no exit strategy (e.g., how will you refinance in 5 years if rates spike to 6%?)
- The private lender is unregulated or has a reputation for predatory terms
