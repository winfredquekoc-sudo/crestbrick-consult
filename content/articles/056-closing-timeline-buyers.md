# Closing Timeline: What Happens After Your Offer Is Accepted

Your offer was accepted. Now what? 60-90 days until you get keys. But what happens each week? Where are the delays?

## Week-by-Week Timeline

**Week 1-2: Paperwork Phase**
- Buyer's lawyer sends contract to seller's lawyer
- Inspections ordered (1-2 weeks out)
- Bank appraisal ordered
- Pre-approval updated to reflect offer details

**Week 2-3: Inspection Phase**
- Home inspection scheduled & completed
- Issues identified; contractor quotes obtained
- Renegotiations happen (if findings material)

**Week 3-4: Appraisal Phase**
- Bank appraisal completed
- If appraisal is low (below offer price), renegotiation required
- Financing contingency triggered (if applicable)

**Week 4-6: Legal Phase**
- Contracts exchanged (buyer + seller both sign)
- Caveat lodged (lawyer registers your interest at land office)
- Mortgage deed prepared

**Week 6-8: Final Approval Phase**
- Mortgage final approval issued (if no issues found)
- Title search completed (any encumbrances identified)
- Insurance policy confirmed

**Week 8-9: Settlement Phase**
- Final walkthrough (verify seller hasn't damaged property)
- Down payment wired to lawyer
- Lawyer pays seller from sale proceeds
- Keys handed over

**Week 9-10: Possession Phase**
- You get keys
- Tenants moved out (if applicable)
- Utilities transferred to your name

---

**Delays to watch:**
- Inspection delays (booking backlogs)
- Appraisal delays (bank backlog)
- Financing delays (employment verification)
- Legal delays (title searches, caveat lodgement)

Standard: 60-70 days. With delays: 90-120 days.
