# When NOT to Sell: The Refusal Strategy

Everyone assumes selling is the goal. But the best sell decision is sometimes to NOT sell. Here's when holding beats selling.

## Hold If: MRT Opens in 1-3 Years

MRT line opening near your property?
- Buy now: prices rise 10-15% as opening approaches
- Sell before opening: you're early; miss 10% appreciation
- Sell after opening: appreciation already priced in

**Math:** Property worth $700k today. MRT opens in 18 months. Expected value at opening: $810k (+15%).

**Sell now:** $700k proceeds
**Sell at opening:** $810k proceeds
**Difference: $110k** for 18 months of holding

---

## Hold If: Refinance vs Sell

Current mortgage: $400k at 3.8%
Refinance rate: 3.2% (rates falling)

**Sell path:**
- Sell at $700k
- Pay off $400k mortgage
- Net proceeds: $300k (after costs)
- Buy smaller property at $500k
- New mortgage: $375k at 3.2%

**Hold path:**
- Refinance existing $400k to 3.2%
- Save $160/month (0.6% rate cut)
- Keep property; keep appreciation upside

**Winner:** Hold (save $160/month, keep property)

---

## Hold If: Tax Timing

Selling in Dec vs Jan can cost $20-40k in ABSD or tax deferral.

Check with accountant on tax year timing before listing.
