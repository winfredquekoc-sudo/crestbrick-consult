# Counter-Offer Psychology: When Buyers are Serious

Buyer offers $700k. You counter $720k. They counter $705k.

Are they serious? Or testing you?

---

## Reading the Pattern

**Pattern 1: Steady increments ($700 → $705 → $710)**
Signal: Serious buyer, slowly approaching your target
Response: Keep countering; they're moving toward you

**Pattern 2: Stuck at same number ($700 → $700 → $700)**
Signal: Buyer's final number; not willing to move
Response: Accept or walk; no movement coming

**Pattern 3: Big jump then freeze ($700 → $720, then silence)**
Signal: Buyer overextended; can't go higher
Response: Might be their max; accept if close to target

**Pattern 4: Slower response time (1-day → 2-day → 3-day)**
Signal: Buyer losing interest
Response: Might need faster close or price reduction to re-engage

---

## When to Walk

- Buyer re-negotiates after inspection (walk; they're testing)
- Buyer adds contingencies after agreeing to none (walk; changing terms)
- Buyer ghosts for 3+ days (walk; lost interest)
