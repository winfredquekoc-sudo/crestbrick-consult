# Occupier vs Investor: Message Matters

Same $700k condo. Two buyer types. Two different closes.

---

## To Occupier

**Message:** "Perfect for young family; top schools; light-filled; modern finishes"
**Price approach:** Full market price ($700k)
**Timeline:** Normal (60-90 days)
**Negotiation:** Lifestyle-focused (less price-sensitive)

---

## To Investor

**Message:** "Strong rental demand $3.5k/month; 6% yield; tenant pool is stable"
**Price approach:** 3-5% discount (yield-math justifies)
**Timeline:** Fast (14-28 days; investors move quick)
**Negotiation:** Yield-focused (very price-sensitive)

---

## Real Example

Original list: $700k (to occupiers)
Result: 45 days, one offer at $680k

Re-market: "Rental comps $3.5k; 6% yield" (to investors)
Result: 20 days, multiple offers $680-690k

**Messaging to right buyer wins.**
