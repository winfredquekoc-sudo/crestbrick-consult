# Executive Condos: The In-Between Market

EC = halfway between HDB and private condo. Owns by Singaporeans only; 99-year lease; $400-600k price range.

---

## Key Rules

**Ownership:** Citizens and PRs only (no foreigners)
**Lease:** 99 years (at purchase; ages like HDB)
**Upgradeability:** Can upgrade to private after 5 years
**Price:** $400-600k (below private; above HDB)
**Financing:** 75% LTV (like private)

---

## Selling EC: Buyer Pool Shift

**First 5 years:** Buyers are upgraders (can't move to private yet)
**After 5 years:** Buyers start comparing to private condos (if private cheaper, EC loses appeal)

---

## Timeline Impact

Sell EC in year 3 (still locked into EC rules):
- Buyer pool: upgraders from HDB
- Price: strong

Sell EC in year 7 (eligible for private upgrade):
- Buyer pool: mixed (some upgrading, some prefer private)
- Price: might soften if private is cheaper
