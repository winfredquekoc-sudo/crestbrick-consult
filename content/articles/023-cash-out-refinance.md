# Cash-Out Refinance: Unlocking Home Equity for Investments

A cash-out refinance lets you extract equity from your home by refinancing for MORE than you owe, pocketing the difference. It's one of the cheapest ways to borrow in Singapore—often at 2–3% mortgage rates versus 5–8% for personal loans.

## How It Works

**Scenario:** Your HDB flat is worth $500,000. You owe $300,000.

**Your equity:** $200,000

**Traditional refinance:** Refinance $300,000 at a lower rate. No cash out.

**Cash-out refinance:** Refinance $380,000. New lender pays off your old $300,000 mortgage, and you get $80,000 in cash.

**New loan balance:** $380,000 (up from $300,000)

## Why Singapore Buyers Do This

**Investment capital:** Use the cash to buy a second property, reducing ABSD impact by borrowing cheaper than alternative financing.

**Upgrade financing:** Extract equity to fund a BTO or EC down payment without selling your current property.

**Business/emergency capital:** Borrow against home equity at mortgage rates instead of personal loan rates.

## The Math: $80,000 Cash-Out Example

**Loan details:**
- New refinanced amount: $380,000 at 2.3%
- Original loan was: $300,000 at 2.8%
- Cash extracted: $80,000

**Monthly payment increase (25-year tenure):**
- Old payment on $300k @ 2.8%: $1,180
- New payment on $380k @ 2.3%: $1,490
- **Increase: $310/month**

**Interest cost of the $80k extracted over 25 years:**
- $80,000 × 2.3% × 25 years ≈ $46,000 in interest

**Compare to: Personal loan for $80,000**
- Rate: 6.5% (typical for unsecured personal loan)
- Tenure: 5 years (maximum term offered)
- Monthly payment: $1,550
- Total interest: $13,000

**Why cash-out refinance is cheaper:**
- Mortgage spreads the cost over 25 years (lower monthly, more total interest)
- Personal loan is shorter term (higher monthly, less total interest)
- But the mortgage rate (2.3%) is much lower than personal loan rate (6.5%)

## HDB Cash-Out Refinance: Rules & Limits

**Maximum cash-out:** Banks typically allow refinancing up to **70–75% of the property's market value**. So if your flat is worth $500k, you can refinance up to $375k.

**Owner-occupied only:** You cannot do a cash-out refinance on an investment property (rental HDB flat). The loan must be for your primary residence.

**Bank valuation:** The lender will order a fresh valuation of your flat. If it's valued lower than you expected, your extraction amount drops.

**Approval:** The bank considers your income, existing debts (TDSR), and job stability. Freelancers or self-employed may face stricter scrutiny.

## Tax Implications in Singapore

**Personal tax:** The cash you extract is NOT taxable income. It's borrowed money, not income.

**Property-related tax:** If you use the cash to buy an **investment property**, you may owe ABSD (Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty) at 20% (citizen, 2nd property) or higher (foreigner). The refinancing itself doesn't trigger ABSD—only the new property purchase does.

**GST/Stamp Duty:** Refinancing itself doesn't trigger stamp duty. You only pay for the legal and bank processing fees.

## When a Cash-Out Refinance Makes Sense

**Scenario 1: Buying a second property**
- Extract $150,000 from your HDB at 2.3%
- Use it for down payment + ABSD on a $600k condo
- Avoid a personal loan at 6.5%

**Scenario 2: Upgrading to a larger HDB**
- Extract $50,000 for down payment on a resale flat
- Avoid waiting 3–5 years for a BTO

**Scenario 3: Investing in stocks or businesses**
- Extract $100,000 at 2.3% mortgage rate
- Invest in index funds or a business with 6–8% expected return
- Profit = investment return minus mortgage interest

## When NOT to Do It

**Overleveraging:** If you're already tight on cash flow, adding $300+/month in mortgage payments risks default.

**Short holding period:** If you plan to sell the property in 3–5 years, you might not recoup the refinancing costs.

**Falling market:** If property values drop, you're underwater (owe more than the property is worth).

**No clear use for the capital:** Extracting cash "just in case" is expensive. Only borrow if you have a specific, high-return use.
