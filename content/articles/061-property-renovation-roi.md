# Renovation Before Sale: Which Upgrades Recover Cost?

Dated kitchen. Tired finishes. Should you renovate before selling?

**Rule:** Only renovate if you recover 80%+ of cost in sale price.

## Renovations That Work

**Kitchen renovation ($30k):** Recover $24-28k (80%) ✓
- Modern finishes attract buyers
- Reduces negotiation pushback

**Bathroom update ($15k):** Recover $12-15k (80-100%) ✓
- Tile, fixtures, vanity
- High ROI; buyers prioritize bathrooms

**Paint only ($2k):** Recover $3-5k (150%+) ✓✓✓
- Cheapest upgrade; highest ROI
- Neutral colors only

## Renovations That Flop

**Complete reno ($80k):** Recover $40-50k (50%) ✗
- Too expensive; buyers have own design taste
- You've already sunk cost; buyer won't pay for it

**Custom finishes ($50k):** Recover $15-25k (30%) ✗
- Your taste ≠ buyer's taste
- Buyers want builder-neutral finishes

**Hardwood floors ($25k):** Recover $8-12k (30-50%) ✗
- Expensive; pet/allergy concerns; buyers often prefer vinyl

---

## The Better Path: Staging

Cost: $3-5k
Recovery: $15-30k (300-600% ROI)

Declutter, rearrange, paint (neutral), add plants. Buyers see potential.

**Staging wins >> renovation in ROI.**
