# Rental Yield Strategy: Targeting 4%+ Returns with Unit Type and Location

Most Singapore rental investors aim for 3-4% gross yield. Elite investors consistently achieve 4%+. The difference isn't luck—it's deliberate strategy around unit type, location timing, and tenant quality.

## Yield Calculation

**Gross Yield = Annual Rent ÷ Purchase Price**

Example: $500k property renting for $20k/year = 4% gross yield

**Net Yield = (Annual Rent - Annual Costs) ÷ Purchase Price**

Annual costs: Property tax ($300-500), maintenance reserve ($2-3k), agent fees (8% of rent). Net yield is typically 0.5-1.5% lower than gross.

## Unit Type Yield Strategy

**2-Bedroom Flats (HDB/Condo):**
- Typical price: $300-400k (HDB) / $600-800k (Condo)
- Typical rent: $2,200-2,800/month
- Gross yield: 6-5.6% (HDB) / 4.4-4% (Condo)
- Best for: High gross yield; HDB 2-rooms strong performers

**3-Bedroom Flats:**
- Typical price: $400-500k (HDB) / $800k-1.2M (Condo)
- Typical rent: $2,800-3,500/month
- Gross yield: 8.4-7% (HDB) / 5.25-3.5% (Condo)
- Best for: Balanced yield + appreciation potential

**4-Bedroom Flats:**
- Typical price: $500-600k (HDB) / $1.2-1.8M (Condo)
- Typical rent: $3,500-5,000/month
- Gross yield: 8.4-7% (HDB) / 5-3.3% (Condo)
- Best for: Upgraded rental (student accommodation, expatriate housing)

**Key insight:** HDB typically yields 6-8% gross; Private condo yields 3-5% gross. If yield is your goal, HDB outperforms significantly.

Same property 3 years later (MRT now open): 4.5% yield @ $550k = $24.75k annual rent

Paradox: Your annual rent drops slightly, but your property appreciated $170k. Early adopters in emerging areas capture BOTH yield ($80k cumulative rent) AND appreciation ($170k). Latecomers get appreciation only.

**Low-yield locations (mature, premium):**
- Central areas (Clementi, Bedok, Tampines): Strong demand but prices bid up to justify 3% yield
- New launch areas: Premium pricing leaves little yield room (2.5-3%)

## Tenant Quality & Yield Reality

**Professional tenants (corporate, stable employment):** Pay on time, less turnover, lower vacancy.
- Expected rent paid: 97-99% of quoted rent annually
- Turnover cost: 0 months (they renew leases)

**Student/transient tenants:** Higher turnover, occasional late payment, but willing to pay premium for proximity to universities.
- Expected rent paid: 90-95% (occasional defaults, vacancy between tenants)
- Turnover cost: 1-2 months every 2 years (restocking, repairs)

**Affordable rental (HDB, budget condo):** Mixed quality, higher defaults risk.
- Expected rent paid: 85-90% (more frequent defaults, longer recovery time)
- Turnover cost: 2-3 months every 3 years (legal action, eviction delays)

## Tenant Quality Scoring Framework

Score each prospective tenant (1=low, 5=high risk):

| Factor | Score 5 (Safe) | Score 3 (Moderate) | Score 1 (High Risk) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Employment** | Established company (10+ yrs tenure) | Mid-size firm or 3+ years | Startup, contract, or unknown |
| **Income** | 3x rent + savings proof | 2x rent, moderate savings | 1.5x rent, minimal savings |
| **Payment History** | References show on-time history | Occasional delays (1-2x) | Late payments frequent or legal action |
| **Lease Duration** | 24+ months signed | 12 months | Month-to-month or short-term |
| **Guarantor** | Parent or employer co-sign | Corporate housing (stable) | No guarantor |

**Scoring:** 
- 20-25 = Professional tenant (hire immediately, 95%+ payment likelihood)
- 15-19 = Mixed tenant (acceptable, plan for 10-15% vacancy/default risk)
- <15 = High-risk tenant (price rent 15-20% premium as buffer, or decline)

**Yield Adjustment:**
- Safe tenant (score 20-25): Use 95% collection rate, 5% vacancy
- Moderate (score 15-19): Use 90% collection rate, 10% vacancy  
- High-risk (score <15): Use 85% collection rate, 15% vacancy OR reject

**Example:** Quoted rent $2,500/month
- Safe tenant: Expect $28,500/year ($2,500 × 11.4 months actual)
- Moderate: Expect $27,000/year ($2,500 × 10.8 months actual)
- High-risk: Expect $25,500/year ($2,500 × 10.2 months actual)

## 4%+ Yield Strategy (Practical Steps)

1. **Target HDB over private condo:** HDB naturally yields higher (6-8% vs. 3-5%)
2. **Buy in pre-growth areas:** Emerging locations with MRT openings in 2-4 years (temporary low prices, high rental demand)
3. **Unit type:** 2-3BR in HDB; strong student or young professional rental markets
4. **Tenant screening:** Professional tenants > transient. Accept 10-20% lower rent for stability.
5. **Manage turnover:** 12-month leases with 2-month notice; professional renewal reduces 3-month vacancy between tenants
