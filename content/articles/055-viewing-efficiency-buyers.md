# Viewing Efficiency: The Math of Finding Your Property

You've viewed 40 properties in 6 weeks. Still haven't made an offer. Meanwhile, your colleague viewed 10 properties and closed in 4 weeks.

The difference? Viewing efficiency. Serious buyers view 8-15 properties before making an offer (high hit rate). Window-shoppers view 50+ (low conviction).

## The Numbers

**Serious buyer:** 10 viewings → 1 offer (10% offer rate)

**Window-shopper:** 50 viewings → 1 offer (2% offer rate)

**Deal indicators (viewing efficiency):**

- If you've viewed 8+ properties without serious interest = market mismatch (budget too low, expectations too high)
- If you've viewed 3-4 and found one you like = normal (make offer)
- If you're viewing properties "just to see" without decision criteria = you're not serious yet

## Red Flags (Skip These Properties)

- Listed 6+ months without price reduction = something's wrong (overpriced or major defect)
- Building has multiple units for sale = distressed owner situation
- Agent is pushy ("you have to decide today") = market is soft; no rush
- Defects visible during viewing (water stains, musty smell, cracks) = inspection will confirm major issues

## Deal Signals (Make Offer)

- Property within your budget range (not stretching)
- Building fundamentals solid (no major defects visible)
- Market timing right (rates stable, supply adequate)
- You could see yourself living there 5+ years
