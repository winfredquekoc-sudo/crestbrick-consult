# Buyer's Remorse: The 6-Month Reality Check

You closed 6 months ago. You're now hearing yourself say:

- "I overpaid by $50k; market went down"
- "The MRT is farther than I thought; commute is 45 min"
- "The finishes are dated; renovation costs $80k"
- "The neighborhood is noisier than expected"
- "The building has maintenance issues; reserves are depleting"

These are the sounds of buyer's remorse. Here's how to avoid them.

## Pre-Purchase Checklist (Prevent Regret)

**Location verification:**
- [ ] Physically commute to work/school at peak hours (not off-peak)
- [ ] Visit neighborhood at night (noise, safety assessment)
- [ ] Talk to 3-5 current residents ("What do you wish you knew?")
- [ ] Check MRT/bus routes; confirm distance (not GPS estimates)

**Property inspection:**
- [ ] Get detailed structural inspection ($1,000)
- [ ] Ask seller when major systems last replaced (roof, HVAC, plumbing)
- [ ] Review building inspection reports (any major defects?)

**Financial verification:**
- [ ] Calculate total cost (purchase + closing + reno budget)
- [ ] Confirm you can afford it at higher interest rates (4.2% vs 3.8%)
- [ ] Build 3-month emergency fund (mortgage + maintenance)

**Market reality check:**
- [ ] Get 3 independent appraisals (not just bank appraisal)
- [ ] Compare to 5 recent comps
- [ ] Ask agent: "Is this fairly priced?"
