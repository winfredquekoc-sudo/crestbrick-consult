# Recession Selling: Price Reset Strategy

Recession = prices falling 5-10% annually. Buyer confidence low. Your $700k property now worth $630-660k.

---

## Strategy During Recession

**Price aggressively:** List 5-8% below last comparable sale (signal: fast close)
**Minimize contingencies:** Offer flexibility (no inspection contingency; close in 14 days)
**Market to non-distressed buyers:** Investors; downsizers; relocating families (not fear sellers)

---

## Timeline

Recession sales take 45-60 days (vs normal 60-90).

Reason: Fewer buyers; those who buy are decisive.

---

## Recovery Timing

Sold in recession at $630k. Market recovers in 3 years; property worth $700k.

**Good news:** You got out; recovered some cash
**Bad news:** Missed 10% recovery if you'd held

Recession selling = stop the bleeding, not max gain.
