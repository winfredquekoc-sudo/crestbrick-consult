# Buyer's Stamp Duty Breakdown: The Real Cost

You're buying a $700k condo. Your banker says "stamp duty is $10k." Is that correct? Is there a way to minimize it? What about ABSD?

Stamp duty varies wildly by buyer profile. Miss one detail and you overpay by $50-150k. Here's the exact breakdown.

## The Buyer Profiles (Singapore 2026)

| Profile | 1st Property | 2nd Property | 3rd+ | Special Cases |
|---------|------------|------------|------|--------------|
| **Singapore Citizen** | 1% | 20% | 30% | Married to foreigner: varies by IRAS ruling |
| **Singapore PR** | 5% | 30% | 35% | Exemptions possible via MAS ruling |
| **Foreigner** | 60% | 60% | 60% | No exemptions (foreign buyer ban in effect) |
| **Entity / Trust** | 65% | 65% | 65% | No exemptions (anti-money-laundering rules) |

**Key rule:** ABSD is *additional* to Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD). Total = BSD + ABSD.

---

## Singapore Citizen Buying 1st Property

**Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD):** 1%

**ABSD:** 0% (citizens exempt on 1st property)

**Total:** 1%

**Example: $700k condo**
- BSD: $7,000
- ABSD: $0
- **Total stamp duty: $7,000**

Other costs:
- Legal fees: $1,500-$2,000
- Valuation fee: $500-$800
- Mortgage insurance: 0-3% of loan amount (if LTV >80%)
- **Total closing costs: $10,000-$15,000**

---

## Singapore Citizen Buying 2nd Property

**Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD):** 1%

**ABSD:** 20%

**Total:** 21%

**Example: $700k condo**
- BSD: $7,000
- ABSD: $140,000
- **Total stamp duty: $147,000**

**That's $140k more than a citizen's 1st property.**

**Why so high?** Government disincentive for investors and upgraders holding multiple properties.

**Exemptions (rare):**
- Self-occupied (you live in it): No exemption; ABSD still applies
- Disposing property within 6 months of prior sale: Possible remission; check with IRAS
- Married to foreigner: Possible remission for 1 property; check IRAS

---

## Singapore Citizen Buying 3rd Property

**Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD):** 1%

**ABSD:** 30%

**Total:** 31%

**Example: $700k condo**
- BSD: $7,000
- ABSD: $210,000
- **Total stamp duty: $217,000**

**Who buys 3rd properties?** Investors, real estate speculators, or (rare) families living separately.

---

## Singapore PR Buying Any Property

**Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD):** 5% (vs 1% for citizens)

**ABSD (1st property):** 5%

**ABSD (2nd+ property):** 30%

**Example: PR buying 1st property, $700k condo**
- BSD: $35,000 (5% vs $7k for citizen)
- ABSD: $35,000 (5% vs $0 for citizen)
- **Total: $70,000**

**Citizen 1st: $7k. PR 1st: $70k. Difference: $63k.**

---

**Example: PR buying 2nd property, $700k**
- BSD: $35,000
- ABSD: $210,000
- **Total: $245,000**

---

## Foreigner Buying Any Property

**Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD):** 60% (yes, sixty percent)

**ABSD:** 60% (stacked on top)

**Total:** 120% *if both applied separately*

**But:** Singapore law stacks them, so it's:
- BSD: 60% of purchase price
- ABSD: 60% of (purchase price + BSD)

**Example: Foreigner buying $700k condo**
- Purchase price: $700,000
- BSD (60%): $420,000
- ABSD (60% of $700k + $420k = $1.12M): $672,000
- **Total stamp duty: $1,092,000**

**Total out-of-pocket: $700k + $1.092M = $1.792M for a $700k property.**

**Foreigner ban (effective 2023):** Foreign individuals cannot own landed residential property in Singapore (freehold or leasehold). Condos still allowed but heavily taxed.

## The Workaround: Entities and Trusts

**Can a foreigner form a Singapore company to buy property and avoid ABSD?**

No. Entities (companies, trusts) are taxed at 65% BSD + 65% ABSD, regardless of owner nationality.

**Example: Foreign-owned Singapore company buying $700k condo**
- BSD (65%): $455,000
- ABSD (65% of $1.155M): $751,500
- **Total: $1,206,500**

**Worse than individual foreigner ($1.092M).** Entities are penalized.

## The Married-to-Foreigner Exemption

**Singapore citizen married to foreigner (rare case):**

Possible exemption from ABSD on 1st property if:
1. Both are joint owners
2. Property is jointly occupied
3. IRAS approves (case-by-case ruling)

**Example: Citizen + foreigner spouse, $700k condo**

Without exemption:
- Citizen (1st prop): $7,000 BSD + $0 ABSD = $7k
- Foreigner (1st prop): $420,000 BSD + $420k ABSD = $840k
- **Total: $847,000**

With exemption (if approved):
- Citizen: $7,000
- Foreigner: $420,000 BSD only (ABSD waived)
- **Total: $427,000**

**Savings: $420k**

*But exemption is NOT automatic. You must apply to IRAS with marriage certificate + joint ownership docs.*

## The Timing Trap: Upgrading Citizens

**Citizen, selling 1st HDB, buying 1st private condo:**

You're entitled to ABSD exemption (still 1st residential property). But IRAS must recognize the property you're selling as your previous "1st property."

**Scenario 1 (Fast sale):**
- Sell HDB: May 15
- Buy private condo: June 20
- Status: Momentary owner of 2 properties (overlap 36 days)
- IRAS treats you as "2nd property buyer" if overlap >30 days
- **You pay 20% ABSD instead of 0%**

**Scenario 2 (No overlap):**
- Sell HDB: May 15
- Buy private: August 1 (75-day gap)
- Status: Sold 1st, now buying 1st again
- IRAS recognizes this as "1st property exemption"
- **You pay 0% ABSD**

**Lesson:** If upgrading, time the sale to close *before* buying (not simultaneously). Overlap = higher ABSD.

## How to Minimize Stamp Duty

**1. Delay 2nd property purchase**

Citizen with $700k property:
- Buy 1st property: $7k stamp duty
- Wait 3 years (own only 1 property)
- Sell 1st property
- Buy 2nd property: $147k stamp duty
- **Total: $154k**

vs.

- Buy 1st property: $7k
- Immediately buy 2nd (overlap): $147k
- Own 2 properties for 3 years
- Sell 1st: keep 2nd
- **Total: $154k (same), but now hold 2 for 3 years (carrying costs)**

**Verdict:** Order doesn't matter for total stamp duty, but timing of sale matters for overlap.

---

**2. Avoid 2nd property if possible**

If you're upgrading, sell the 1st *before* buying the 2nd (avoids overlap ABSD surcharge).

---

**3. PR considering citizenship**

PR paying 5% BSD + 5% ABSD ($70k on $700k 1st property) should consider PR→citizenship path:
- Citizenship: $1k BSD (huge savings)
- Timeline: 2-5 years
- Cost: $0 (application free)

---

**4. Married to foreigner?**

Apply to IRAS for exemption *before* buying. If approved, save $420k ABSD on 1st property.
