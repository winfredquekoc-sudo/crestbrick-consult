# Timing Your Purchase: When to Buy (And When to Wait)

Interest rates are rising. Developer supply is high. But your friend just bought and urged you to act fast. Should you?

The answer depends on macro signals: Are rates peaking? Is supply about to dry up? Is an MRT opening shifting demand? This article teaches you to read market cycles so you buy at the bottom, not the top.

## The Four Macro Signals

**Signal 1: Interest Rates (40% of timing decision)**

Current rate: 3.8%. If rates are rising, buy now (lock in before going to 4.2%). If rates are falling, wait (refinance later at 3.4%).

**Signal 2: Developer Supply (30% of timing)**

High supply (2,000+ units): Prices soft, buyer power high. Low supply (300-500 units): Prices firm, seller power high.

**Signal 3: School/MRT Openings (20% of timing)**

New MRT in 2 years? Buy now (prices rise 10-15%). Already open 1+ year? Too late; appreciation priced in.

**Signal 4: Buyer Sentiment (10% of timing)**

Market crash fears = soft prices, best time to buy. Market boom = high prices, worst time to buy.

## The 4-Year Cycle

**Year 1: Recovery** → Start hunting, pre-approve, don't rush

**Year 2: Expansion** → Make offers, close deals (not desperate)

**Year 3: Peak** → Don't buy (prices at high)

**Year 4: Correction** → Buy aggressively (cycle bottom)
