# Multiple Offers: When Bidding Wars Favor You

You have 5 offers. One is $700k cash; one is $720k with financing contingency; one is $690k no contingency.

Which do you accept?

Not always the highest. Strategic choice.

---

## The Rankings

**Best offer (in order):**
1. Highest price + no contingencies + fast close
2. Highest price + no contingencies + normal close
3. High price + appraisal contingency (but proven buyer)
4. Highest price + financing contingency (risk)
5. Lower price + all contingencies (avoid)

---

## Counter-Strategy

When you have multiple, counter the highest at +$10k:

Buyer A: $700k
Counter: $710k (to buyer A, and to others)

Forces competitive bids. Most will walk. One or two stay bidding.

Final: $720k (instead of original $700k).
