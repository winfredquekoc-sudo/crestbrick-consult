# Buyer Psychology: How Sellers Trigger Your FOMO

You saw a property online. The listing says "Multiple inquiries, viewing ends Friday."

Your heart rate rises. You call your agent. "Can I see it TODAY?" You haven't even confirmed if it fits your needs. You're now operating on emotional triggers, not logic.

This is buyer psychology. Sellers engineer these triggers to speed your decision.

## The Three Triggers

**Trigger 1: Scarcity (40% of emotional buys)**

"Only 2 units left in this building." Forces urgency.

Your logic: "There are 3,000 condos for sale in Singapore."
Your emotion: "But THIS building, THIS unit, might be gone."

---

**Trigger 2: Social Proof (35% of emotional buys)**

"This unit is similar to the one sold last month for $750k."

Your logic: "Market conditions change; comps are historical."
Your emotion: "Everyone else is paying $750k; so should I."

---

**Trigger 3: Urgency/Time Pressure (25% of emotional buys)**

"Seller needs to close by end of month; viewings end Friday."

Your logic: "There will be other properties."
Your emotion: "This is MY property; I can't lose it."

---

## How to Avoid Emotional Buying

- [ ] Set a strict budget BEFORE viewing (don't overbid emotionally)
- [ ] View 8-10 properties (removes scarcity perception of any single unit)
- [ ] Walk away from 5-6 properties (proves to yourself that choices exist)
- [ ] Wait 48 hours before making offer (sleep on it; emotion fades)
- [ ] Compare to actual comps (don't let seller's comp cherry-picking influence you)
