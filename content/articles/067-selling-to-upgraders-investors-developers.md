# Know Your Buyer: Tailor Your Pitch

Same property. Three buyer types. Three different sales approaches.

---

## Upgraders (40% of buyers)

**What they want:** Lifestyle, location, move-in ready
**How to sell:** Emphasize finishes, schools, MRT access, neighborhood vibe
**Negotiation:** Less price-sensitive; more lifestyle-sensitive

---

## Investors (40% of buyers)

**What they want:** Yield, rental demand, tenant pool
**How to sell:** Show rental comps, tenant demand, ROI potential
**Negotiation:** Price-sensitive; will negotiate hard on yield math

---

## Developers (20% of buyers)

**What they want:** Land value, acquisition at volume
**How to sell:** Emphasize land value, zoning, acquisition multiple
**Negotiation:** Will offer 5-10% below market for multiple units

---

## Real Example

Property: $700k condo, prime location, good schools

**To upgrader:** "Perfect for young family; top schools; 10-min MRT"
**To investor:** "Rental comps $3.5k; 6% yield; strong tenant demand"
**To developer:** "Land value $400/sqft; acquisition opportunity"
