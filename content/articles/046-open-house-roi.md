# Open House Reality: ROI, Timing & When to Skip It

Open houses sound logical: 100 people view simultaneously, competitive energy, fast close. Reality: 1-2 actual buyers show up, zero offers result, you hosted a 3-hour open house for curiosity-seekers.

## The Brutal Stats

**National average (Singapore market data proxy):**
- Open houses: 2-5% convert to offers
- Private viewings by appointment: 8-15% convert to offers
- Open house attendees who are actual buyers: 15-20%

**Example: 50 people attend open house**
- Actual buyers: 8-10 people
- Serious about this property: 2-3
- Submit offers: 0-1

**Contrast: 10 people view by appointment (pre-filtered by agent)**
- Actual buyers: 8-10 people
- Serious about this property: 6-8
- Submit offers: 1-3

**Lesson:** Open houses attract volume, not quality.

## When Open Houses Work (3 Conditions)

**Condition 1: Price-to-market is excellent** ($50-100k below market)
- Buyers are hungry for deals
- Open house attracts deal-hunters who bite fast
- ROI: 3-5% convert

**Condition 2: Property is staged perfectly** (professional staging, marketing-grade photos, model-home feel)
- Buyers feel confident ("This place is showroom-ready")
- Word-of-mouth is strong
- ROI: 4-8% convert

**Condition 3: Heavy buyer traffic normally in your district** (hot market, many first-time buyers, new MRT opening nearby)
- Open house rides market momentum
- Attendee pool is larger; more serious buyers in mix
- ROI: 5-10% convert

**If fewer than 2 conditions are met: Skip the open house. Waste of time.**

## What Happens at Open House (Breakdown)

**Attendees:**
- 40%: Curious neighbors ("What's this unit worth?")
- 30%: Window-shopping buyers (not serious, just looking)
- 20%: Tire-kickers (agents bringing clients to comparative shop)
- 10%: Actual serious buyers

**Time commitment (seller perspective):**
- 2 hours: Prep (cleaning, staging, declutter)
- 3 hours: Open house (standing around, answering questions)
- 1 hour: Cleanup

**Total: 6 hours for 5-10 serious buyer interactions**

**Alternative (private viewing by appointment):**
- 30 min: Prep (standard maintenance, not intensive)
- 1 hour: 2 back-to-back viewings
- 15 min: Light cleanup

**Total: ~2 hours for 2 targeted buyer interactions**

**Efficiency:** Private appointments: 1 buyer per hour. Open house: 1-2 buyers per 6 hours. Private appointments win 3-4x.

## The Offer Paradox

**Offers rarely come from open house attendees.** Why?

Buyers who attend open houses are in "research mode" (browsing, comparing, not decided). Buyers who call agent to schedule private appointment are in "decision mode" (narrowed to 2-3 finalists, ready to move).

**Rare exception:** Couple attends open house, both love it instantly, next-day offer. Happens <5% of the time.

**Normal path:** Open house attendee thinks "nice place," does additional research, contacts agent 2 weeks later asking "Is that property still available?" By then, other offers have come in.

## When to SKIP Open House

- Market is soft (inventory > demand; open houses attract browsers, not buyers)
- Property is dated/needs work (don't showcase weakness; private viewings allow buyer imagination)
- Neighborhood has security concerns (open house draws wanderers with no intent to buy)
- You have limited energy/availability (you're busy; 6-hour commitment is high)
- Offer already in hand (open house might scuttle it with competing offer drama)
- Selling during off-season (few active buyers; attendee quality is low)

## When to HOLD Open House

- Market is hot (inventory < demand; buyers are hungry)
- Price is exceptional (below-market attracts deal-hunters who act fast)
- Property is move-in ready (staging is perfect; show it off)
- Buyer traffic in district is naturally high (post-MRT opening, new school, etc.)
- You have 2+ agents actively marketing (they bring qualified buyers)

## The Alternative: Open House Lite

**Instead of full open house, try:**

1. **"Agent open" (agents only):** Invite all agents in district to viewing. Agents bring pre-screened buyers. ROI: 5-10% because attendees are already qualified.

2. **Social media open:** Post "viewing available Sat 2-5pm" on social channels. Buyers call agent to schedule. Attracts self-selected pool. ROI: 3-5%.

3. **Private appointment blitz:** Schedule 8-10 back-to-back 20-minute appointments over 2 hours (multiple agents, multiple buyers). Quick viewings, high quality. ROI: 10-15%.

## The Offer Mechanics (When One Does Come)

If open house miraculously generates an offer:
- It usually comes 24-48 hours after open house (buyer is serious, has time to think)
- Offer is often at asking price or slightly below (buyer didn't haggle emotionally during viewing)
- Closing timeline is usually longer (buyer is less committed than one who scheduled appointment)

**Lesson:** If open house generates offer, it's often from the ONE serious buyer in the crowd. They would have scheduled private appointment anyway.

## The Checklist: Should You Open House?

- [ ] Market conditions: Hot or soft?
- [ ] Property condition: Move-in ready or needs work?
- [ ] Your energy: Available 6+ hours?
- [ ] Buyer traffic in district: High or low?
- [ ] Competing offers: Any in hand already?
- [ ] Alternative: Can agent do private blitz instead?

**If 3+ conditions favor open house, do it. Otherwise, skip and invest time in targeted private appointments.**
