# Sale Collapse: Recovery Strategy

You had an accepted offer. Buyer's financing fell through. Deal dies. Now what?

---

## Immediate Actions

**Day 1:** Retrieve earnest deposit (if buyer backed out with cause)
**Day 2:** Get feedback (why did deal collapse? Market signal?)
**Day 3:** Re-list (with price adjustment if needed)

---

## Price Reset

If deal died due to low appraisal:
- Original list: $720k
- Appraisal came in: $680k
- New list price: $685k (accept reality)

Trying to re-list at $720k wastes time (market already said $680k).

---

## Market Response

**Soft market (many deals dying):** Reduce price 5-10%; close faster
**Normal market:** Reduce price 2-3%; remarket with new photos
**Hot market:** Relist at same price; new buyers will come

---

## Prevention

**Don't let buyer contingencies delay:** Close financing ASAP
**Don't rely on one buyer:** Keep property actively shown during inspection period
**Don't lose deposit:** Ensure earnest deposit is substantial ($20k+)
