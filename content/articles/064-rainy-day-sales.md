# Rainy Day Sales: When You NEED to Sell Fast

Job loss. Medical crisis. Relocation in 30 days. You need to sell NOW, not in 90 days.

Fast-close strategy:

---

## What Works

**Price:** List 5-8% below market (attracts urgent buyers)
**Marketing:** "Motivated seller" language (subtly signals urgency)
**Contingencies:** None (no inspection contingency, no appraisal contingency)
**Close timeline:** 14-21 days (not 45)
**Staging:** Bare minimum (clean, not renovated)

---

## What Doesn't

**Listing at market price:** Sends "I'm not desperate" signal (you are)
**Long close timeline:** "I'll decide later" mentality in buyers
**Heavy contingencies:** Buyers walk; you don't have time to re-list

---

## Real Example

Market price: $700k
Your urgency: Sell in 30 days

**Strategy:**
- List at $660k (6% discount)
- "Fast close available; owner motivated"
- Accept any offer $640k+ within 2 weeks
- Close in 14 days (no contingencies)

**Result:** Sell for $655k in 18 days (vs. $700k in 90 days = +$55k loss for faster exit)
