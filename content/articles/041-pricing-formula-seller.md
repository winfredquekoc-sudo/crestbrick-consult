# The 5-Factor Pricing Formula: How to Price Your Property Correctly

Overpriced properties sit on market 6-12 months. Underpriced properties sell in 2-4 weeks for $20-50k less than achievable. The difference is a simple formula. Most agents use vague language ("market comps," "trending down"). Here's the mechanics.

## The 5-Factor Model

**Floor price = District baseline per sqft × your sqft**

Example: Tampines baseline (typical 3BR resale) = $900/sqft. Your 1,000 sqft = $900k floor.

**Factor 1: Size (±3-5% variance)**
- Smaller units: -3% (dense living, harder resale)
- Larger units: +3-5% (premium scarcity)

**Factor 2: Age (±5-10% variance)**
- <5 years new: +5-10% (modern, warranty)
- 5-15 years: 0% (sweet spot)
- 15+ years: -5-10% (older, potential repairs)

**Factor 3: Location within district (±3-8% variance)**
- MRT <400m: +5-8% (convenience premium)
- MRT 400-800m: +2-3% (still accessible)
- MRT 800m+: 0% to -3% (isolation tax)

**Factor 4: Views/orientation (±2-5% variance)**
- High floor + unobstructed view: +3-5%
- Low floor / blocked view: -2-3%

**Factor 5: Condition (±5-15% variance)**
- Renovated, move-in ready: +5-10%
- Dated, needs work: -5-15%

## Real Example: Tampines 3BR HDB

**Floor:** 1,000 sqft × $900/sqft = $900,000

**Adjustments:**
- Size (1,000 sqft = average): 0%
- Age (12 years old = mid-range): 0%
- MRT (550m away = good access): +4%
- Views (high floor, unobstructed): +3%
- Condition (renovated 2022, move-in): +6%

**Total variance: +13%**
**Target price: $900k × 1.13 = $1,017,000**

Market comps confirm: similar units selling $1,010-1,025k. Price at $1,015k (midpoint).

## When Agents Lie (The Overpricing Game)

Agents list at $1,100k to win your business ("I can get you more!"). Reality: it sits 120+ days, relisted at $980k, sells for $30k LESS than it would have at $1,015k originally. You lose time + money + buyer confidence.

**Red flag:** Agent valuation >5% above recent comps = they're playing you.

## When Sellers Cheat Themselves (The Underpricing Gamble)

You list at $950k thinking "quick sale is worth $65k discount." It sells in 10 days. You feel smart; you're actually $40-50k short of fair market.

**Check:** Compare your price to formula. If formula says $1,015k and you're at $950k, you're leaving $65k on the table.

## Private Condos: Same Formula, Higher Variance

Premium condo in District 9 (Orchard): baseline $1,200/sqft, 1,500 sqft = $1.8M floor.

Variance is bigger because luxury has more subjective factors (view, finishes, building prestige). Range: $1.65M-$2.1M (±8-15%).

## The Checklist

- [ ] Identify district baseline $/sqft (recent comps, agent reports)
- [ ] Measure your sqft accurately
- [ ] Calculate floor price
- [ ] Score each of 5 factors (size, age, location, views, condition)
- [ ] Apply variance total (±5% conservative, ±15% aggressive)
- [ ] Compare to recent comps (must align)
- [ ] If agent valuation >5% off formula, get second opinion
