# Photography & Listing: The Visual Edge

Professional listing photos: $400-800
Result: 30-50% more inquiries, 5-10% higher sale price
ROI: 15-30x

Phone photos: $0
Result: 10% fewer inquiries, 3-5% lower price
ROI: Negative

---

## What Works

**Professional photography:** Wide angles, natural light, staged
**Virtual tour:** 3D walkthrough (3-minute viewing)
**Floor plan:** Clear, labeled (sqft, room names)
**Listing copy:** "Light, spacious, well-maintained" beats "nice place"

---

## What Doesn't

**Phone photos:** Blurry, weird angles, unclean
**No floor plan:** Buyers can't visualize layout
**Weak copy:** "Apartment for sale" tells nothing
