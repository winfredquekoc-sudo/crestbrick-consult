# Contingency Clauses: Protect Yourself

Buyer makes offer with three contingencies:
1. Subject to inspection
2. Subject to financing
3. Subject to appraisal

Each gives buyer a walk-away option. You're holding property off market for 60+ days, and buyer might bail.

---

## Contingency Strategy

**Accept:** Inspection contingency (unavoidable; buyers need protection)
**Limit:** Financing contingency (require pre-approval proof)
**Minimize:** Appraisal contingency (require appraisal-waiver clause if possible)

---

## What To Negotiate

**In offer with contingencies:**
- Require earnest deposit ($20k minimum; forfeited if buyer walks without cause)
- Require inspection within 7 days (not 14; speeds timeline)
- Require appraisal within 10 days
- Limit inspection scope (structural + systems; not cosmetics)
