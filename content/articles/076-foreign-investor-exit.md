# Foreign Investor Exit: Complex Taxation

Foreigner bought condo 5 years ago for $700k. Now worth $850k. Selling triggers multiple taxes.

---

## Taxes on Exit

**ABSD:** Already paid $420k at purchase (can't recover)
**Withholding tax:** 10-15% of gain ($15k-22.5k on $150k gain)
**Stamp duty:** 5% on sale proceeds ($42.5k)

---

## Total Cost of Exit

Sale price: $850k
- Withholding tax: $18k
- Stamp duty: $42.5k
- Agent fee (2%): $17k
- Legal: $2k

**Net after costs: $770.5k** (only $70.5k gain on 5 years)

---

## Timing Strategy

If market is rising 3-5% annually:
- Sell now: $850k proceeds, $770.5k net
- Sell in 2 years: $950k proceeds, $860k net (better)

Exit timing for foreign investors = maximize capital gains before withholding tax eats them.
