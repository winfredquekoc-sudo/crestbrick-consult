# Selling Inherited Property: Probate, Timeline & Tax Implications

You've inherited a property. It's in your family's name, but you're not on the title. Selling inherited property has legal gates that regular sales skip: probate, dual-ownership mechanics, and a critical tax-free window. Miss any of these and you're either delayed 12+ months or overpay taxes.

## The Probate Timeline (3-18 Months)

**Scenario: Parent dies, leaves $800k property to 3 adult children equally**

**Months 1-3: Letters of Administration**
- File death certificate with Supreme Court
- Petition for Letters of Administration (proof you're authorized to act)
- Cost: $500-1,500 (legal fees)
- Delay: 4-12 weeks (courts queue applications)

**Months 2-4: Debt settlement & asset valuation**
- Identify all debts (mortgage, creditor claims, funeral costs)
- Appraise property (IRS valuation for estate tax, if applicable)
- Settle debts from estate (if any)

**Months 4-6: Deed transfer to beneficiaries**
- Court issues probate order (you're now co-owner)
- Property transferred to all beneficiaries (3 siblings on title jointly)
- Land office updates records

**Months 6-18: Sale (if all siblings agree)**
- If any sibling disagrees with sale, you're stuck (need unanimous consent to list)
- If one sibling is incapacitated/overseas, delays extend further

## The Co-Ownership Problem

Three siblings own the property jointly. To sell:
- All 3 must sign the Option to Purchase
- All 3 must sign the Sale & Purchase agreement
- All 3 must collect proceeds (unless you have Power of Attorney)

**What if one sibling is:**
- Overseas and unreachable: Get Power of Attorney (costs $1-2k, delays 4-8 weeks)
- Incapacitated/mentally unfit: Court guardianship order (delays 3-6 months)
- Refuses to sell: You cannot force sale (unless court orders partition sale, expensive and slow)

**Solution:** Negotiate upfront. Before listing, get written consent from all siblings agreeing to sale price and timeline. If one sibling objects, work through mediation now (costs $2-5k, 4-6 weeks) vs. mid-sale crisis.

## The Tax-Free Window (Critical!)

**Singapore inheritance tax:** There is no inheritance tax in Singapore (since 2008). But there IS a "owner-occupier exemption" on capital gains:

**Scenario: Parent bought property 20 years ago for $300k. Today it's worth $800k. Gain = $500k.**

**If you sell within 12 months of inheriting:**
- Gain may qualify for stepped-up basis (you inherit at $800k market value, zero gain)
- Sell at $800k = zero taxable gain

**If you sell 13+ months after inheriting:**
- Stepped-up basis may lapse in some jurisdictions (Singapore policy unclear)
- Consider selling within 12 months if gain is large

**Best practice:** Consult a tax advisor (costs $500-800) before listing. Confirm your specific tax treatment. If stepped-up basis applies and gain is >$200k, time your sale within 12 months of inheritance.

## Sibling Buyout Strategy (Common Scenario)

One sibling wants to buy out the other two and keep the property.

**Fair buyout calculation:**
- Appraise property: $800k
- Sibling A owns 1/3, wants to keep property
- Sibling B & C own 1/3 each (want cash)

**Buyout amount:** Each of B & C gets $800k ÷ 3 = $266.7k

**Financing catch:** Sibling A must refinance or come up with $533.4k cash. Most can't. Negotiation happens:
- "I'll pay you $230k each ($460.5k total) over 12 months"
- Costs B & C liquidity but keeps property in family
- Or: Sell property, split $800k 3 ways ($266.7k each), B & C happy, A gets nothing

## The Probate Will vs. No-Will Scenario

**With a will:** Executor named in will; faster path (2-6 months)
**Without a will:** All beneficiaries must petition jointly; slower (3-12 months), higher legal costs

**Singapore law:** If deceased has no will, property goes to intestate heirs (spouse, children, parents in that order). If multiple children, all are co-owners automatically. Get legal advice immediately to understand your specific chain.

## Selling Timeline Expectation

- Months 1-6: Probate clearance, Letters of Administration, deed transfer
- Months 6-8: Sibling agreement, agent selection, pricing (if no delays)
- Months 8-14: Listing, showing, offers, inspection, closing
- **Total: 12-18 months from death to cash in hand**

If siblings disagree or title complications emerge, timeline can extend to 24+ months.

## Inherited Property Checklist

- [ ] Letters of Administration filed (or will executor appointed)
- [ ] All siblings' consent obtained in writing (for sale)
- [ ] Property appraised for tax purposes
- [ ] Tax advisor consulted (stepped-up basis confirmation)
- [ ] Mortgage or debts on property identified & plan to settle
- [ ] Power of Attorney obtained (if any sibling unavailable to sign)
- [ ] Mediation arranged (if any sibling disputes sale)
- [ ] Title search done (any caveats or encumbrances?)
- [ ] Joint tenancy or tenancy-in-common structure confirmed

## Real-World Inherited Sale Example

Property: $700k HDB, three siblings as co-owners, deceased owned 20 years

Timeline:
- Month 1-3: Probate clearance ($1.2k legal fees)
- Month 4-5: Sibling mediation ($3.5k for two disagreeing on price)
- Month 5-6: Agree $700k; agent selected
- Month 6-12: Listed, 6-month slow market (market soft)
- Month 12: Offer at $650k (renegotiated down due to length of listing)
- Month 12-13: Closing, split proceeds 3 ways ($216k each after agent fees & costs)

**Lesson:** Family delays + market timing = significant impact. Each sibling compromise costs money.
