# 6 Months to Selling: Your 180-Day Prep Checklist

Six months until listing is your decision window. By day 180, you should have chosen your agent, understand your likely sale price (±5%), and have zero outstanding maintenance surprises. First-time sellers, upgraders, and investors each have different priorities at this stage.

## Money: Pricing Research & Agent Selection (Weeks 1-8)

**Interview 3-5 agents; don't pick the highest valuation.** Agents who overprice ($50-100k above market) are setting you up for 6-month stagnation and eventual repricing downward (buyers sense desperation). Pick the agent whose valuation is grounded in recent comps and market evidence.

**Run a valuation checklist:**
- Size: square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms (matches comps?)
- Age/condition: brand new vs. 20-year-old (5-10% variance)
- Views/location: corner unit, high floor, MRT proximity (3-8% variance)
- Recent comps: units in your building sold in last 90 days? Prices? Holding period?

**Target price range: narrow it to ±3%.** If comps suggest $700-720k, price at $715k (midpoint). Overpricing by 5% ($750k) costs you 3-4 months of wasted market time and eventual repricing loss.

**Agree on agent commission structure now.** Standard is 2-2.5%; negotiate down to 2% if market is strong. Get it in writing.

## Timing: Market Position & Infrastructure Timing (Weeks 8-16)

**Identify buyer seasonality for your property type:**
- HDB upgraders? List January-March (post-CNY, before school fees drain savings)
- Private condo mature area? List post-summer (July-August soft; September picks up)
- Investor property? List 30 days before tenant lease ends (empty possession premium)

**Check for near-term infrastructure changes** (MRT openings, school expansions, cooling measures):
- MRT opening in 6-12 months? List AFTER opening (connectivity is real, not speculative; buyers pay for reality)
- School expansion nearby (30% more capacity)? List 12 months BEFORE (market doesn't price it in yet; you capture the pre-announcement upside)
- New cooling measures announced? List 60 days before implementation (buyers rush to beat ABSD increase or LTV tightening)

**Calculate your optimal listing window.** Most likely: 4-6 months from now (90-180 days).

## Safety: Inspection & Disclosure Prep (Weeks 12-26)

**Commission a professional inspection (not a casual walkthrough).** Costs $1,500-2,500; identifies structural issues, electrical faults, plumbing leaks. If the inspection finds $30k in work needed, you decide NOW whether to fix or discount. Buyers will inspect anyway; your pre-emptive inspection avoids surprises and price-collision negotiation trauma.

**Get contractor quotes for any major findings.** Roof leak? $5-15k depending on scope. Electrical non-compliance? $2-8k. Have these quotes ready for buyers' inspectors and negotiators.

**Prepare your disclosure document:**
- Previous water damage? Disclose it (with photos of repairs)
- Pest treatment? When, what type, follow-up schedule
- Structural work? Ceiling cracks, re-leveling, reinforcement
- Appliance failures? Age of major systems (aircon, water heater)

**Proactive disclosure reduces buyer friction by 30-40%.** A buyer who sees "water damage 2020, professionally repaired June 2022" trusts you more than one who discovers a stain during their inspection and assumes a cover-up.

## Audience-Specific Actions (Weeks 16-26)

**Upgraders:** Confirm your next property search is live. By month 6, you should have 2-3 target properties in mind. Your sale will take 60-90 days; you need 30 days overlap to avoid rental gap. Coordinate your timelines.

**Investors:** If property is tenanted, issue 4-month notice now for 2-month overlap pre-sale (tenant moves, you stage/repair). Empty possession sells 5-10% faster. If tenant is month-to-month, convert to fixed 12-month lease starting now (gives you control at month 9).

**First-time sellers:** Start reading contracts. Understand Option to Purchase (OTP) mechanics, when inspections happen, what's non-negotiable vs. negotiable.

## The 180-Day Milestone

By day 180, you should have: agent selected, price range locked, inspection completed, disclosure doc ready, and buyer seasonality window identified. You're no longer in prep mode; you're in launch-readiness mode. The next 90 days are execution: staging, marketing, showing, negotiating.
