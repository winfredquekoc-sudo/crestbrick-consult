# Condo Fees: The Seller's Liability

Condo fees: $300/month
Buyer sees total cost: $300/month × 360 = $108k over 30 years (added to mortgage)

High fees scare buyers.

---

## Disclosure Requirements

**Must disclose:**
- Current condo fees ($300/month)
- Recent increases (was $250; jumped to $300)
- Reserve fund status (healthy or depleting?)
- Major upcoming repairs (roof, piping, concrete spalling)

**Failure to disclose:** Buyer can sue for non-disclosure (cost: $20-50k)

---

## Buyer Pushback

Buyer sees $300/month fees + aging building + low reserves:

"I'm reducing my offer by $50k because long-term costs are high."

You lose $50k due to fee disclosure.

---

## Mitigation

**If fees are high:** Be transparent early (price into initial listing)
**If building is aging:** Budget major repair cost into sale price
**If reserves are low:** Mention upcoming special assessment (buyers will learn anyway)
