# Landed Property: Land Value Dominates

For condos, 80% value = building.
For landed, 80% value = land.

Selling landed property is really selling LAND.

---

## Market Dynamics

**Developer interest:** May offer premium if zoning allows redevelopment
**Preservation orders:** Can restrict renovations; buyers less interested
**Land value:** Drives price more than structure condition

---

## Selling Strategy

**Position as land:** Don't renovate structure (developer will tear down)
**Highlight zoning:** Good location? Close to MRT? Can be redeveloped?
**Target developers:** Direct outreach (not just retail buyers)

---

## Example

Landed property: $1.2M (mostly land value)
Structure: Dated, needs $200k reno

**Don't:** Renovate ($200k spent, add $50k value)
**Do:** Sell as-is to developer at premium (they value land; renovations irrelevant)
