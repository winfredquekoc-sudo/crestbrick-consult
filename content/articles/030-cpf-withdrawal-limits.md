# CPF Withdrawal Limits: When You Can Draw Down for Housing

CPF (Central Provident Fund) provides preferential withdrawal rights for housing, but there are strict limits. Understanding these limits helps you plan realistic down payments and avoid withdrawal rejections.

## CPF Housing Withdrawal Entitlement

**Basic rule:** You can withdraw CPF OA (Ordinary Account) for housing if you're buying your first residential property and meet age/property criteria.

**Withdrawal limits by property type:**

**HDB flat (owner-occupied):**
- Max withdrawal: 100% of OA (no limit)
- Age limit: None (can withdraw at any age)
- Timeline: Can withdraw before property is purchased (if you have a valid HDB booking offer)

**Private residential property (condo, landed):**
- Max withdrawal: Lesser of (a) $200,000, or (b) 100% of OA
- Age limit: 35 years or older (some exceptions for younger first-time buyers)
- Timeline: Only after completion (property is yours)

**Key constraint:** If buying private property below age 35, CPF withdrawal is restricted or unavailable. You must use cash.

## Mandatory Set-Aside (Medisave & Special Account)

CPF isn't fully available—portions are ring-fenced:

**Medisave Account (MA):**
- Mandatory set-aside for healthcare: $7,725 (age 65+) down to $450 (younger)
- Cannot withdraw for housing

**Special Account (SA):**
- Mandatory set-aside for retirement: Set amount by age
- Cannot withdraw for housing

**Example (age 35):**
- Total CPF OA: $100,000
- Less MA: -$7,725
- Less SA: -$30,000
- **Available for housing: $62,275**

This means even if you accumulate $100k in CPF, only ~$62k is available for housing.

## Withdrawal Process & Timeline

**For HDB:**
1. Obtain HDB Booking Offer
2. Apply to CPF for withdrawal (via CPF Board website)
3. CPF approves and releases funds to HDB Lawyer
4. Funds transferred at completion

**Timeline: 2–4 weeks**

**For Private Property:**
1. Complete the purchase (property transfer registered)
2. Apply to CPF for withdrawal (via CPF Board)
3. CPF approves and releases funds to your bank account or lawyer
4. Funds transferred after completion

**Timeline: 3–6 weeks (must wait for completion first)**

## MSR/TDSR Constraints on CPF

Interestingly, CPF withdrawal affects MSR/TDSR calculations:

**Scenario:** You withdraw $50k CPF and use $20k cash for a total $70k down payment.

**TDSR calculation:**
- Remaining loan: $280,000
- Monthly payment: $1,340
- Your income: $5,000
- MSR: $1,340 ÷ $5,000 = 26.8% ✅ (passes)

**If you had less CPF and needed to borrow more:**
- Loan: $330,000
- Monthly payment: $1,575
- MSR: $1,575 ÷ $5,000 = 31.5% ❌ (fails)

**Takeaway:** Larger CPF withdrawals → lower loan amounts → easier MSR/TDSR qualification.

## Voluntary vs Mandatory CPF Contributions

**Mandatory:** Employer + employee contributions (17.5% + 8% of salary)

**Voluntary contributions:** You can contribute extra CPF above mandatory limits, up to $15,000/year.

**Benefit of voluntary contributions:** You get tax relief (SGD contributions reduce taxable income by $37,740/year max), and the money grows at CPF rates (currently 2.5–4%).

**Strategy:** If planning to buy in 3–5 years, maximize voluntary CPF contributions to boost your housing withdrawal pool.

## CPF Withdrawal for Second Property

**Can you withdraw CPF for a 2nd property?** 

**No.** CPF housing withdrawal is limited to your first residential property purchase. If you already own a property (HDB or private), CPF withdrawal for a 2nd property is not permitted.

**Exception:** If you sell your first property and buy a second, you may be eligible to withdraw CPF for the new purchase, as long as you no longer own the first.
