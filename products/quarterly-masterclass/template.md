# Quarterly Masterclass — Template

**Format:** 90-min live (Zoom), recording sent to ticket holders
**Cadence:** Quarterly, aligned to 4-Pillar quarterly editorial themes
**Price:** SGD $99 / ticket (early-bird $79)
**Capacity:** 100–150 live; replay sold separately at $49 post-event
**Host:** Winfred Quek

---

## Strategic intent

Each quarter takes ONE pillar or ONE timely policy / market shift and goes deep. Not a generic market update — a strategic working session. Attendees leave with a worksheet, not just notes.

Quarterly theme rotation (default):
- Q1 — Affordability + interest rate outlook
- Q2 — Structure: ABSD/decoupling deep dive
- Q3 — Cash flow + rental cycle
- Q4 — Exit strategy + year-ahead playbook

| Block | Time | Section |

## Slides outline (15 core slides)

1. Title slide — Masterclass # / Quarter / Topic
2. Who this is for + ground rules
3. The market signal — chart of the trigger event
4. Why most people will get this wrong
5. The Crestbrick / 4-Pillar take (POV slide)
6. Framework intro — diagram
7. Framework component 1
8. Framework component 2
9. Framework component 3
10. Case study — the setup
11. Case study — the analysis
12. Case study — the outcome + lesson
13. Worksheet walkthrough
14. Common mistakes / disqualifiers
15. Next steps + offer slide (Cohort, Course, 1:1)

---

## Case study structure

- **Setup (3 min):** Anonymised household — age, income, current property, decision facing them
- **Analysis (10 min):** Apply the pillar framework live; show the math
- **Outcome (5 min):** What they did, what we'd do differently, the takeaway
- **Lesson box:** 3 bullets attendees can copy into notes

Use real deals from the past 6 months. Get written permission. Strip identifying details.

---

## Q&A structure

- Submit questions via Zoom Q&A tab (not chat)
- Triage: Winfred reads → groups by theme → answers in batches
- Time-box: 30 min hard cap; unanswered questions get follow-up email
- Do NOT give CEA-regulated advice live; redirect property-specific questions to 1:1
