# Lawyer White-Label Portfolio Audit — Spec

**Product:** B2B2C delivery of the Crestbrick 4-Pillar Portfolio Audit, white-labelled to Singapore conveyancing / family law firms
**Model:** Fee split with referring firm; client-facing brand is the firm's, methodology and analyst is Winfred / Crestbrick
**Target partners:** Mid-size SG firms (5–25 lawyers) handling conveyancing, matrimonial asset division, estate planning
**Pilot size:** 5 firms, 6-month pilot

---

## Why this exists

Conveyancing and family-law clients regularly face property decisions their lawyer cannot advise on (CEA boundary). Firms either:
- Refer to an agent (loses control of the relationship)
- Stay silent (client makes a poor decision, firm takes reputational risk)

White-labelled Portfolio Audit gives the firm a value-add deliverable:
- Branded as the firm's "Strategic Property Review"
- Delivered alongside the legal work
- Increases firm's perceived value, deepens client relationship
- Crestbrick gets distribution + a pipeline of investor-mind clients

---

## Standard audit deliverable (white-label)

PDF report, 12–18 pages, lawyer-branded cover and footer, Crestbrick methodology page in appendix.

### Sections
1. Cover (firm logo, client name, date, matter reference)
2. Executive summary — 1 page
3. Client situation snapshot — 1 page
4. Pillar 1: Affordability analysis — 2 pages
5. Pillar 2: Stamp duty + structure — 2–3 pages (most-used in conveyancing context)
6. Pillar 3: Cash flow + holding power — 2 pages
7. Pillar 4: Exit strategy — 2 pages
8. Synthesis: Blueprint score + recommendations — 2 pages
9. Appendix: methodology, "prepared in collaboration with Crestbrick", disclaimers, CEA notice

### Variants
- **Conveyancing variant:** emphasis on Pillar 2 (structure) + Pillar 1 (affordability stress)
- **Matrimonial variant:** emphasis on division mechanics + Pillar 4 (exit) for the retained party
- **Estate variant:** emphasis on Pillar 4 (legacy hold) + structure for heirs

---

## Lawyer-branded cover spec

- Firm logo top-left (provided by firm, vector)
- Firm colour as accent stripe
- "Strategic Property Review" or firm-chosen title
- Client name, matter reference, date
- Footer: "Prepared in collaboration with Crestbrick. Property analysis methodology by Winfred Quek (CEA R073319H)."

Crestbrick branding is appendix-only and footer-only. Client-facing identity is the firm's.

---

## Commercial terms

- **Fee per audit:** SGD $1,200 (standard) / $1,800 (matrimonial or estate variant — more complex)
- **Split:** 50/50 between firm and Crestbrick
- **Firm collects:** firm bills client, remits Crestbrick share monthly
- **Volume tier:** above 10 audits/quarter, split shifts to 60 firm / 40 Crestbrick
- **Exclusivity:** non-exclusive; firms may engage other advisors for non-property matters
- **Term:** 6-month pilot, then annual auto-renew unless either party gives 30-day notice

## Intake form (filled by lawyer or client, sent to Crestbrick)

1. Client name + matter reference
2. Variant requested (conveyancing / matrimonial / estate / general)
3. Properties owned (address, tenure, year acquired, current value est., outstanding loan)
4. Household income + dependents
5. Decision facing client (buy / sell / decouple / hold / divide)
6. Timeline / urgency
7. Specific questions the lawyer wants addressed
8. Documents attached (CPF statements, IRAS docs, valuation reports, mortgage letters)
9. Lawyer's redaction notes (what NOT to include in client-facing report)
10. Delivery deadline

Form lives in shared firm portal (or simple Typeform initially). Crestbrick SLA: 5 business days from complete intake to delivery.

---

## Workflow

1. Firm identifies client need → sends intake form
2. Crestbrick reviews intake within 1 business day; flags gaps
3. Crestbrick prepares audit (3–4 business days)
4. Draft sent to firm for review (firm checks for legal-positioning conflicts)
5. Firm signs off → final PDF delivered to firm-branded
6. Firm presents to client
7. Crestbrick available for 30-min joint call with firm + client if requested ($300, billed separately, split per main terms)

## Compliance

- Crestbrick / Winfred remains the licensed CEA party for any property advisory
- Firm does NOT cross into property agency work; their value-add is the relationship + the legal work
- Audit explicitly states: not legal advice, not a valuation, not an offer to act as agent
- Each report carries CEA registration line + firm's UEN
