# Investor Cohort — Charter

**Format:** Private Telegram group + monthly live Q&A (Zoom)
**Price:** SGD $99 / month (or $999 / year — 2 months free)
**Cap:** 150 members (waitlist beyond)
**Host:** Winfred Quek

---

## Who this is for

- Singapore-based property investors / upgraders making a move in the next 6–24 months
- People who've read the Almanac, taken the course, or attended a masterclass and want ongoing intel
- Owners of 1–3 properties weighing decoupling, second property, or exit
- Professionals (lawyers, accountants, mortgage brokers) who want to keep current on SG residential

This is NOT for: pure speculators, agents prospecting members, or hobbyists wanting tip-style flips.

---

## What's promised

### Weekly market intel (every Monday)
- 1 short Telegram post: the one thing that mattered last week + what to do about it
- Link drops: URA / HDB / MAS releases as they happen, with Winfred's read
- Launch-week notes when a major project drops

### Monthly live Q&A (last Thursday, 8pm SGT, 60 min)
- Zoom, recorded for members
- Submit questions in advance via Telegram
- Winfred answers in batches by theme
- Guest expert ~quarterly (mortgage broker, conveyancing lawyer, etc.)

### Member directory (opt-in)
- Searchable list: name, area of interest, profession, what they're working on
- Use case: find a sparring partner, swap notes, quiet intros

### Quarterly Almanac addendum
- 4–6 page PDF, members-only, between annual editions

### Office-hours bookings (members-only rate)
- 30-min 1:1 at member rate ($99 vs public $199)

---

## What's explicitly NOT included

- Tip-style speculation ("buy this unit now")
- Specific buy/sell calls on individual units (CEA boundary)
- Free unlimited 1:1 advisory
- Mortgage brokering, legal advice, tax advice (referrals only)
- Off-topic content (crypto, equities, MLM, etc. — instant remove)
- Agent-style listings dumps

If you want individualised advice on a specific property, that's a 1:1 engagement, not the cohort.

---

## Behaviour code

1. **No selling to members.** No DMs pitching properties, services, courses. One warning, then removed (no refund).
2. **No screenshots out.** Cohort discussion is private. Sharing screenshots externally = removed + banned.
3. **Cite or caveat.** If you're stating a market fact, cite the source. If you're guessing, say "guess".
4. **Disagree on substance, not on people.** Robust debate is the point.
5. **No agent prospecting.** Practising agents may join as members but cannot solicit.
6. **English primary.** Side channels in other languages OK; main feed English so all can follow.
7. **Stay on-topic.** SG residential property + adjacent (rates, policy, macro). Nothing else.

Violations: warning → 7-day mute → removal. Egregious (doxxing, harassment, leaks): immediate removal, no refund, banned from all Crestbrick products.

- **Crestbrick-initiated discontinuation of the cohort:** pro-rata refund of unused period.
- **Re-entry after voluntary exit:** allowed, subject to cap + waitlist.
- **Re-entry after removal for cause:** not allowed.
