# SG Property Almanac — Annual Edition Template

**Format:** PDF, ~50 pages, designed (not just text)
**Cadence:** Annual, published mid-January (covers prior calendar year + year-ahead view)
**Distribution:**
- **Free tier:** Email opt-in (lead magnet) — full PDF
- **Paid tier (optional A/B):** $49 — bundled with quarterly addenda + Excel data tables
**Author:** Winfred Quek, Crestbrick

---

## Strategic intent

The Almanac is Crestbrick's flagship authority asset. One document a year that:
1. Compounds positioning — "the Almanac guy"
2. Feeds the email list (free tier)
3. Anchors every other product (course, cohort, masterclass cite the Almanac)
4. Forces Winfred to do the deep annual research that informs all advisory work

Voice: investor-minded almanac, not agent newsletter. Cite sources. Show working. Be willing to be wrong on the record.

## §1 — Year in Review (URA / HDB / MAS)

- URA: PPI movement, transaction volumes, CCR/RCR/OCR splits, rental index
- HDB: resale price index, MOP supply pulse, BTO launch tally, million-dollar flat trends
- MAS: rate trajectory, TDSR/LTV changes, mortgage stress data
- One page each: URA chart, HDB chart, MAS chart — annotated
- Closing summary: "What actually moved the market this year"

---

## §2 — Policy Timeline

- Single-page visual timeline (Jan to Dec) of every policy / cooling measure / regulatory note
- For each major event: 1 paragraph context + 1 paragraph "what investors should have done"
- Red-flag boxes for events still playing out

---

## §3 — District Performance Tables

- Table 1: Top 10 / bottom 10 districts by % price change
- Table 2: Top 10 / bottom 10 by rental yield change
- Table 3: Volume leaders (transaction count)
- Table 4: HDB town heatmap
- Commentary: 2 pages picking apart the surprises (not the obvious winners)

---

## §4 — New Launch Calendar Lookback

- Every major new launch from the year: project, developer, tenure, units, top-line price psf, sell-through %
- Winners + losers (2-page commentary)
- Lessons: which launches investors should have skipped, which they should have chased

---

## §5 — Predictions for Next Year

- 5–7 specific, testable predictions (price band, policy, segment)
- Confidence rating per prediction (low / med / high)
- "What would change my mind" trigger per prediction
- One contrarian call clearly flagged
- Prior-year prediction scorecard (be honest about misses)

---

## §6 — The 4-Pillar Framework Primer

- Condensed version of the full course intro
- One page per pillar: definition, key questions, common mistakes
- Closing: how to read the rest of the Almanac through the 4-Pillar lens
- CTA to course / cohort / masterclass

---

## §7 — Appendix

- Data sources + URLs (URA, HDB, MAS, SRX, EdgeProp)
- Methodology notes (how district tables computed, what's excluded)
- Glossary: TDSR, MSR, BSD, ABSD, SSD, MOP, en-bloc, etc.

---

## §8 — Back Matter

- About Winfred (CEA R073319H, PropNex, Crestbrick)
- What's next: masterclass schedule, cohort signup, 1:1 booking
- Disclaimer (no individualised advice; CEA compliance line)

## Visual / design spec

- Cover: bold typographic, year prominent, "Volume [N]" — collectible feel
- Section opener pages with full-bleed photo + pull quote
- Charts: consistent colour palette (Crestbrick dark + accent)
- Tables: monospaced numerics, zebra rows, source line beneath each
- Pull-quote callouts every 4–6 pages for skimmers
- Page numbers + section header in footer

## Production timeline (annual)

- **Oct:** outline + data pull begins
- **Nov:** §1–§4 drafted (the historical sections)
- **Dec:** §5 predictions + last-minute year-end data
- **Early Jan:** design pass
- **Mid Jan:** publish + launch email sequence
- **Quarterly:** addenda PDFs (paid tier) — 4–6 pages each

- **Path A (recommended for Volume 1):** Free, email-gated. Maximises list growth + authority.
- **Path B:** $49 paid PDF + free 8-page "executive summary" as lead magnet.
- Decide after Volume 1 data: if list grows >2,000 from launch, stay free; if conversion to paid products lags, switch to paid.
