## What this product is

A weekly research letter for Singapore property investors who already own at least one property and want diagnostic, math-led analysis they can't get from agent newsletters or property portals. Each issue takes one topic and goes deep — tables, sourced numbers, contrarian framing, and a "what I would do" closer.

It is not a deal-flow service. It is not a market roundup. It is not a product placement vehicle for new launches. The premium tier exists because the free list cannot, by design, carry the depth and specificity that paid subscribers need.

---

## What this product is not

- Not a daily/weekly listing newsletter
- Not a "buy now" tipsheet
- Not gated behind upsells, referrals, or hidden costs
- Not a community/forum (that's a separate product, possibly later)
- Not for first-time buyers — the assumed reader has been around the block

---

## Audience profile

**Tier 1 ICP:** SG private property owner, 38–58, household income $20k+/mo, holds 1–3 properties, considering a portfolio move (decoupling, upgrade, second purchase, lease rotation, en-bloc decision) within 24 months.

**Tier 2 ICP:** Professional in finance, law, or tech who treats SG property as a portfolio asset, not a home. Values rigour over speed.

**Anti-ICP:** Tip-chasers, flippers, anyone whose first question is "which condo to buy."

## Launch gating (do not launch before both clear)

1. Free list ≥ 5,000 active subscribers (active = opened/clicked any of trailing 6 issues)
2. Free-list open rate ≥ 40% sustained over trailing 8 issues, two consecutive months

These gates are non-negotiable. Premium without proven free-list quality is a one-and-done product.

## Cap policy

The 250-sub cap is the product. When full:

1. Open a waitlist with quarterly intake batches (60–80 seats per batch as churn allows).
2. Do not raise the cap.
3. If demand consistently exceeds supply for four consecutive quarters, raise the price ($29 → $39). Existing members grandfathered.

---

## Voice and quality bar (every issue must clear all four)

1. **Single-topic depth.** No roundups. No three-things-this-week.
2. **Math or sourced numbers visible.** A table, a chart reference, or specific URA/IRAS/SLA citations. No vague "prices are up."
3. **One contrarian or counter-conventional take.** What the standard advice gets wrong, and why.
4. **A "what I would do" closing paragraph.** Diagnostic before prescriptive. Honest about what depends on the reader's situation.

If an issue fails any of the four, it does not ship. It gets rewritten or pulled and replaced from the buffer.
