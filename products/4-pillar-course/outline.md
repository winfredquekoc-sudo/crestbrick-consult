## Module 1 — Foundations: Investor-Mind vs Agent-Mind

**Learning objective:** Student can articulate the difference between buying property as a consumer vs as an investor, and identify which mode their last decision was made in.

**Lessons:**
1. Why most Singaporeans pick the wrong property (and don't know it)
2. The agent-mind: emotional, transactional, single-deal thinking
3. The investor-mind: portfolio, optionality, decade-long view
4. The 4-Pillar Portfolio Audit — overview
5. How the rest of the course is structured

**Exercise:** Audit your last property decision. Write 1 page: what drove it — emotion, agent push, family pressure, or investor logic?

**Deliverable:** "Decision Mode" self-score (1–10) carried forward into Module 6.

---

## Module 2 — Pillar 1: Affordability

**Learning objective:** Student can compute their true affordability ceiling — not just bank TDSR — and stress-test it against rate moves and life events.

**Lessons:**
1. TDSR / MSR mechanics — what banks actually check
2. The hidden affordability layers: opportunity cost, liquidity, dependents
3. Stress-testing for +200bps rate moves
4. Affordability vs sustainability — what happens at year 7
5. Common over-extension traps (the "stretch and pray" deal)

**Exercise:** Build your personal Affordability Ceiling spreadsheet (template provided).

**Deliverable:** Pillar 1 score + max safe quantum.

---

## Module 3 — Pillar 2: Stamp Duties + Structure

**Learning objective:** Student understands BSD, ABSD, SSD, and decoupling — and can model how ownership structure changes total cost and exit flexibility.

**Lessons:**
1. BSD / ABSD / SSD — current rates and trigger points
2. Single-name vs joint-name vs decoupling
3. When decoupling makes sense (and the 3 cases it doesn't)
4. Trust structures, 99-1, and recent IRAS guidance
5. The cost of getting structure wrong — case studies

**Exercise:** Map two ownership scenarios for your household and compute total stamp duty + future ABSD exposure.

**Deliverable:** Pillar 2 score + chosen structure.

---

## Module 4 — Pillar 3: Cash Flow + Holding Power

**Learning objective:** Student can build a 10-year cash flow projection for any property and identify the holding-power break point.

**Lessons:**
1. Real cash flow vs marketed yield — what the brochure hides
2. Vacancy, maintenance, MCST, property tax — the drag stack
3. Rental cycles in SG (2010–2025 lookback)
4. Building 10-year holding-power projections
5. When negative carry is acceptable (and when it isn't)

**Exercise:** Build cash flow model for a target property using provided template.

**Deliverable:** Pillar 3 score + minimum cash buffer required.

---

## Module 5 — Pillar 4: Exit Strategy

**Learning objective:** Student designs the exit before they buy — knowing which buyer pool they'll sell into and what conditions trigger an exit.

**Lessons:**
1. Why "I'll just sell it later" is not a strategy
2. The 3 exit types: upgrade-funder, cash-out, legacy hold
3. Buyer pool analysis — who buys your unit at year 8?
4. MOP, lease decay, en-bloc probability
5. Pre-committing exit triggers (price, time, life event)

**Exercise:** Write a 1-page exit memo for your target property.

**Deliverable:** Pillar 4 score + written exit triggers.

---

## Module 6 — Synthesis: Building Your Blueprint

**Learning objective:** Student combines all 4 pillar scores into a single Property Blueprint and uses it to greenlight, modify, or kill a deal.

**Lessons:**
1. Combining the 4 scores — weighting and trade-offs
2. The Blueprint scorecard (template)
3. Walking through 3 real-deal blueprints (case studies)
4. When to walk away — disqualifier patterns
5. Reviewing and updating your Blueprint annually

**Exercise:** Complete the full Property Blueprint for your next intended move.

**Deliverable:** Signed-off Blueprint PDF + optional 1:1 review session add-on.
