## Opening hook (0:00–0:45)

There's a reason this course is called the *4-Pillar* Investor Course and not the "10 Property Tips" course or the "Ultimate Guide to Singapore Real Estate."

Four. That's it. If you can hold four ideas in your head, in the right order, and refuse to sign anything until all four clear — you will outperform 90% of Singapore property buyers across your lifetime.

The 4-Pillar framework isn't theory. It's the exact audit I run on every client portfolio that walks into my office. Every "should we buy this?" question I get reduces to these four. Let's lay them out.

## Core teaching (0:45–8:30)

### Why four — and why these four

Property decisions in Singapore have a thousand variables. Lease decay, MCST fees, rental yields, ABSD rates, MOP, en-bloc probability, district scoring, school proximity, MRT proximity. You can drown in variables.

The 4-Pillar framework is a forcing function. It says: *of all the variables, these four are the ones that, if you get any of them wrong, the deal fails — regardless of how right everything else is.*

You can buy the right unit, in the right district, at the right time, and still lose money if any one Pillar collapses. Conversely, you can buy a "boring" unit and win if all four Pillars are solid.

### Pillar 1 — Affordability

**The question it answers:** *Can I sustain this through the worst 18 months of the next decade?*

Not "can I service the loan today" — banks already check that and they're checking the wrong horizon. Pillar 1 stress-tests against:

- +200bps rate move
- One income temporarily gone (parental leave, retrenchment, sabbatical)
- A child enters international school / university
- An aging parent needs medical capital

If any combination of two of those breaks the deal, Pillar 1 fails. The fix is usually quantum reduction, not deal abandonment. We do the full mechanics in Module 2.

### Pillar 2 — Structure (Stamp Duties + Ownership)

**The question it answers:** *Does this ownership structure preserve my next move, or kill it?*

Pillar 2 is where the most expensive avoidable mistakes live. BSD, ABSD, SSD, single-name vs joint, decoupling, 99-1 trusts, recent IRAS guidance.

The Pillar 2 audit asks: given your household's *next* likely move (3–10 years out), does the structure on *this* purchase open or close that move? Wrong answer often costs $30K–$80K. Module 3 walks through every common scenario.

### Pillar 3 — Cash Flow + Holding Power

**The question it answers:** *How long can I hold this if the market doesn't cooperate?*

Pillar 3 is the antidote to "marketed yield." Brochures show 3.2% gross. Reality after vacancy, MCST, property tax, agent fees, maintenance reserve, and tenant turnover is often 1.4–1.8%.

Pillar 3 forces a 10-year cash flow projection — every line item, every assumption stress-tested. Output: minimum cash buffer required, and the holding-power break point in months. Module 4.

### Pillar 4 — Exit Strategy

**The question it answers:** *Who buys this from me at year 8, and what do they pay?*

The Pillar most people skip entirely. "We'll see how the market is" is not an exit strategy — it's a wish.

Pillar 4 forces three things, *before* signing:

1. The buyer pool at exit (upgrader, downsizer, investor, foreigner — and who's allowed to buy at that point).
2. The exit triggers (price target, time limit, life event).
3. The exit type — upgrade-funder, cash-out, or legacy hold. Each has a different optimal hold period.

Module 5.

### How the 4-Pillar audit actually runs

For every property decision, you score each Pillar 1–10. The Blueprint scorecard (Module 6) combines them into a single readout:

- **All four ≥ 7:** green-light.
- **One Pillar 4–6, others ≥ 7:** yellow — modify the deal (different unit, different structure, different quantum) until that Pillar clears.
- **Any Pillar ≤ 3:** red — kill the deal. No exceptions. Even if the other three are 10s.

The reason "one red kills it" is non-negotiable: a Pillar at 3 is the failure mode that wrecks the other three over time. A weak exit eventually crushes affordability. A weak structure eventually crushes cash flow. The Pillars are connected.

## Real example (8:30–10:30)

Live audit, abbreviated. Client at the time of consult — single male, 41, $200K income, no existing private property, $400K cash + CPF, looking at a $1.7M 2-bedder CCR resale.

Scoring:

- **Pillar 1 (Affordability):** 8/10. Comfortably within stress-test envelope. Income stable, no dependents.
- **Pillar 2 (Structure):** 9/10. Single-name, his only private, no ABSD complications. Clean.
- **Pillar 3 (Cash Flow):** 5/10. CCR rental yield was thin (~2.1% gross, ~1.3% net). Negative carry ~$1,400/month if rented out. He intended to live in it — that mitigates but doesn't erase the issue if life changes.
- **Pillar 4 (Exit):** 4/10. CCR resale 2-bedder buyer pool at year 8 in this stack was a coin flip — could be other singles, could be foreigner-affected, could be thin.

Final: green on the first two, yellow-to-red on the back two. Verdict was *not* "don't buy" — it was "not this unit, not this stack."

We pivoted to a $1.45M 2-bedder in a different CCR project with a better-defined buyer pool and a more livable layout. Same Pillar 1 and 2 scores. Pillar 3 moved to 7. Pillar 4 moved to 7. Green light.

He's still in it. Two and a half years in. Pillars are holding.

## Exercise (10:30–11:15)

Take the property you've been thinking about — or your current home, if there's no active hunt. Score each Pillar from 1 to 10 *based on what you know right now*. You're allowed to put question marks where you don't have data.

The point of doing it now, before Modules 2–5, is to see how much you don't yet know. By the end of the course you'll redo this exercise and the question marks will be gone.

This is your **Decision-Mode self-score**, and it carries through to Module 6.

---

## Key takeaways

- The 4-Pillar framework: Affordability, Structure, Cash Flow, Exit. In that order.
- Each Pillar answers one specific question that the others can't answer for it.
- Scoring is 1–10 per Pillar; one red kills the deal regardless of the other three.
- Modules 2–5 each go deep on one Pillar; Module 6 synthesises into your Blueprint.
