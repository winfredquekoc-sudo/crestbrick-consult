### Score interpretation

- **48–60** — Investor-mind. Already operating in portfolio mode. The course will sharpen, not rebuild.
- **30–47** — Hybrid. Strong instincts, missing structure. The 4-Pillar framework is the upgrade you need.
- **0–29** — Agent-mind on this decision. Don't be defensive — most Singaporeans score here on their first property. The whole rest of the course is built for this starting point.
