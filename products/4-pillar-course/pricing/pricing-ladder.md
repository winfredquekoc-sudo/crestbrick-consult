## Tier 1 — Self-Paced ($499)

**Who it's for:** HDB upgraders or first/second private buyers who want the framework on their own schedule. Math-led, self-directed, comfortable with reading and spreadsheet work without a facilitator.

**What's included:**
- Lifetime access to all 6 modules (~25 video lessons, ~6 hours total)
- Workbook PDF (60–80 pages) with worksheets and exercises
- 4 Excel/Google Sheets templates (Affordability Ceiling, Stamp Duty Modeller, Cash Flow 10-yr Projection, Blueprint Scorecard)
- 3 anonymised case studies
- Module quizzes with answer keys
- Welcome + completion email sequences
- Free updates to v1.1, v1.2 content as IRAS / cooling-measure changes are issued
- Crestbrick alumni-only Almanac discount (15% off the next annual edition)

**What's NOT included:**
- Any direct access to Winfred (no email Q&A, no 1:1, no community)
- Personalised review of their numbers

**Price logic:** $499 sits at the upper end of evergreen self-paced courses in SG and the lower end of "professional financial framework" pricing. It's high enough to filter for serious buyers, low enough that the math obviously pays for itself ($499 vs the $42K K&M case-study mistake = 84x ROI on a single avoided structure error). Round to $499 not $500 for psychology + Stripe-friendly.

## Tier 2 — Self-Paced + 1:1 Blueprint Review ($799)

**Who it's for:** Self-paced buyers who want one set of expert eyes on their completed Blueprint before they actually pull the trigger on a property. Most common buyer: an upgrader 60–90 days from a real decision.

**What's included:**
- Everything in Tier 1
- **One 60-minute 1:1 Zoom session with Winfred Quek** after Module 6 completion
- Personalised review of their completed Blueprint scorecard
- Written follow-up summary (email, ~1 page) of session takeaways and recommended next steps
- Priority email Q&A for 30 days post-session

**What's NOT included:**
- Ongoing advisory after the 30-day post-session window (that's the Cohort or a separate retainer)
- A property recommendation (this is education + framework, not a transaction-tied service — important for CEA compliance)

**Capacity:** Soft cap ~6 sessions/month based on Winfred's calendar. If demand exceeds, queue with delivery within 6 weeks of purchase. Don't oversell.

**Price logic:** $300 over base. A standalone 60-min advisory session with Winfred privately is in the $400–$600 range, so $300 over the $499 base is a discount-via-bundle that rewards course completion. The 1:1 is *gated by Module 6 completion* — students who don't finish the course don't get the session, which keeps quality high and reduces no-show waste.

## Tier 3 — Cohort ($1,499)

**Who it's for:** Buyers who want accountability, peer learning, and live access. Typical profile: a couple making a decision in the next 3–6 months, comfortable with a structured weekly commitment.

**What's included:**
- Everything in Tier 1 (full course access from week 1)
- **6-week structured cohort run** — one module per week (Modules 1–5), final week for Blueprint synthesis and live presentations
- **Weekly live Q&A on Zoom** with Winfred (90 min, recorded for replay)
- **Private Discord community** for the cohort — peer questions, accountability check-ins, deal-stage discussions
- **Peer Blueprint reviews** — paired student-to-student review in week 5, plus group review in week 6
- **One 60-min 1:1 with Winfred** post-cohort (same as Tier 2)
- Crestbrick alumni status — invite to a private alumni Discord across all cohort runs, ongoing
- Cohort-only office hours: ad hoc 30-min slots through cohort run

**What's NOT included:**
- Recordings of other cohorts (each cohort is self-contained)
- Permanent 1:1 access (alumni Discord is the long-term touchpoint)

**Capacity:** **Hard cap 10 students per cohort.** This is non-negotiable — the format breaks above 10. Run 3–4 cohorts/year (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4). Maximum revenue: 4 × 10 × $1,499 = $59,960/year from this tier alone, before any base-tier sales.

**Price logic:** 3x the base course. Justified by:
- ~9 hours of live Winfred time per cohort run (6 × 90-min live Q&A + 60-min 1:1) = ~54 minutes per student of direct facilitator time, plus async Discord
- Peer learning value (10 motivated investor-minded peers; the network effect is part of the product)
- Accountability — completion rates on cohort versions of courses run 70–90%, vs 5–15% on self-paced
- Scarcity — only 30–40 seats/year. Acts as the natural "capacity sink" for premium demand without burning out the operator

**Launch sequencing:** Don't run cohort in v1 launch month. Sell self-paced first; open cohort wait-list after ~30 evergreen sales to pre-validate demand. First cohort run no earlier than 60 days after public launch.

## Discounting and bundles (defer / explicit no's)

- **Almanac bundle:** Annual Almanac PDF as a free add-on for any course tier at launch (limited-time, first 50 buyers). Reverts to paid bundle after.
- **Alumni → Cohort upgrade:** Tier 1 graduates get $300 off the cohort within 12 months (effective $1,199 for alumni upgraders).
- **No "early bird" course discounts.** Pricing is the pricing. Discounting at launch trains buyers to wait.
- **No affiliate program in v1.** Revisit at month 6 if volume warrants.
- **No coupon codes for media / podcast partners** unless the partner is producing dedicated long-form content; otherwise it's free distribution that erodes pricing.
