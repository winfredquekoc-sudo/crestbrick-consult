Hi [NAME],

Here is something that catches many HDB sellers off guard.

When you sell, the CPF savings you used toward your flat must be refunded to your CPF account, together with the accrued interest those savings would have earned had you never touched them. It is not a penalty. It is simply your own money being made whole. But it does mean the cash in hand after a sale is often smaller than people assume, which directly affects what you can put toward the upgrade.

Knowing this number early changes how you plan. It is one of the figures we work through in the free 30 min numbers session, alongside your loan position and your timeline.
