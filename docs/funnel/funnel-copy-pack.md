## 4. FAQ / OBJECTION HANDLING (NEW)

**Section heading:**
Questions Upgraders Ask Before They Book

**Q1. Is it really free?**
Yes. The 30 min session and your roadmap cost you nothing. It exists so you can make a clear decision with real numbers. If we are a good fit to work together later, great. If not, you still walk away better informed.

**Q2. Will you pressure me to buy or sell?**
No. This is a diagnostic, not a sales pitch. The whole point is to find the right answer for you, even when that answer is do nothing right now.

**Q3. What if my CPF or cash is not enough?**
Then you will know that clearly, and you will know what would need to change for the move to work. Sometimes the gap is small and fixable with the right sequencing. Sometimes the honest call is to wait and prepare. Either way, you stop guessing.

**Q4. I am not ready to sell my HDB yet. Is this still for me?**
Especially for you. The best upgrade decisions are made well before you list anything. Knowing your numbers early means you move when the timing is right, not when you feel rushed.

**Q5. How is this different from a regular agent?**
A regular agent leads with listings. Winfred leads with your numbers. You get a strategy, an exit plan and a clear go or wait decision first. The property search only begins once the plan makes sense.

**Q6. What exactly do I walk away with?**
A clear read on what your CPF and cash can carry, your realistic price range, your loan and MSR position, your timing options, and a straight answer on whether to move now or wait. Plain numbers you can act on.
