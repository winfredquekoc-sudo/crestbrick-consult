## Title Tag Issues

### Systematic Truncation Problem (High Priority)

53 of 132 insight articles (40%) have title tags that are cut off mid-phrase, producing misleading search snippets. The `<title>` tag content ends early while the `og:title` contains the full intended title. This is a template-level bug, not individual article issues.

**Example of the mismatch:**
- `<title>`: `ABSD Singapore 2026: Every | Winfred Quek CEA R073319H`
- `og:title`: `ABSD Singapore 2026: The Definitive Reference, Winfred Quek`
- The `<title>` ends with the word "Every" — not a complete thought, and "The Definitive Reference" is a keyword-rich phrase that never appears in the title tag.

Google predominantly uses `<title>` for search result snippets. When the title is truncated, Google either rewrites it from the body text (unpredictable) or shows the truncated version (poor CTR).

### Five Specific Article Title Recommendations

| `decoupling-singapore` | `Decoupling Singapore 2026: When \| Winfred Quek CEA R073319H` | "When" is incomplete; primary keyword "cost" and "ABSD" absent from title | `Decoupling in Singapore 2026: Cost, Math & ABSD Saved` |
