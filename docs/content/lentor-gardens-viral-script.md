## STEP 1: Reel and social intelligence

What actually performs for Singapore new launch property content, synthesised from publicly accessible creator guidance and established short form best practice. Honest caveat: Instagram and TikTok engagement metrics are gated, so no view or like counts are claimed here. This is pattern level guidance, not scraped numbers.

### Strongest 3 second hook formulas
1. Curiosity gap question. "Why does nobody show you the real math on a Lentor unit." Question style hooks are the single largest category in large clip studies.
2. Contrarian or myth bust. "Stop balloting this launch until you watch this." Disrupts the buyer assumption and earns the watch.
3. Number led. "Three bedroom types here. One I would not ballot." Specific numbers signal utility and a payoff.
4. Authority plus data. "As a licensed advisor, here is what the floor plan is hiding." Calm authority beats hype.
5. Direct address pain. "If you are an HDB upgrader eyeing Lentor, this saves you a costly mistake."

### Winning formats for SG property
- Talking head with bold captions. Cheapest to film, strong for trust and authority. Works because most viewers watch on mute.
- Walk and walk. Filming near Lentor MRT, the mall, the green spine. High context, feels real.
- Data reveal. One number revealed at a time, captions doing the work. Highly saveable.
- Myth bust. "Everyone says X about Lentor. Here is what the data says." Polarising enough to drive comments.
- "Do not ballot until you watch this." Strong retention device when the payoff is genuinely useful, not clickbait.

### On screen text styles that earn saves and shares
- Bold high contrast single line captions, one to three seconds each, reinforcing the voiceover rather than repeating it word for word.
- Text hook visible in the first one to two seconds so a muted scroll still lands.
- Tight detail cuts (floor plan corner, a sight line, a number on screen) rather than slow sweeping shots.
- Saveable beats watchable: give a framework, a checklist, or a number people want to come back to. Saves and shares signal value to the algorithm more than a like.

### How top SG agents frame new launches
- Lead with the buyer problem (quantum, TDSR headroom, which unit type), not the brochure.
- Anchor to the precinct story (Lentor Hills transformation, TEL connectivity) rather than hype words.
- Use absolute quantum, not just PSF, because families think in total dollars.
- Multi platform: film once, post to Reels, TikTok and Shorts the same hour.

### Content gaps Winfred can own
- The calm investor lens. Most new launch content is hype. Winfred can be the one who shows the math and says when NOT to ballot.
- The four lens framework: Capital, Cashflow, Progression, Protection applied to a single unit.
- "Which unit to ballot and which to skip" honesty. Rare and highly shareable.
- HDB upgrader translation: what your flat sale proceeds plus CPF actually buy you here.
