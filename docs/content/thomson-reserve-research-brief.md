## 1. The facts

- **Project:** Thomson Reserve (former Thomson View Condominium en bloc site)
- **Developer / consortium (CONFIRMED):** UOL Group, Singapore Land Group (SingLand) and
  CapitaLand Development, a joint venture.
  Source: https://thomson-reserves.sg/ and https://stackedhomes.com/new-launch-condos-2026-singapore-complete-guide/
- **Address (CONFIRMED):** Bright Hill Drive, Upper Thomson.
- **District (VERIFY, sources conflict):** The project hub and 99.co list District 20; one
  guide listed District 11. Thomson View sits in the Upper Thomson / Bright Hill area which
  is District 20. Treat as D20 and confirm before filming.
  Sources: https://thomson-reserves.sg/ ; https://stackedhomes.com/new-launch-condos-2026-singapore-complete-guide/
- **Tenure (CONFIRMED):** 99 year leasehold.
  Source: https://thomson-reserves.sg/
- **Site area (VERIFY):** Approx 540,000 sq ft (about 50,170 sqm) per the project hub.
  Confirm against the official factsheet.
  Source: https://thomson-reserves.sg/
- **En bloc price (CONFIRMED):** 810 million dollars collective sale.
  Source: https://stackedhomes.com/new-launch-condos-2026-singapore-complete-guide/
- **Land rate (CONFIRMED):** about 1,178 dollars psf ppr.
  Source: https://stackedhomes.com/new-launch-condos-2026-singapore-complete-guide/ ; https://thomson-reserves.sg/
- **Total units (VERIFY):** Around 1,240 to 1,268 (sources give both). Confirm the final
  count from the developer.
  Sources: https://thomson-reserves.sg/ (about 1,240) ; https://www.99.co/singapore/insider/new-condo-launches-2026/ (1,268)
- **Unit mix and sizes (VERIFY, unreleased):** Pricing guides suggest a spread from one
  bedroom plus study through four bedroom. No official mix or floor sizes released. Do not
  quote any size on camera yet.
- **Launch PSF (VERIFY, unreleased, estimate only):** Analyst guides estimate roughly 2,300
  to 2,600 dollars psf, with reservoir facing high floors at the top of that band. This is
  NOT official pricing. The developer sets and announces price only on booking day.
  Source: https://thomson-reserves.sg/
- **Quantum by bedroom (VERIFY):** Not released. Do not state a quantum until the price list
  is out.
- **Expected TOP (VERIFY):** Around 2031 to 2032 per guides. Confirm.
  Sources: https://thomson-reserves.sg/ (2032) ; https://www.99.co/singapore/insider/new-condo-launches-2026/ (2031)
- **Preview / launch / balloting (VERIFY):** Guides point to a VVIP preview around Q3 2026
  and a public launch around Q4 2026, with one list citing a June 2026 launch. Dates are not
  yet officially fixed. Confirm the exact preview, e application and balloting window before
  any reel goes out.
  Sources: https://thomson-reserves.sg/ ; https://www.99.co/singapore/insider/new-condo-launches-2026/
- **Key dated milestones (CONFIRMED):** High Court approval of the collective sale 1 July
  2025; site acquisition completed 2 October 2025.
  Source: https://thomson-reserves.sg/

## 2. Location and estate story

- **Nearest MRT (CONFIRMED):** Upper Thomson station (TE8) on the Thomson East Coast Line,
  about a 5 to 8 minute walk per the project hub (VERIFY the exact walk time).
  Source: https://thomson-reserves.sg/
- **Future connectivity (VERIFY):** A Cross Island Line interchange at Bright Hill is slated
  to open around 2030, which would give the estate a second line. Confirm the opening year
  from LTA before stating it.
  Source: https://thomson-reserves.sg/
- **Nature (CONFIRMED):** The site abuts the Central Catchment Nature Reserve and is close to
  MacRitchie Reservoir Park and Lower Peirce Reservoir. This is a genuine, rare, low supply
  green frontage.
  Sources: https://thomson-reserves.sg/ ; https://stackedhomes.com/new-launch-condos-2026-singapore-complete-guide/
- **Schools (VERIFY catchment):** Ai Tong School is cited within the 1km band; other primary
  schools sit in the 1km to 2km bands. Confirm each catchment on the official school finder
  before naming any school on camera.
  Source: https://thomson-reserves.sg/
- **Amenities (CONFIRMED):** Thomson Plaza and the established Upper Thomson food belt are
  nearby.
  Source: https://stackedhomes.com/new-launch-condos-2026-singapore-complete-guide/
- **Masterplan / maturing story (CONFIRMED, supply angle):** HDB is launching about 1,200 new
  BTO flats in Upper Thomson from June 2026, described as the first BTO there in over 40
  years. This signals fresh public investment and household formation in the same precinct.
  Source: https://www.propertyguru.com.sg/property-guides/upcoming-new-launch-condo-2026-97570

## 3. Comparable launches and absorption

- **AMO Residence, Ang Mo Kio Rise (CONFIRMED):** 372 units, a UOL plus SingLand plus Kheng
  Leong project, 99 year leasehold. Sold 98 percent on launch day, 23 July 2022, with only 7
  units left, from about 1,890 dollars psf. Same northern precinct, same lead developer
  group. This is the strongest true comparable for developer track record.
  Sources: https://mustsharenews.com/amk-condo-sell-out/ ; https://www.amo-residences-official.com.sg/
- **Lentor Central Residences (CONFIRMED):** Achieved about 93.3 percent take up at launch at
  an average around 2,200 dollars psf.
  Sources: https://www.99.co/singapore/insider/lentor-central-residences-take-up-rate/ ; https://www.edgeprop.sg/property-news/hong-leong-led-consortiums-lentor-central-residences-achieves-93-sales-average-price-2200-psf-launch
- **Lentor Modern (CONFIRMED):** Roughly 605 units, integrated with Lentor MRT, transacting
  around 2,379 dollars psf over the past year.
  Source: https://www.99.co/singapore/condos-apartments/lentor-modern
- **Read across:** Quality 99 year leasehold launches on the TEL line in the north have
  cleared strongly at the 2,100 to 2,400 dollars psf band over the last few years. That frames
  where the market sits, not where Thomson Reserve will be priced. Pricing is set by the
  developer on booking day.

## 4. The hard, verifiable backbone (the non hypey spine)

The land rate is the spine. The consortium paid about **1,178 dollars psf ppr** (CONFIRMED)
for the Thomson View site. Construction, financing and margin sit on top of that, which is why
analyst guides estimate launch pricing well above the land cost. Buyers who understand this
read launch pricing as cost driven, not hype driven. Pair that with two genuine, hard to
replicate facts: a **direct Central Catchment nature frontage** beside MacRitchie (CONFIRMED),
and a **TEL station within walking distance today plus a planned Cross Island Line interchange
nearby** (CONFIRMED line, VERIFY year). Land facing a protected reserve cannot be manufactured
and is rarely released, which is the credible scarcity, not a sales line.

## 5. Ranked target buyer segments

1. **HDB upgraders in the north (Ang Mo Kio, Bishan, Thomson, Sembawang), primary.**
   - Budget band: VERIFY against released quantum; likely entry one and two bedroom buyers.
   - Rational trigger: first sizeable private launch beside the reserve on the TEL line near
     where they already live and work.
   - Emotional trigger: staying rooted in a familiar, green, well connected estate while
     stepping up.
2. **Right sizing owner occupiers and empty nesters from landed or older condos nearby.**
   - Rational: new 99 year leasehold, low rise green setting, established food and retail belt.
   - Emotional: calm, nature fronting lifestyle without leaving Thomson.
3. **Long horizon investors on the TEL growth corridor.**
   - Rational: dual line connectivity story and comparable launch absorption in the corridor.
   - Emotional: conviction in a maturing, supply constrained nature fronting pocket.
4. **Families chasing primary school catchment (VERIFY each catchment).**

### Top 5 objections and an honest counter to each

1. "99 year leasehold, not freehold." True. The counter is connectivity, the reserve frontage,
   and developer track record. Do not claim leasehold will outperform freehold.
2. "Launch pricing looks high." Pricing is unreleased. Frame it against the confirmed land rate
   and recent comparable launches, and tell buyers to wait for the official price list.
3. "Will there be oversupply on the TEL line." Fair concern given Lentor volume. The honest
   counter is that this specific pocket fronts a protected reserve, which caps nearby new
   supply, unlike the denser Lentor cluster. Cite supply facts, not opinion.
4. "TOP is years away." True, around 2031 to 2032 (VERIFY). Counter with progressive payment
   and the buyer's own time horizon, never with a promise that prices will rise.
5. "Developer track record." UOL plus SingLand led AMO Residence which cleared 98 percent on
   launch day (CONFIRMED). State the record, make no future guarantee.

## 6. The single most compelling TRUE angle

**A nature fronting, dual line connected 99 year leasehold launch from a consortium whose last
northern project sold 98 percent on launch day, on land bought at about 1,178 dollars psf ppr,
beside a reserve where new supply cannot simply be added.** Every clause is on the public
record. The urgency is real ballot and e application dynamics plus genuinely scarce
reserve facing land, not invented scarcity.
