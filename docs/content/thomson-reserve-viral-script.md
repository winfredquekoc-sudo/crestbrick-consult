## Phase 2: Reel and social intelligence

What actually performs for Singapore new launch property content, from publicly accessible
sources and established short form practice (no fabricated metrics).

- **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds with on screen text.** Most viewers watch sound off, so the
  algorithm rewards an opening caption a viewer can read instantly, for example a number or a
  contrarian claim.
  Source: https://drealm.sg/ai-video-real-estate-singapore-tiktok-7-steps/
- **Winning formats** for this niche: the data reveal (a single hard number on screen), the
  calm myth bust ("before you queue for this launch, watch this"), the walk and talk past the
  actual site or MRT, and the right sizing decision framework. Talking head works when the
  first line is a real stake, not a greeting.
- **What earns saves and shares:** a decision framework the viewer can reuse (how to read
  launch pricing against land cost), a genuine map or supply insight, and an honest "who this
  is for and who it is not for". Saves come from usefulness, shares from a clear point of view.
- **On screen text style:** large, high contrast captions, one idea per beat, the key number
  held on screen, captions on throughout.
- **The content gap Winfred can own:** most launch reels are pure hype and price teasers. The
  open lane is the **calm investor read**: land cost as the spine, real comparable absorption,
  and an honest objection counter. Few creators pair "make the right buyer act" with "tell the
  truth and flag what is unverified". That is Winfred's voice and his edge.

Sources: https://drealm.sg/ai-video-real-estate-singapore-tiktok-7-steps/ ;
https://stackedhomes.com/first-new-launch-condo-booking-day-ballot/

**Three line strategy**
- Who the reel targets: northern HDB upgraders and right sizers who already live, work or
  school around Thomson, Bishan and Ang Mo Kio.
- The ONE irresistible true promise: you will understand whether this launch is worth
  balloting for in under a minute, with the real numbers, not the hype.
- The emotion to pull: quiet conviction. The calm of deciding from facts while others queue on
  feeling.

## Shared filming and posting plan

- Length: 30 to 45 seconds each.
- Format: vertical 9 by 16, captions on the whole time, key numbers held large on screen.
- Open with the hook line and on screen text inside the first 1.5 seconds, no greeting.
- Always on screen and in the sign off: Winfred Quek, CEA R073319H.
- Posting time: aim for weekday evenings around 7 to 9pm Singapore time and weekend late
  mornings, when upgraders scroll. Test and adjust.
- Use original spoken audio for credibility; add a soft licensed track underneath if desired.
- Never state launch PSF, quantum, unit sizes, exact dates or the CRL year as fact until the
  brief is confirmed.
