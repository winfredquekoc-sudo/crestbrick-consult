## SCHEDULING EFFICIENCY TIPS

### High Availability (LL_003, LL_014)
- **Batch multiple tenants:** Schedule 3-4 tenants in one day if possible
- **Move fast:** Lock in viewings within 24 hours of inquiry

### Medium Availability (LL_008, LL_009, LL_010, LL_012, LL_013, LL_015, LL_017, LL_018, LL_019)
- **Provide options:** Always offer 3-5 time slots
- **Confirm in advance:** Give 2-3 days notice for weekday viewings, 1 week for recurring slots

### Low Availability (LL_007, LL_011, LL_018, LL_021)
- **Plan ahead:** 1+ week lead time
- **Be grateful:** These landlords are selective - show appreciation for their time
- **Respect constraints:** Don't push outside their stated windows

### Unknown Availability (LL_004, LL_005, LL_006, LL_016, LL_020)
- **Ask specific questions:** "Mornings or evenings?" not "When are you free?"
- **Follow up:** If no response in 24 hours, try again or call
- **Get commitment:** "Great, so Wednesday 7pm works - I'll bring tenant then."
