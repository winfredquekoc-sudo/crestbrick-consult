**Action:** Skip portfolio investors entirely (5+ listings = demanding, low margin).

### Red-Hot Signals (Tier 1 — Outreach within 48 hours)

1. **Listing age 45–90 days** → Pain point activated
2. **Price reduction 5%+** → Explicit urgency signal
3. **Poor photos + active in comments** → Needs help, growing desperate
4. **Reposted 2+ times in 90 days** → Frustrated
5. **Posted from overseas or visa dates in description** → Hard deadline

### Warm Signals (Tier 2 — Outreach within 1 week)

- Listing age 30-59 days
- One price reduction (3-5%)
- Moderate effort in photos (5-8 decent photos)
- Active in comments (responds within 24h)

### Scoring Model (Automate This)

```
Tier 1 candidate if:
  - Listing age >= 45 AND <= 90 days
  - AND (price_reduction > 3% OR photo_count < 6 OR description_length < 200)
  - AND (unanswered_comments >= 2 OR response_time > 24 hours)
```

## 4. PROCESS & TIMING WORKFLOW

### Optimal Cadence: Every 3-5 Days

```
Day 1:   Warm intro (within 2 hours of listing publication)
Day 4:   Reality check (acknowledge the noise they're seeing)
Day 7:   Objection handling (address "why pay commission?")
Day 11:  Social proof (30+ sales, negotiation value)
Day 16:  Soft close (graceful exit if no engagement)
```

### Best Sending Window

- **Time:** 8:30am SGT (morning check-in, higher open rates)
- **Day:** Weekday > weekend (FSBO sellers less engaged weekends)
- **First touch:** Within 2 hours of listing (algorithmic freshness matters on Carousell)

### Fresh Listers (0-7 Days) vs Aged (30+ Days)

| Age | Tone | Cadence | CTA |
|-----|------|---------|-----|
| **0-7 days** | Educational | Daily presence | None (capture intent) |
| **7-14 days** | Problem-solver | Every 3 days | Soft Calendly CTA |
| **15-30 days** | Value-focused | Weekly | Direct but graceful |
| **30+ days** | Archive | Stop | Restart only on re-list |

### Persistence Window: 30 Days (Not 45)

**Why:** FSBO decision window is much shorter than tenant search
- Days 10-25 are critical (when pain emerges)
- Day 25+: Market has already told them if price/positioning is wrong
- **Reactivation rule:** Only restart on re-list or direct seller inbound

### Soft Yes vs No Interest Detection

| Signal | Interpretation | Action |
|--------|---|---|
| Reply same day | HIGH interest | Send reply immediately; collapse cadence |
| Read + no reply within 2hrs | Interested but busy | Continue normal cadence |
| Unread for 7 days | Low interest | Pause; wait for outbound signal |
| "Offer received" mention | Soft yes | Offer to review terms within 1hr |
| Listing price drops 10%+ | Panic signal | Perfect moment for Touch 3 value prop |
| Block / No status view | Negative | Remove from list |

**No-spam guardrails:**
- Never multi-message same day
- Always include CEA# (R073319H)
- Avoid time-pressure tactics
- Use warm, advisor voice
