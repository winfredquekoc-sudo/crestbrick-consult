District guides

Pick the right district before you pick the unit.

All 28 Singapore districts region tag, key areas, and my view on where each one sits today. Type your postal code below to find your district instantly.

Quick answer: Singapore has 28 postal districts, District 1 through District 28, set by URA. Each district groups postal code sectors (the first two digits of a 6 digit postal code) into one area, and every property listing, URA caveat and market report references it. District 1 to District 11 sit largely in the Core Central Region (CCR), District 3, 4, 8, 12 to 15 and 21 form the Rest of Central Region (RCR) or city fringe, and the remaining districts are the Outside Central Region (OCR), the suburban belt. D9 · CCR

Orchard · River Valley

+

Median PSF

~S$2,950 psf

Supply outlook

Constrained. Very limited GLS. Most activity is en bloc driven.

Ideal buyer

Investors chasing capital preservation; HNW foreign buyers prioritizing prestige and rental depth.

Winfred's POV

D9 isn't a cashflow story it's a capital preservation story. If yield is the goal, the math usually pulls me outward. If legacy, discretion, and district prestige matter, this is where I spend time.

D10 · CCR

Bukit Timah · Holland

+

Median PSF

~S$2,550 psf

Supply outlook

Selective. Freehold stock dominates; new supply limited to targeted redevelopments.

Ideal buyer

Families prioritizing schools and greenery; long hold investors who value freehold tenure.

Winfred's POV

D10 ages well. Not the fastest moving district; usually the one my clients still thank me for a decade later. School catchment and freehold are the compounding inputs.

D11 · CCR

Novena · Newton

+

Median PSF

~S$2,400 psf

Supply outlook

Health City Novena pipeline supports long run demand; moderate new launch supply.

Ideal buyer

Medical professionals, expat families, investors who want rental depth near CBD.

Winfred's POV

Novena is the CCR district I most often recommend for investor use rentals. The demand pool is broad, central, and sticky. The risk is stack selection some are better than others by margins that matter.

D15 · RCR

East Coast · Katong

+

Median PSF

~S$2,100 psf

Supply outlook

Thomson East Coast Line stations unlocking value. Moderate new supply.

Ideal buyer

Families, lifestyle led professionals, upgraders prioritizing food, sea, and connectivity.

Winfred's POV

The east is where "live in" usually wins the argument over "pure invest." Strong owner occupier bias keeps liquidity healthy. The TEL impact is still working through don't just buy near a station, buy ahead of where the district is densifying.

D19 · OCR

Sengkang · Punggol

+

Median PSF

~S$1,750 psf (private)

Supply outlook

Punggol Digital District maturing; EC/HDB pipeline deep; private launches selectively placed.

Ideal buyer

HDB upgraders, dual income young families, investors with a multi cycle horizon.

Winfred's POV

The biggest single upgrader catchment on the island. Most of my HDB to condo sequencing work happens here. The projects that age best are the ones close to the HDB to private migration path not the ones deepest into the precinct.

D22 · OCR

Jurong Lake District

+

Median PSF

~S$1,850 psf

Supply outlook

Major GLS pipeline. Second CBD ambitions continue to drive master plan anchor investments.

Ideal buyer

Long horizon investors comfortable underwriting a 10 to 15 year transformation story.

Winfred's POV

JLD is a bet on the plan executing. The plan is real, the capex commitments are real, but the timeline is long. I like it for patient capital not for clients who need the asset to perform in the next 24 months.
