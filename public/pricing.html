Why 2%?

Because you're not just paying for someone to list your property. You're paying for a complete selling strategy. My 2% covers everything from the backend paperwork to the front-end presentation: photography, targeted marketing, 360° virtual viewing, staging support, financial calculations, and portfolio planning for where the sale proceeds go next.

These aren't upsells. They're services that create real value, save you time, and position your property properly, not hopefully.

The fee is aligned with your outcome, not with the transaction -- which is why we start with the property portfolio analysis, not the listing.

Buyer representation

No fee from you.

S$0

Standard co-broke arrangement

Under the standard Singapore co-broke model, buyer representation is paid by the seller's side through the co-broke commission. You get full advocacy: sourcing, negotiation, due diligence, and closing, without paying a direct fee.

If a property has no co-broke (rare), I'll disclose that upfront and we agree on a buyer-side fee before any viewing. No surprises.

CEA requires a signed Estate Agency Agreement before representation begins. You'll see the full disclosure, including any commission source, in writing.
