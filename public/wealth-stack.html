Property in Your Wealth Stack

Most agents stop at the property. I think about property as one layer in your household's wealth stack , sitting alongside cash, equities, trusts, insurance, and estate planning. Where it fits matters more than which unit you buy.

The 8 layers I'll discuss with you

01
Liquidity reserve6-12 months expenses, accessible. Property is illiquid , this is the buffer.
02
Insurance backboneTerm life, ILP review, MRT/HPS, PI for self-employed. Most clients are over-insured in some categories, under-insured in others.
03
Property coreYour home + investment property. Where I focus most directly.
04
CPF strategyOA → property vs SA → retirement. Long-term implications most owners never fully model.
05
Public-market portfolioEquities, REITs, bonds. Not my domain directly , partner introductions to fee-only advisors.
06
Trust structures (HNW)Family trusts, holding companies, asset protection. Refer-out to specialist legal counsel.
07
Estate planWill, lasting power of attorney, beneficiary nominations. Every property-owning client should have these current.
08
Cross-border / multi-currency (selected)For families with overseas exposure , UK, Iskandar, US, AU. Cross-border desk + FX hedging partners.

What an integrated review looks like

Once a year, we walk through all 8 layers together. Not to sell anything from each , but to surface gaps. Common gaps I see:

  • Net worth is 75%+ in a single SG residential property , concentration risk most don't see
  • No will, despite owning $2M+ property
  • CPF used aggressively for property without modeling retirement-cliff implications
  • Insurance coverage hasn't been reviewed since the property was bought , premiums often 30%+ overpriced
  • Foreign income or property held in personal name when corporate structure would save substantial tax
