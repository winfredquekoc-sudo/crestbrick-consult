The Investor Cohort

An 8-week paid course on building a Singapore property portfolio. Cohort-based , 12 to 15 students per round, live sessions, real client case studies, peer accountability. Limited seats.

The 8-week curriculum

  1. Week 1 , The 4-Pillar framework. Income, ABSD, financing, time horizon. Diagnose your starting position.
  2. Week 2 , Singapore market mechanics. URA cycles, cooling measures, supply pipeline, district hierarchy.
  3. Week 3 , Financing. TDSR, MSR, LTV, refinance windows, SORA. Building the loan stack.
  4. Week 4 , CPF strategy. When to use OA, accrued interest, retirement-age implications.
  5. Week 5 , Restructuring & ABSD. When transferring ownership saves more than the cost.
  6. Week 6 , New launches vs resale. The progressive payment math, deferred payment, why "discount" misleads.
  7. Week 7 , Yield investing. Rental yield by district, dual-key, co-living, leasehold tail.
  8. Week 8 , Portfolio Q&A. Each student presents their plan; cohort + Winfred review.

Who it's for

  • You've owned 1-2 properties and want to scale to 3-5
  • You want frameworks, not just "should I buy this unit"
  • You learn better in a peer group than 1-on-1
