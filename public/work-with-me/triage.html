What's included

15 minutes, structured

Minutes 0-3: You tell me where you are. One sentence on your current holding, one on the question you're sitting with.

Minutes 3-12: I ask the four or five questions that usually surface the real issue. We don't model anything in detail. We just identify what kind of decision this is.

Minutes 12-15: I tell you, plainly, whether a full Property Portfolio Analysis is worth $499 to you, or whether a free tool, a different advisor, or simply waiting is the better call.

No prep. No documents. No follow-up sequence. If we're a fit, you'll know. If not, you've spent 15 minutes and got an honest answer.
