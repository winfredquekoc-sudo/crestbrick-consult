How to start

Application, not booking

Step 1. Email a one paragraph note: who's in the household, what the portfolio looks like at a high level, and what you'd want this engagement to do for you in year one.

Step 2. If we're a fit on paper, a 60 minute discovery meeting at a venue of your choice. No fee.

Step 3. If we proceed, the engagement begins on a calendar year basis. Annual fee paid quarterly in advance. Terminable at year end with 60 days' notice.

Slots are not held. If five households are engaged, applications are queued for the next intake.
