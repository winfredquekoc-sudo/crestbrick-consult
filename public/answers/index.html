Direct Answers

Direct answers to the questions you actually ask

Not keywords, real questions. Each page below answers one specific question in full, with the numbers traced back to IRAS, HDB, MAS, or CPF Board rules as reported on this site.

By Winfred Quek · CEA R073319H · Updated 5 Aug 2026

PR, citizenship and foreigner rules

Answer
Can a PR buy an HDB flat in Singapore?

Yes, a resale flat only, after a 3 year wait from getting PR status. No BTO, ever.

Answer
Can singles buy an HDB flat in Singapore?

Yes, from age 35, under the Single Singapore Citizen Scheme.

Answer
What happened to the 15 month wait out rule?

Removed with immediate effect on 28 Jul 2026. Here is exactly what changed and what did not.

Answer
Can foreigners buy property in Singapore?

Private condos yes, at 60% ABSD. HDB flats and most landed property, no.

Answer
How much stamp duty does a PR pay on a first property?

5% ABSD plus tiered BSD, roughly $119,600 in total on a $1.5M purchase.

HDB and private property together

Answer
Can I buy a condo while owning an HDB?

Yes for citizens, once your flat passes its 5 year MOP. PRs cannot do this at all.

Answer
What is restructuring and does it still work?

Decoupling can save up to $400,000 in ABSD on a $2M purchase, but it has its own costs.

Answer
BTO or resale, which should I choose?

BTO is 20 to 35% cheaper but takes 4 to 5 years. Resale is immediate. It comes down to timing.

Answer
Who is eligible to buy an Executive Condo?

At least one SC applicant, household income under $16,000, no private property owned.

Answer
Can I rent out my HDB flat or a room in it?

Yes after MOP, but room and whole flat subletting follow different rules.

Stamp duty: buying and selling

Answer
How much is stamp duty on a second property?

20% ABSD plus tiered BSD. On $1.5M, that is roughly $344,600 in total.

Answer
Do I pay seller stamp duty if I sell after 3 years?

Depends entirely on your purchase date. The schedule changed on 4 Jul 2025.

Affordability and loans

Answer
How much do I need to earn for a $1.5M condo?

Roughly $9,750 a month gross, assuming no other debt, at MAS's 4.0% stress test rate.

Answer
How much cash do I need upfront for a resale condo?

About $425,000 on a $1.5M first property purchase, downpayment, BSD, legal fees and all.

Answer
Is it better to rent or buy in Singapore right now?

Mostly a question of how long you plan to stay, not what the market is doing this quarter.

Answer
What is TDSR and how much can I borrow?

55% of gross income, stress tested at 4% p.a. Usually 10 to 20% less than online calculators suggest.

Answer
How much downpayment do I need for an HDB flat?

20% with an HDB loan, no mandatory cash. 25% with a bank loan, 5% must be cash.

Answer
What is a bridging loan and when do I need one?

Covers the cash gap when your new home completes before your old one sells. 3.5 to 5% p.a.

Selling and costs

Answer
How long does it take to sell an HDB flat?

12 to 16 weeks from OTP to completion, once you've passed your 5 year MOP.

Answer
What are the costs of selling a condo?

Outstanding loan, CPF refund, agent commission, legal fees, and SSD if you're still inside the window.

Answer
Do I have to refund CPF with accrued interest when I sell?

Yes, principal plus 2.5% compounding, before any cash reaches you.

Fees, yields and market timing

Answer
How much commission does a property agent charge?

Around 2% plus GST for sellers, billed on completion. Buyers usually pay nothing directly.

Answer
Which areas in Singapore have the best rental yield?

Jurong East leads HDB towns at 7.38% gross yield, Bukit Timah is lowest at 4.24%.

Answer
What happens when a 99 year lease runs out?

It returns to the state, no renewal. The real problems start decades earlier.

Answer
Is 2026 a good time to buy property in Singapore?

No universal answer, it comes down to your own Money, Timing and Safety.
