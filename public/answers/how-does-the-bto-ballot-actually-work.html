How does the BTO ballot actually work?

By Winfred Quek · CEA R073319H · Published 5 Aug 2026

Quick answer: Each BTO launch opens for one to two weeks, you apply for one project and flat type for a $10 non refundable fee, then HDB runs a computer ballot. First timers get priority queue positions, typically over 95% of larger flats reserved for them, with results released two to three weeks after applications close.

The ballot itself is simple in mechanics but the priority structure underneath it decides your real odds, and most first time applicants have never seen how the queue is actually built.

Quotable: As of August 2026, first timer applicants receive priority balloting, with typically 95% of 4 room and larger BTO units reserved for them (HDB).

How to apply

Each BTO launch opens for applications for roughly one to two weeks via the HDB Flat Portal. You select one project and one flat type per application, and the $10 application fee is non refundable regardless of outcome. You need a valid HFE letter before you can apply.

How the priority queue actually works

After the application window closes, HDB runs a computer ballot, but your queue position is randomised within priority tiers, not across the whole applicant pool. First timers and Fiance/Fiancee Scheme applicants sit in a dedicated priority queue, and most flat types reserve a significant proportion of units, typically 95% of 4 room and larger flats, for first timers. Second timers, who have already received a subsidised flat before, get half the ballot chances of a first timer.

What happens after you get a queue number

Ballot results are released roughly two to three weeks after applications close. A queue number within the available unit count invites you to a flat selection appointment; a number outside it may still be called if others decline, or it registers toward future priority boosting. Missing your selection appointment without a valid reason typically forfeits the booking.

Not all launches are equal

Experienced applicants weigh location classification (Standard, Plus or Prime, each with different subsidy and resale restriction trade offs), published oversubscription rates from the previous exercise, MRT proximity, and flat mix, larger launches with more 4 room units generally give first timers better statistical odds.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for BTO if I am not yet married?

Yes, under the Fiance/Fiancee Scheme, provided you solemnise the marriage before the flat is booked, typically within 3 months of application. If the marriage does not proceed, the application is cancelled.

What happens if I miss my flat selection appointment?

Missing your appointment without a valid reason typically forfeits the booking, and your queue number lapses. You would need to reapply in a future launch. Contact HDB immediately if you cannot attend, they may reschedule under extenuating circumstances.

Is there an appeal if I am unsuccessful in the ballot?

There is no formal appeal process, the ballot is computerised and random within its priority tiers. You can register interest in remaining units after the initial selection round, or apply in the next launch with any accumulated ballot chances.
