BTO or resale, which should I choose?

By Winfred Quek · CEA R073319H · Published 5 Aug 2026 · Last reviewed August 2026

Quick answer: BTO is typically 20 to 35% cheaper than comparable resale flats and carries the largest HDB grants, but takes 4 to 5 years from application to key collection. Resale gives immediate possession and lets you pick the exact block, floor, and location. The right choice comes down to whether you can wait.

This is rarely a numbers only question. Both paths are financially sound; the deciding factor is almost always your timeline.

The price gap

BTO flats typically transact 20 to 35% below comparable resale flats in the same area, and carry the largest available HDB grants, up to S$120,000 in Enhanced Housing Grant for eligible first timer households at or below S$9,000 monthly income.

Quotable: As of August 2026, eligible first timer households earning up to S$9,000 a month can receive up to S$120,000 in Enhanced Housing Grant on a BTO purchase (HDB).

The wait

A standard BTO launch runs 4 to 5 years from application to key collection. A resale flat gives immediate possession, and lets you choose the exact block, floor, and proximity to schools or parents, something no BTO ballot can guarantee.

Income ceilings differ by flat type

BTO eligibility is capped by household income: S$14,000 a month for 4 room and 5 room flats, S$7,000 a month for 2 room and 3 room flats. These ceilings do not apply to resale flats bought without a grant.

Which one fits you

Run it through Money, Timing and Safety rather than price alone. Money favours BTO, cheaper and better subsidised. Timing depends entirely on your circumstances, whether you can comfortably wait 4 to 5 years, or need a home now for a growing family or an approaching lease. Safety is about certainty, resale price and unit are known today, a BTO ballot outcome and construction timeline are not.

Frequently asked questions

Is BTO always cheaper than resale?

Typically, yes, by roughly 20 to 35% versus a comparable resale flat, but the exact gap depends on the specific project, location, and how mature the resale estate is.

What if I can't wait 4 to 5 years for a BTO?

Then resale is usually the practical choice. It gives immediate possession and lets you choose your exact block, floor, and neighbourhood; the trade off is a higher price and a smaller grant.
