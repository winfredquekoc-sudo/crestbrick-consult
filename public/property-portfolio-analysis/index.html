The Move

What gets mapped in your property portfolio analysis.

01

Capital

ABSD exposure, financing ceiling, liquidity runway. Where your deployable dollars actually go, and how much is truly available after stamp duties, CPF accruals, and existing debt are netted out.

02

Cashflow

Rental yield realism, mortgage servicing, vacancy buffer. What lands in your account every month after the property runs, not the gross number on a brochure, the net number on a spreadsheet.

03

Progression

Sequencing, sell-first vs buy-first, restructuring math, MOP timing. The 10-year map, not the next purchase. Every decision you make now changes the menu of options you have in year 5.

04

Protection

Insurance, trust structures, spousal rights, exit plan. The downside math most agents skip. What happens to the portfolio if income drops, rates spike, or a forced sale is required on an illiquid timeline.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this free?

Yes. No fee, no obligation. The property portfolio analysis is the first step of my investor-grade consultation. I only earn if and when I represent you on a transaction, under the standard Singapore co-broke model.

How long does it take?

About 4 minutes online, then a 30-minute strategy session (virtual or in-person) once I've reviewed your property portfolio analysis. The form is conversational, you will not be staring at a spreadsheet.

What do I need to bring?

Nothing for the online form. For the call, it helps to have a rough sense of your monthly income, existing debt, and any current property holdings. If you don't know the exact numbers, ballpark is fine, that's what the call is for.

Who is this for?

First-time buyers, HDB upgraders, restructuring couples, foreigners and PRs, and existing investors with 1 to 5+ properties. If you want a strategy and not just a listing, this is the right starting point.

What happens to my data?
