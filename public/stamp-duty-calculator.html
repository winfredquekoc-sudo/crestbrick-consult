Stamp Duty Calculator

Quick answer: Your total stamp duty in Singapore is BSD plus, where applicable, ABSD. BSD is tiered from 1% to 6% and every buyer pays it. ABSD is layered on top and varies by profile and property count, from 0% for a Citizen first home up to 65% for an entity.

When is stamp duty due? BSD and ABSD are payable within 14 days of signing the Sales and Purchase Agreement (SPA), or within 30 days if the SPA is executed overseas.

SC + PR joint purchase: IRAS applies the SC ABSD rate where at least one buyer is SC and it is a joint purchase of a first property (with ABSD remission available). Always confirm eligibility with a legal advisor.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between BSD and ABSD?

BSD, Buyer's Stamp Duty, is the base duty every buyer pays, charged in tiers from 1% to 6% of the price. ABSD, Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty, is an extra layer that applies based on your residency and how many homes you own, ranging from 0% to 65%. Your total upfront stamp duty is the two added together.

How much total stamp duty will I pay on a S$1.5M home?

BSD on a S$1.5M residential property is about S$44,600. A Singapore Citizen first time buyer adds 0% ABSD, so the total is roughly S$44,600. A Citizen second property adds 20%, or S$300,000, lifting the total to around S$344,600. Enter your profile and count to see your exact combined figure.

When must stamp duty be paid in Singapore?

BSD and ABSD are both payable within 14 days of signing the Sale and Purchase Agreement or Option to Purchase, or within 30 days if the document is executed overseas. ABSD must be settled in cash. Missing the deadline can attract penalties, so budget the full stamp duty before you commit to the purchase.

Does this calculator handle PR and foreigner buyers?

Yes. You can select Singapore Citizen, PR, foreigner or entity, along with your property count, and the tool applies the correct ABSD rate alongside the tiered BSD. For joint purchases with mixed profiles, remember IRAS applies the highest rate to the whole price, which the rate reference table on the page lays out.

Is stamp duty payable on the price or the valuation?

Both BSD and ABSD are computed on the higher of the purchase price or the market value of the property. If you negotiate a price below valuation, stamp duty is still assessed on the valuation. This matters most on below market or related party transfers, where the duty can exceed what the price alone suggests.
