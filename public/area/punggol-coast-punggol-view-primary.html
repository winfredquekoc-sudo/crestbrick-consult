Living near Punggol Coast with kids at Punggol View Primary.

Punggol View Primary is approximately 1.4 km from Punggol Coast MRT — around 17 min. That places it in the 1–2 km band — Phase 2C priority but not Phase 2B.

Distance band — what it means for primary 1 registration

Inside the 1–2 km band.

Singapore's primary 1 registration uses three distance bands: within 1 km (priority in Phase 2A and 2B), 1–2 km (priority in Phase 2C), and beyond 2 km (Phase 2C only if seats remain after the 1–2 km band).

An address near Punggol Coast MRT typically falls into the 1–2 km band for Punggol View Primary. Phase 2C applicants in the 1–2 km band ballot ahead of Phase 2C applicants beyond 2 km. If the school is oversubscribed at Phase 2C, the 1 km band gets a meaningful edge — but you'll need to register early in 2C.

Distances above are approximate, measured station entrance to school gate. MOE measures by registered home address straight-line distance — confirm with the school and MOE's catchment finder before committing to a unit.

FAQ

Common questions parents ask.

Is an address near Punggol Coast MRT close enough for Phase 2C priority at Punggol View Primary?

Partly — at this proximity, you sit inside the 1–2 km band, which is Phase 2C priority. Phase 2B and earlier (alumni, sibling, volunteer) still rank ahead. Distance alone does not lock a spot in oversubscribed years.

Should I buy HDB resale or wait for a BTO near Punggol Coast?

For Phase 2B priority you generally need to be in the catchment for at least 30 months before P1 registration. If your child is > 4 years old, BTO timelines often miss that window — resale (or a private condo if budget allows) is usually faster. Run the affordability and ABSD math first using the affordability calculator.

What about ABSD if we already own a home?

A second residential property triggers ABSD: 20% for SC second, 30% for PR second, 60% for foreigners. Check exposure with the ABSD calculator — and consider whether ownership restructuring is on the table before you buy.

Does living within 1 km guarantee a P1 spot at Punggol View Primary?

No — MOE allocates by ballot when phases are oversubscribed. The 1 km band raises priority, but balloting still applies in popular schools. Distance is necessary, not sufficient.
