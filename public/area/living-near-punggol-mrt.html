Living near Punggol MRT.

Punggol is Singapore's young-family magnet, Waterway Point, the LRT loop, and a fully built-out BTO ecosystem centred on the Punggol Waterway. This station is served by NEL and Punggol LRT, an interchange, in District 19 (Serangoon, Hougang, Punggol).

Getting around

What the commute actually looks like.

Punggol MRT is served by NEL and Punggol LRT as an interchange station, so you can change lines without leaving the paid gates.

NEL gets you into the city in a comfortable rail ride, with the Punggol LRT loop covering the estate's interior for the last mile to most blocks.

The area in one paragraph

What Punggol feels like to live in.

Punggol is Singapore's young-family magnet, Waterway Point, the LRT loop, and a fully built-out BTO ecosystem centred on the Punggol Waterway. The planned Punggol Digital District and the Cross Island Line extension will keep reshaping demand here through the 2030s.

Housing stock nearby

HDB, condo, and landed within reach of the station.

Housing supply is BTO-led, Northshore, Matilda, and the newer Punggol Coast pipeline (served by its own station, one stop from Punggol proper). Watertown condo and Piermont Grand EC are the mature private anchors along the waterway.
