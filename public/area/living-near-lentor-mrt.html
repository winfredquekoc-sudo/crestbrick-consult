Lentor MRT · TEL Single line station

Living near Lentor MRT.

Lentor is a fast evolving residential pocket where the original landed house enclave is being layered with new condo launches, Lentor Modern, Lentor Hills Residences, Hillock Green, and the Lentor Mansion line all sit within a short walk of the station. This station is served by TEL in District 26 (Upper Thomson, Springleaf).

Getting around

What the commute actually looks like.

Lentor MRT is served by TEL, with no cross platform interchange at this station.

TEL trains put the city centre roughly 25 minutes away, one of the more direct northern commutes now that the full TEL corridor is open, without the interchange transfer older Ang Mo Kio addresses required.

The area in one paragraph

What Lentor feels like to live in.

Lentor is a fast evolving residential pocket where the original landed house enclave is being layered with new condo launches, Lentor Modern, Lentor Hills Residences, Hillock Green, and the Lentor Mansion line all sit within a short walk of the station. The area was largely quiet and car dependent until the Thomson-East Coast Line (TEL) arrived in 2021.

Housing stock nearby

HDB, condo, and landed within reach of the station.

Housing stock here is being actively reshaped. The original landed enclave anchors the upper end of the market; the new wave of condo launches is adding several hundred units within walking distance of the station. HDB stock is thin in the immediate vicinity, most nearby resale flats sit further out towards the older Ang Mo Kio blocks.
