FAQs

What's your commission rate? +

1% to 1.5% of total annual rent, depending on property type and market. Paid only on successful placement. No upfront fees.

How long does it take to find a tenant? +

Average 8-10 days from listing to signed agreement. My active tenant network and screening process minimize viewing time and vacancy risk.

Do I need to do viewings? +

Yes, but I coordinate. I pre-screen tenants and coordinate viewing schedules so you only meet qualified candidates. Most owners do 1-2 final viewings before signing.

What if I sell or need to end the arrangement? +

No exclusive listing required. You can sell or switch anytime. I don't lock you in. Our agreement ends the moment you find another arrangement.

Can I list on other platforms too? +

Yes. I list on PropertyGuru and 99.co, but you can use Carousell, Facebook, or other sites in parallel. First person to sign wins.
