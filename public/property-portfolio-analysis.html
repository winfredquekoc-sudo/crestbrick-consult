What is the PPA?

Most people making a $1M+ property decision are working off incomplete information they know their income and their CPF balance, but they haven't mapped out their ABSD exposure, their accrued interest liability, their TDSR ceiling, or what their property is actually worth net of all costs if they sold today.

The Property Portfolio Analysis is a 30-minute structured conversation where I run through your full picture using the Money, Timing & Safety framework. You walk out knowing exactly where you stand and what your options are with no obligation to do anything.

The Money, Timing & Safety framework

01
Money

Is your current property a good asset to hold, and what's your financial picture if you move? We look at remaining lease, PSF vs comparable projects, rental yield, and appreciation trajectory, alongside CPF accrued interest exposure, net equity after CPF refund, TDSR ceiling for your next purchase, cash war chest, and what you actually walk away with if you sell today.

02
Timing

The clearest sequence of moves from where you are now to where you want to be whether that's upgrading, building a second property, optimising ownership structure, or timing your exit.

03
Safety

ABSD on your next move, cashflow stress under a rate spike or vacancy, concentration risk if property is your only asset, and policy risk from future cooling measures.

What you get

  • ✦A clear picture of your net equity position today after CPF refund, accrued interest, outstanding loan, and estimated selling costs
  • ✦Your ABSD exposure and TDSR ceiling for any next purchase no surprises when you're mid-negotiation
  • ✦At least 2 to 3 specific next move options ranked by financial impact and feasibility
  • ✦A plain English verdict on whether to hold, sell, upgrade, or restructure and the sequencing that makes sense for your timeline
  • ✦No sales pitch. If the right answer is to do nothing for 2 years, I'll tell you that.
