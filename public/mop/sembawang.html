What crossing MOP means for a Sembawang owner

Before MOP completes, an owner cannot sell on the open market, sublet the whole flat, or buy private property while keeping the flat, outside four narrow HDB approved exceptions: financial hardship, medical reasons, a court order following matrimonial breakdown, or compulsory acquisition such as SERS. Once MOP completes, none of those restrictions apply. The transactions above are Sembawang owners who reached that point and chose to sell.

A separate factual change also affects who can buy: in July 2026 HDB removed the 15 month wait out period that had applied to private property owners buying an HDB resale flat. That widens the buyer pool for every resale flat on the market right now, Sembawang included. Read what the 15 month wait out removal changed for the full detail.

The resale process, start to finish

Crossing MOP only removes the restriction, it does not start the clock on a sale. For the actual steps, pricing strategy, the HDB resale portal, and the CPF refund mechanics, see the full step by step HDB resale guide.

Frequently asked questions

How many Sembawang HDB resale flats crossed MOP recently?

79 resale transactions were recorded in Sembawang between 2026 to 02 and 2026 to 07, among flats with a lease commencing in 2020 or 2021, the cohort whose 5 year MOP falls due roughly 2025 to 2027 (source: data.gov.sg, HDB Resale Flat Prices).

What is the median resale price for a post MOP Sembawang flat?

The median resale price across those 79 transactions was S$630,000, with a median of S$7,936 per square metre. Prices ranged from S$322,000 to S$895,000 depending on flat type, floor, and remaining lease.

What flat type sells most after MOP in Sembawang?

4 Room flats accounted for the largest share of the 79 transactions in this cohort. See the table above for the median price and price per square metre by flat type.
