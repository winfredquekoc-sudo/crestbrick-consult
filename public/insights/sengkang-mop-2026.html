Sengkang HDB MOP 2026: Upgrading When Your Entire Block Is Also Selling

By Winfred Quek · CEA R073319H · PropNex Realty

Quick answer: Sengkang's 2026 MOP cohort — spanning Fernvale Vines, Rivervale Shores, Anchorvale Parkview and surrounding BTO clusters — is one of Singapore's largest simultaneous MOP releases with over 8,000 units eligible to sell. Supply pressure is compressing Sengkang resale prices to $550–$620 PSF for 5-room flats. Upgraders who act now face a soft exit but entry into private condos with less competition from fellow upgraders who are still waiting.

Why the Sengkang 2026 MOP Cohort Is Unusually Large

Between 2019 and 2021, HDB launched a massive wave of BTO projects across Sengkang — responding to strong demand from young families priced out of mature estates. The result is that 2024 to 2026 sees a concentrated cluster of completions all hitting MOP within the same 24-month window.

Key projects reaching or recently past MOP in the 2025–2026 window include:

  • Fernvale Vines (Fernvale Road) — ~1,500 units, MOP 2025
  • Rivervale Shores — ~1,800 units, MOP 2025–2026
  • Anchorvale Parkview — ~1,200 units, MOP 2026
  • Compassvale Mast / Bow / Arc cluster — ~2,000 units, MOP 2025–2026
  • Multiple smaller projects across Cheng San, Buangkok, and Punggol border areas

This supply wave is not isolated to Sengkang — the broader D19 (Serangoon, Hougang, Punggol, Sengkang) is experiencing elevated MOP supply across all four towns simultaneously. Buyers in the resale market have choices; sellers face real competition.

New Launch Options Near Sengkang for MOP Upgraders

Sengkang upgraders looking at private property in 2026 have several entry points depending on budget:

  • Executive Condominiums (EC): If household income ≤$16,000, ECs in D19/D27 (Parc Woods, North Gaia) remain available. EC prices ~$1,150–$1,300 PSF — lower entry than fully private.
  • D19 resale condos: Rivervale Crest, A Treasure Trove, Parc Vera — $1,200–$1,450 PSF for 2BR/3BR. Good rental yield, familiar locale.
  • D20 new launches: Bishan/Ang Mo Kio corridor — $1,800–$2,100 PSF, tighter budget stretch but stronger long-term capital appreciation.
  • D28 (Yishun) or D27 (Sembawang): More affordable new launches at $1,400–$1,600 PSF for those prioritising value over prestige.
The timing trap: Sengkang upgraders who wait for a better exit price may find the condo they want has already risen. In 2023–2024, sellers who delayed by 12 months gained ~3–4% on their HDB exit but missed new launches that appreciated 8–12% during the same period. Timing both sides of the trade perfectly is very difficult — focus on the net position, not the individual legs.

Sell First or Buy First? The Bridging Loan Reality

Most Sengkang MOP upgraders should sell first before committing to a private purchase. The reasons:

  • Retaining HDB while buying private triggers 20% ABSD on the private purchase (SC second property rate). On a $1.2M condo, that is $240,000 — a sum that wipes out most of the upgrade math.
  • A couples' ABSD remission pathway exists (sell HDB within 6 months of buying private) but requires confidence in the HDB sale timeline.
  • Bridging loans are available at ~4–5% p.a. to cover the gap between buying and selling — workable for a few months but costly if the HDB sale drags.

The Step-by-Step Sengkang MOP Upgrade Path

Confirm MOP date — log into HDB My Flat Dashboard or check your HDB letter. MOP is exactly 5 years from key collection, not from BTO launch or booking date.
Get a property valuation — commission an HDB-licensed valuer or check recent transacted prices on HDB Resale Portal for your block and floor to anchor your expected exit price.
Run your condo budget — use the table above as a starting point, then get a TDSR and affordability assessment from a mortgage broker or broker before committing.
Decide on new launch vs resale — new launches offer deferred payment (progressive payments) but limited availability; resale condos offer immediacy and negotiability.
List and sell the HDB — once IPA is confirmed for the condo loan, list the HDB. Standard resale timeline is 8–12 weeks from listing to completion. Factor this into your condo exercise date timeline.
