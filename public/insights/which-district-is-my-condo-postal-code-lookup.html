Which District Is My Condo In: Postal Code to District Lookup

Published: 7 September 2026 · By Winfred Quek, Crestbrick Pte Ltd

You are scrolling a listing and it says District 27. You thought the block was in Sembawang. It is, and Sembawang is District 27. The confusion is normal. Singapore does not label property by town on the paperwork that agents, banks and analysts actually use. It labels by one of 28 postal districts, and the district is read off the first two digits of the six digit postal code, not the town name on the sign outside your block.

This matters more than it sounds. A listing titled "Bukit Canberra area" and one titled "District 27" can be the exact same project. If you search only by town name on a portal, or only by district, you can miss half the listings that match what you actually want.

Quick check: take your postal code, read the first two digits, then match them to the table below.

Money: why the district number matters

Banks, valuers and property portals report price per square foot by district, not by estate name. When someone tells you the "district 15 psf" or the "district 9 median", they are pulling a number that groups every project inside that postal sector range, whether it faces the sea or a car park. Two condos five minutes apart by foot can sit in different districts if a boundary road runs between the postal sectors, and their price data gets grouped separately as a result.

Knowing your real district lets you pull the right comparison set. If you compare your unit against the wrong district's transactions, you will misread whether the asking price is fair. This is also how agents and analysts describe a submarket to a buyer over the phone. Saying "district 10" is faster and more precise than listing five estate names, and it matches exactly what shows up in URA and bank valuation reports.

Timing: how this affects your search

When a new launch calendar comes out, it is announced by district first, project name second. If you only track your preferred town by name, you can be a week behind buyers who filter by district number on the same portal alert. During a busy launch window, that lag is the difference between an early viewing slot and a sold out showflat.

The same applies to resale hunting. Most portals let you filter search results by district. If you assume your target estate falls in a district it does not, your filter silently excludes the very listings you are trying to find. Confirm the district from the postal code first, then set your filters.

Common mix ups

Three confusions come up again and again with buyers and tenants.

  • Bukit Canberra and Sembawang: Bukit Canberra sits inside Sembawang, and both are District 27, along with Yishun. Buyers sometimes search for Bukit Canberra separately and miss the wider district 27 comparison set.
  • Bishan: Bishan is District 20, grouped with Ang Mo Kio. It is often lumped in conversation with the Thomson corridor, which actually sits in District 11 and District 26.
  • Seletar: Seletar is its own district, D28, separate from the nearby Yio Chu Kang and Hougang area, which falls under D19.
Watch out: a district number is a postal grouping, not a value judgement. Two projects in the same district can differ sharply in price and condition.

Safety: don't let the district number replace due diligence

The district table is a useful shorthand for search and comparison. It is not a substitute for checking the actual project. Before you rely on a district level price average to judge a specific unit, look up the project's own transaction history on data.gov.sg or through a valuer, and check the exact postal code of the block on its title documents, not just the marketing brochure. Marketing names change over the years, but the postal code does not.

If you are comparing your own flat or condo to district averages for a sale or purchase decision, treat the district figure as a starting point for a conversation with a professional, not a final number. Frequently asked questions

How do I find my district from a postal code?
Take the first two digits of your six digit Singapore postal code and match them against the postal sector table. Each sector range maps to exactly one of the 28 postal districts, labelled D01 to D28.
Why is Bukit Canberra listed as District 27?
Bukit Canberra sits within the Sembawang planning area, and Sembawang's postal sectors fall under District 27, along with Yishun. The estate name and the postal district are two different labelling systems, and District 27 is the correct one for price and listing comparisons.
Does the district number affect property value directly?
The district itself is just a postal grouping and does not set value on its own. What matters for value is the specific project, its condition, tenure and transaction history. The district number is useful for finding comparable transactions, not for pricing a unit by itself.
