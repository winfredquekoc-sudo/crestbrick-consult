CPF housing grant Singapore: maximizing your first timer benefits

By Winfred Quek · 8 minute read · Last reviewed Aug 2026

Quick answer: First time buyers in Singapore can access up to $120,000 in Enhanced CPF Housing Grants (EHG) plus additional family and proximity grants, depending on income and property type. These grants are stackable, credited to your CPF OA, and can be used for downpayment, monthly instalments, and stamp duty. The key is understanding which grants apply to you and claiming them correctly before settlement.

Rates and thresholds change. Verify with HDB and CPF Board before committing.

Facts verified: Aug 2026 · Sources linked below

Key Takeaways

  • The Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG) can reach $120,000 for families earning $1,500 per month or less, applying to both BTO and resale HDB purchases.
  • Family grants and proximity grants are stackable with EHG, potentially bringing total grants to $230,000 or more for eligible resale buyers.
  • Singapore Citizen couples receive higher grant amounts than SC PR couples, and PR PR couples receive no CPF housing grants at all.
  • Both applicants must have worked continuously for 12 months before applying to qualify for most CPF housing grants.
  • Grants are credited to your CPF OA after completion and can pay downpayment, instalments, stamp duty, and legal fees—but not renovation.

Singapore's first timer grant system is deliberately generous, but it is also layered and income tested. Many first timers leave money on the table simply because they don't understand which grants apply to their situation or they don't claim all of them. I've seen buyers save $30,000 to $120,000 by understanding the grant landscape before they start house hunting.

What are the types of CPF housing grants?

There are four main CPF housing grants available to first timers. Each has different income thresholds, applicability to BTO vs resale, and stackability rules. Understanding the layers is essential because they can add up significantly.

Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG)

This is the flagship grant available to both BTO and resale buyers. Families earning $1,500 per month or less receive $120,000. The grant tapers down in tiers as income rises: $100,000 at $3,000 per month, $80,000 at $5,000, $60,000 at $7,000, and $40,000 at the ceiling of $9,000 for 4 room and 5 room flats. The 2 room and 3 room ceilings are lower. Both applicants must have worked continuously for 12 months.

CPF Housing Grant (Family Grant) — resale only

Available exclusively on resale HDB purchases, this grant is stackable with the EHG. Singapore Citizen couples receive up to $80,000 for 4 room and smaller flats, $50,000 for 5 room and larger. SC PR couples receive $10,000 less per tier. No income limit applies to resale purchases, but both applicants must still meet the 12 month employment requirement.

Proximity Housing Grant (PHG)

If you are buying a resale flat to live with your parents or children in the same block, you receive up to $30,000. If you are buying within 4 kilometres of your parents or children but not in the same flat, you receive up to $20,000. This grant is available on both BTO and resale and is stackable with both EHG and the family grant.

Step Up CPF Housing Grant

This grant applies to buyers upgrading from a 2 room Flexi HDB or smaller to a larger flat. $15,000 grant. Most first timers buying a 4 room are not moving up from an existing HDB purchase, so this grant is rarely relevant for pure first timers.

How much will you actually get?

The total depends on your household income, citizenship status, property type, relationship to parents or children, and whether you are buying BTO or resale. Here is a worked example showing how grants stack.

The 12 month employment requirement

Both applicants must have worked continuously for at least 12 months immediately before the HFE application to qualify for most CPF housing grants. This is a strict requirement. If you change jobs, the clock restarts. If you take unpaid leave or have gaps in employment, you may not qualify.

There is some flexibility for self-employed and freelance applicants. HDB uses averaged income over 12 months rather than a payslip, but the continuous engagement requirement still applies. Discuss your specific situation with HDB before applying.

Citizenship matters: SC vs PR vs foreign buyers

Your citizenship status directly affects grant amounts. Singapore Citizen couples receive the highest grants. SC PR couples (one SC, one PR) receive lower grants. PR PR couples receive no CPF housing grants. Foreign nationals cannot buy HDB at all and receive no CPF grants.

If you are a PR couple considering purchasing, you should understand that you are forfeiting all CPF housing grants and will need significantly more cash upfront. This is often the hidden cost of buying as a PR that nobody mentions until you start the application.

How to claim and verify your grants

The process is straightforward but follows a specific order. First, get your HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter. You apply via MyHDBPage with your income documents, employment letters, and CPF statements. HDB will issue an HFE letter showing your eligible grant amounts and maximum loan quantum. The HFE is valid for 9 months. Only after you have the HFE in hand should you start actively hunting for a property.

Once you have exercised the Option to Purchase and committed to a property, you will submit the full application for grant drawdown. After completion, HDB credits the grant to your CPF OA. You then use the CPF balance to pay your downpayment, monthly instalments, stamp duty, and legal fees. The grant never hits your bank account; it stays within the CPF system for property related expenses only.

Winfred's Take

The CPF housing grant is one of the few remaining subsidies in Singapore that rewards ordinary working families. When I review a buyer's numbers, the single biggest driver of affordability is almost always the grant total. Couples who are borderline on affording a property often become very comfortable once grants are credited. The key is knowing your numbers—your household income, your citizenship status, whether you have parents or kids to qualify for proximity—before you fall in love with a listing and start negotiating.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the CPF housing grant 2026?

The Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG) can be up to $120,000 for families earning $1,500 per month or below, tapering down as income rises to the $9,000 per month ceiling. Family grants for resale add up to $80,000 for 4 room and smaller flats. Proximity grants add up to $30,000 if buying near parents or children.

What is the income limit for CPF housing grant?

For BTO flats, the household income ceiling depends on flat type: 2 room and Flexi flats $8,000 per month, 3 room and 4 room flats $14,000 per month, and 5 room flats $14,000 per month. For resale HDB, there is no income ceiling. For private property, no CPF housing grants are available.

Can I claim multiple housing grants at once?

Yes. Grants are stackable. A first timer SC couple buying a resale HDB can receive the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant plus the Family CPF Housing Grant plus the Proximity Housing Grant simultaneously, subject to income eligibility for each grant.

Do I get the housing grant as cash or reduction in price?

The housing grant is credited to your CPF OA account by HDB after the property purchase is completed. You use the OA balance to pay the downpayment, monthly instalments, stamp duty, and legal fees. It is not a direct cash refund but a CPF credit available for property related expenses.

What is the 12 month continuous employment requirement?

Both applicants must have worked continuously for at least 12 months immediately before the application. Job changes or gaps in employment can affect eligibility. Self-employed individuals and freelancers are subject to different income verification rules set by HDB.
