Kallang-Bugis Property Corridor 2026: New Launch vs Resale Along the River

By Winfred Quek · CEA R073319H · PropNex Realty

Quick answer: The Kallang River corridor (D7, D12, D14) is a URA priority transformation zone for 2026–2030. Kampong Bugis — an 8-hectare waterfront site — is Singapore's most anticipated new residential masterplan with ~4,000 homes and first GLS tenders expected 2026–2027. Current new launches in D7 are $2,200–$2,800 PSF. Resale options in D12 and D14 offer a 30–40% discount with strong MRT access.

The Kampong Bugis Masterplan

Kampong Bugis is a 8-hectare waterfront site bounded by the Kallang River, Lavender Street, and Kallang Road. URA's Urban Transformation masterplan designates it for a high-density mixed-use precinct featuring approximately 4,000 residential units alongside parks, a waterfront promenade, and commercial/retail spaces.

Key features of the masterplan:

  • Car-lite precinct with walking/cycling connections to Lavender and Kallang MRT
  • Waterfront public spaces along the Kallang River
  • Mixed residential and commercial programming
  • First GLS tenders expected 2026–2027 — monitor URA's H2 2026 GLS programme

When launched, Kampong Bugis will likely price at $2,500–$3,200 PSF given its CCL adjacency, waterfront positioning, and transformation premium.

Transport Connectivity

  • Circle Line (CCL): Stadium, Kallang, Nicoll Highway stations — direct to Dhoby Ghaut and Harbour Front
  • North-East Line (NEL): Boon Keng station — connects to Dhoby Ghaut and Punggol
  • East-West Line (EWL): Paya Lebar station — serves D14 well, connects to Changi and Jurong
  • Lavender MRT (EWL): Gateway to D7/D12 area

The corridor sits at the intersection of 3 MRT lines — a rare multi-modal node that consistently commands a connectivity premium.

The Kallang Alive Masterplan

URA and Sport Singapore's Kallang Alive masterplan transforms the Singapore Sports Hub precinct into a year-round events and entertainment district. This increases event-driven rental demand — short-term and executive lets near the stadium command premiums on event weekends. Investors targeting expat and executive tenants find this a compelling narrative.

Investor Thesis: Where to Look

For growth investors (5–10 year hold): D7 at Kampong Bugis launch prices when tenders open — early GLS buyers historically benefit from transformation uplift as surrounding masterplan matures.

For yield-focused investors: D12 resale at $1,400–$1,600 PSF offers the best yield-to-price ratio in the corridor, with neighbourhood stability and established tenant demand from the healthcare and education cluster (Tan Tock Seng Hospital, SIM).

For upgraders/own use: D14 post-Geylang rezoning offers newer builds with EWL access and Paya Lebar Quarter amenities at a meaningful discount to D7 prices.
