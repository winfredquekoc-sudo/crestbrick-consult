When Is the Next Sale of Balance Flats Exercise

Published: 7 September 2026 · By Winfred Quek, Crestbrick Pte Ltd

HDB now runs about one Sale of Balance Flats exercise a year, and 2026's ran in February with 4,320 flats offered alongside a BTO launch. As of early September 2026, no second SBF exercise has been confirmed for the rest of the year. Watch HDB's own announcements and MyNiceHome rather than assuming a fixed date, since the cadence has changed from the old twice yearly pattern.

If you missed the February ballot, or applied and were not successful, the natural next question is simple: when do I get another shot. The honest answer starts with how HDB actually runs SBF today, which is different from a few years ago.

Timing: How SBF Actually Works

Sale of Balance Flats offers flats that came back into HDB's hands from earlier BTO exercises, surplus flats from the Selective En Bloc Redevelopment Scheme, and repurchased flats, rather than newly built units designed for a single launch. Because a portion of an SBF batch is often already completed or close to it, successful applicants can sometimes book and collect keys within months, a real contrast to the 3 to 4 year wait typical of a fresh BTO project.

SBF still runs as a ballot, not first come first served. You apply within the exercise window, HDB draws a queue number for successful applicants, and you book a flat according to that number, similar in spirit to BTO but against a different, already existing pool of flats. This is different from Open Booking of Flats, HDB's continuous mode where applicants queue and can book as early as the next working day where flats are available.

Speed is the trade off worth understanding: SBF flats can move faster to key collection than BTO, but the flat mix, unit layout, and location depend entirely on what happened to come back into HDB's inventory that year.

Timing: What Happened in 2026 So Far

HDB launched its February 2026 BTO and SBF exercises together, with 9,012 flats in total and 4,320 of those under SBF. That single exercise appears to have been the year's main SBF window. Since 2024, HDB has generally run one SBF exercise a year rather than the two a year pattern used in 2025 and earlier, so a second SBF launch in the same calendar year is no longer something you can assume by default.

The next confirmed BTO exercise has moved from October to November 2026, planned at around 7,960 flats across Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun, plus Community Care Apartments in Toa Payoh. HDB has set 25 September 2026 as the deadline for HFE supporting documents for buyers planning to ballot in that November exercise. Whether that November launch carries an accompanying SBF component has not been confirmed in HDB's own announcements at the time of writing, so treat any claim that it does as unverified until HDB's own launch details are out.

Do not assume a schedule: the shift to roughly one SBF exercise a year means the safest approach is to check HDB's own news page each quarter rather than expecting a set month.

Money and Safety: How To Prepare Now

Whether the next SBF window lands with the November BTO exercise or later, the preparation is the same, so there is no cost to getting ready early.

  • Get your HFE letter first. A valid HDB Flat Eligibility letter is required before you can apply to any exercise, and processing can take about a month or more during peak periods. Submit early rather than scrambling once a launch is announced.
  • Decide if speed matters more than choice to you. SBF can get you into a flat faster, sometimes within months if the unit is already completed, but you are choosing from what happens to be available that round, not a fresh project designed with a full range of stack and floor options.
  • Keep your documents current. Income statements, CPF details, and any prior housing subsidy history should be ready to resubmit, since an HFE letter has a validity period and stale documents slow down reprocessing.
  • Watch the right channels. HDB's news page and the MyNiceHome portal are where exercise dates and flat details are published, not secondary blogs, however useful those are for context.

If you were not successful in February, nothing about that outcome carries forward automatically to the next exercise. Each SBF ballot is a fresh draw, so your odds this time depend on the flats on offer and how many others apply, not on your history.

One more comparison worth making while you wait is resale. SBF flats are still subsidised, and typically priced below an equivalent resale unit in the same area, but you give up the ability to choose the exact block, floor or facing that a resale purchase allows. If your timeline is tight, for example a lease running out on a current rental or a family event forcing a move, weigh a resale purchase now against waiting for an SBF or BTO window with no confirmed date. There is no universally right answer, only a trade off between price, choice and certainty of timing that only you can weigh against your own situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Sale of Balance Flats exercises happen in a year?

Since 2024, HDB has generally run about one SBF exercise a year, typically alongside a BTO launch, rather than the two a year pattern used before. In 2026, the confirmed SBF exercise ran in February with 4,320 flats offered alongside 4,692 BTO flats.

Is there a second SBF exercise coming later in 2026?

As of early September 2026, HDB has not confirmed a second SBF exercise for the year. The next known launch is the November 2026 BTO exercise of around 7,960 flats, and whether it includes an SBF component has not been announced. Check HDB's own news page for confirmation rather than assuming one.

Is SBF faster than BTO?

Often yes, because SBF offers flats that already exist or are closer to completion, sometimes allowing key collection within months rather than the 3 to 4 years typical of a new BTO project. The trade off is that you choose from whatever flats have come back into HDB's inventory that round, not a purpose planned new project.
