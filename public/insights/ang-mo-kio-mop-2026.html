Why AMK is Underrated

Ang Mo Kio's mature estate premium is often overlooked compared to Bishan (D20 enclave) and Toa Payoh. But three factors make it compelling for 2026 upgraders:

  • Thomson-East Coast Line (TEL): Ang Mo Kio station connects to Orchard in ~12 minutes, Woodlands in ~20 minutes, and future East Coast destinations. TEL premium is still being priced in.
  • Mature amenity ring: AMK Hub (mall + hawker), AMK Market, parks, reputable primary schools (CHIJ St Nicholas, Ai Tong)
  • Limited new supply: D20 has very few new launch condos — most demand channels into resale, supporting prices

How to Calculate Your Net Sale Proceeds: Step-by-Step

Step 1 — Get your HDB valuation. Book an HDB-registered valuer or check recent transactions in your block on HDB Resale Portal. AMK 5-room flats at mid-floors have been transacting at $840K–$940K in Q1 2026.
Step 2 — Pull your CPF statement. Log in to my.cpf.gov.sg → My Statement → Transactions. Download the "CPF used for property + accrued interest" figure. This is what you must refund upon sale.
Step 3 — Check your outstanding HDB loan. Log in to HDB MyFlat Portal → My Flat → Financial Information. Add any outstanding MCST arrears.
Step 4 — Add up your selling costs. Agent commission (~1%), legal fees ($2,000–$3,000), HDB resale levy if applicable (usually $0 for first-timer upgraders), and any outstanding town council arrears.
Step 5 — Net cash = Sale price − HDB loan − CPF refund − selling costs. CPF refund goes back to your OA — it is real money you can redeploy for the next purchase. Cash is the remainder after all deductions.

Common Mistakes AMK Upgraders Make

  • Underestimating CPF accrued interest: If you bought your AMK flat 10 years ago at $450K using $200K CPF, the accrued interest at 2.5% compounds to ~$55K on top — you refund $255K not $200K. Many upgraders are shocked by this figure.
  • Timing: buying before selling. If you purchase a private property first without selling your HDB, you trigger 20% ABSD on the private purchase. You must sell the HDB within 6 months of private purchase. Most AMK upgraders should sell HDB first to avoid ABSD risk.
  • Ignoring the interim rental cost. Selling AMK HDB first means renting during the 3–6 month gap. AMK 4-bedroom rentals run $3,500–$4,500/month. Budget $15K–$25K for this period.
  • Using all CPF for downpayment with nothing left for renovation. Leave at least $50K–$80K for renovation, furniture, and moving costs on the condo purchase.
  • D20 condo "stretch" without stress-testing the mortgage. If your combined household income is $12K/month, a $1.6M mortgage at 30yr = $5,600/month instalment. That is 47% of income — tight under TDSR (55% limit). Get a bank IPA before committing to your target price.

Frequently Asked Questions

When exactly is my AMK BTO MOP date?

MOP is 5 years from the date of key collection (not BTO application date, not estimated possession date). Log in to HDB MyFlat Portal → My Flat → Loan Information to see your exact key collection date. Add exactly 5 years. Note that any period you rented out the entire flat (subletting whole flat) does not count toward MOP.

Do I pay ABSD if I buy a condo before selling my AMK HDB?

Yes. If you complete the purchase of a private property while still owning your HDB flat, you pay 20% ABSD upfront on the private property purchase price. You can claim a refund of this 20% ABSD only after you sell the HDB within 6 months of the private property completion. The refund takes 3–4 months from IRAS. This is a major cash flow burden — most HDB upgraders sell first to avoid it entirely.

Can I rent out my AMK flat after MOP while I search for a condo?

Yes, once your MOP is met, you can rent out the entire flat while you search for a private property. You must sell the flat within 6 months of completing a private property purchase. Rental income from AMK 5-room flats currently runs $3,200–$4,200/month — this can help offset your interim rental costs while you search.

What is the HDB resale levy and does it apply to me?

HDB resale levy applies only if you received a housing grant on your BTO purchase and you intend to buy another subsidised flat (BTO or resale flat with CPF grant) — not if you are upgrading to a private condo. If you are moving from your AMK BTO to a private property, no resale levy is payable.
