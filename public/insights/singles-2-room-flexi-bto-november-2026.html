Singles and 2 Room Flexi in the November BTO

Published: 7 September 2026 · By Winfred Quek, Crestbrick Pte Ltd

Singles aged 35 and above can apply for a 2 room Flexi flat under the new $8,000 income ceiling effective 24 August 2026. HDB has named the November 2026 launch towns as Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun, with roughly 7,960 flats planned, though which specific projects carry 2 room Flexi units is published only at launch.

If you are single, 35 or above, and finally ready to apply, the practical questions are less about policy and more about logistics: do you qualify, where might the flats be, and what should be sitting ready on your desktop before the exercise opens.

Timing: What Is Confirmed for the November Exercise

The next BTO exercise has moved from October to November 2026, planned at around 7,960 flats spread across Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun, along with Community Care Apartments in Toa Payoh. HDB has set 25 September 2026 as the deadline for HFE supporting documents for anyone planning to ballot in this exercise, which is roughly six weeks before the launch itself. Project classifications, unit mix, and pricing are released only at launch, so any specific claim about which project has 2 room Flexi units before that date should be treated as unconfirmed.

What is known versus what is not: the towns are named, the flat count is estimated, but the exact 2 room Flexi allocation per project only appears when HDB opens the exercise.

Money: Eligibility Under the New Ceiling

To apply for a 2 room Flexi flat as a single, you generally need to be a Singapore citizen aged 35 or above, and your income must fall within the applicable ceiling. HDB raised the singles income ceiling to $8,000 a month effective 24 August 2026, the first increase since 2019. If your income was previously just above the old limit, this is the change that may bring you back into eligibility, so it is worth rechecking your numbers against the new figure rather than assuming your earlier assessment still holds.

The age requirement and the flat type itself, 2 room Flexi, were not changed alongside the income ceiling increase. A separate government review into possibly lowering the age below 35 is ongoing, but it has no confirmed date and does not affect this November exercise.

Timing: Which Towns Typically Carry 2 Room Flexi

Historically, 2 room Flexi flats have appeared across both mature and non mature estates, though supply and demand vary widely by location. Rather than guessing which of the six confirmed November towns will include Flexi units and in what quantity, the reliable approach is to check HDB's own launch page once the exercise opens, since project classifications are only released at that point. Use the interim weeks to prepare your paperwork so you are ready to apply the moment listings go live, rather than spending that time speculating on location.

Safety: How Ballot Odds Tend To Compare

This section describes direction only, since exact odds depend on the specific project and are not published in advance. 2 room Flexi flats for singles have generally drawn strong demand relative to their quota, and ballot competitiveness tends to be tighter in mature, centrally located estates and comparatively easier in newer, non mature towns still building up their resident base. Family applicants and singles apply under separate quotas, so a single applicant is not competing directly against a family applicant for the same allocation, but is competing against other eligible singles for the Flexi quota specifically.

No guessing on numbers: ballot odds are not published ahead of an exercise, so treat any specific percentage you see online before launch as unverified.

Safety: What To Prepare Before You Apply

A few practical steps now save a rushed scramble in November.

  • Apply for your HFE letter early. With the 25 September 2026 document deadline for this exercise, and processing sometimes taking about a month or more in peak periods, submit your income statements, CPF details, and identification well ahead of that date.
  • Decide on lease length. 2 room Flexi flats offer shorter lease options pegged to the applicant's age alongside the standard longer lease, at a correspondingly different price. Work out which suits your retirement and CPF plans before the exercise, not during it.
  • Confirm your income against the new ceiling. Reassess your monthly income against the $8,000 figure now in force, especially if you were previously just over the old limit.
  • Watch HDB's own channels. Project details, pricing, and exact flat type availability by project are released at launch on HDB's website and the MyNiceHome portal, not on secondary blogs, however useful those are for background reading.

None of this guarantees a successful ballot, but it removes the avoidable delays, an expired HFE letter, missing documents, an undecided lease length, that turn a winning ballot number into a missed booking window.

It also helps to think past the ballot itself. If you are successful, you will need to move through booking, financing and completion on a timeline set by HDB, so having your CPF and cash position mapped out in advance, alongside a rough view of the monthly instalment at different lease lengths, means you can commit with confidence rather than working the numbers out under pressure once a unit is offered to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a single Singaporean earning $7,500 a month apply for a 2 room Flexi flat now?

Yes, if aged 35 or above and otherwise eligible. HDB raised the singles income ceiling to $8,000 a month effective 24 August 2026, so an income of $7,500 that may have exceeded an older limit now generally falls within the current ceiling. Confirm your specific eligibility with an HDB Flat Eligibility letter.

Which towns will have 2 room Flexi flats in the November 2026 BTO exercise?

The confirmed towns for the November 2026 exercise are Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun, plus Community Care Apartments in Toa Payoh, with around 7,960 flats planned. Which specific projects include 2 room Flexi units, and in what quantity, is released only when HDB opens the exercise.

What should I prepare before the November BTO exercise opens?

Apply for your HDB Flat Eligibility letter well before the 25 September 2026 document deadline, since processing can take about a month or more in peak periods. Also decide on your preferred lease length in advance and recheck your income against the new $8,000 singles ceiling.
