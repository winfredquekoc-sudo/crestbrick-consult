Bendemeer, Kolam Ayer and Whampoa: the quiet central fringe

By Winfred Quek, Associate Marketing Consultant · CEA R073319H · Crestbrick Pte Ltd (L31010886H) · Published 13 July 2026

Quick answer: Bendemeer, Kolam Ayer and Whampoa form a central fringe HDB cluster within the Kallang planning area, sitting between Toa Payoh, Novena and the Kallang River corridor. Newer BTO precincts, particularly around Bendemeer, sit inside an otherwise mature, well served neighbourhood, giving buyers fresh leases without leaving the central region. Boon Keng and Bendemeer MRT on the Downtown Line put the CBD, Bugis and the eastern waterfront within a short ride. Because the area gets less attention than the Kallang River waterfront story next door, it often trades on fundamentals rather than narrative, which can suit a buyer who wants central fringe access without paying for a view.

Facts verified: 13 July 2026 · Development plans and timelines are official targets or estimates, subject to change · Sources attributed below

Ask a hundred property buyers to name a central fringe estate and you will hear Kallang, Novena, Toa Payoh and increasingly the waterfront precincts around the old National Stadium. Fewer will say Bendemeer, Kolam Ayer or Whampoa, even though all three sit inside the same short radius from the city. That gap between reputation and geography is exactly what makes this cluster worth a proper look. As an investor minded advisor, an area that is genuinely central but under discussed is either a mispricing or a red flag, and the honest answer here is closer to the former.

Where this cluster actually sits

Bendemeer, Kolam Ayer and Whampoa fall within the Kallang planning area, bordered by Toa Payoh to the north, Novena to the west, and the Kallang River corridor running south east toward the waterfront and the stadium precinct. It is old Singapore geography, close to Balestier and the original Whampoa estate that gave the area its name, redeveloped in phases over the decades into the mix of older rental blocks, mature resale flats and newer build to order precincts you see today.

Transport access is the part that surprises people who have not looked closely. Boon Keng and Bendemeer stations on the Downtown Line sit inside the cluster, with Novena and Toa Payoh MRT reachable on the western edge. That gives residents a direct line into Bugis, the CBD fringe and onward into the financial district, without the premium typically attached to stations one or two stops closer in.

The newer BTO precincts and what they change

The Bendemeer area in particular has been built out through a series of newer HDB precincts over the past decade, replacing older low rise blocks with taller, better specified developments close to the Downtown Line stations. That matters for two reasons. First, it means a buyer entering through a Bendemeer BTO or a relatively young resale flat gets a long remaining lease inside a genuinely central postal district, a combination that is increasingly rare the closer you get to the city. Second, it has gradually lifted the profile of the surrounding Kolam Ayer and Whampoa precincts, which sit alongside the newer developments and benefit from the same amenities and transport upgrades without themselves being rebuilt.

For a first time buyer weighing where a ballot might actually be winnable versus where competition is fiercest, this cluster sits in an interesting middle ground: central enough to matter for a long term hold, but not yet carrying the ballot pressure of the most talked about central estates. For the general framework on how to weigh a BTO ballot against buying resale immediately, see my HDB BTO versus resale guide.

Proximity to the CBD and Kallang

The single biggest driver of value in this cluster is travel time, not scenery. A short MRT ride puts residents into the CBD fringe and Bugis for work, and an equally short ride or drive reaches the Kallang River corridor, the sports hub and the eastern park connector network for leisure. That dual proximity, work in one direction and recreation in the other, is the kind of positioning that tends to hold up across property cycles because it is structural rather than dependent on any single development completing on schedule.

It also means residents are not dependent on any one precinct's amenities. Toa Payoh's town centre, Novena's medical and retail cluster, and the newer malls and food options growing up around the Kallang waterfront are all within easy reach, which spreads the area's amenity risk across several established nodes rather than concentrating it in one still developing hub.

Who this cluster actually suits

This is not an area I would pitch as an undiscovered goldmine, because that framing rarely survives contact with reality. What I would say is that it suits a specific buyer profile well: someone who wants genuine central fringe travel times, values a long lease over a view, and is not chasing the attention or the price tag that comes with a more heavily marketed waterfront address. It also suits landlords targeting tenants who work in the CBD or the Novena medical cluster and want a short, reliable commute without paying rents pegged to a lifestyle narrative.

Do not confuse under covered with undervalued. Less media attention does not automatically mean a bargain. Some of that quiet is simply because the area is genuinely HDB dominated with limited private stock, which caps how much investor capital flows in regardless of fundamentals. Underwrite the purchase on transport access, lease age and your own use case, not on the assumption that recognition is inevitably coming.

How to think about entry here

  1. Weigh lease age heavily. The value of this cluster leans on long remaining leases in the newer precincts. An older Whampoa or Kolam Ayer block bought purely for price can carry the same lease decay risk as anywhere else in the central region.
  2. Check both MRT lines you can reach. Downtown Line access via Boon Keng and Bendemeer is the core case, but proximity to Novena and Toa Payoh MRT on the western edge adds optionality that some blocks capture better than others.
  3. Compare against Kallang River before committing. If you are choosing between this cluster and the waterfront precinct next door, be honest about whether you are paying for genuine differences in access or simply for a more marketed address.
  4. Match the estate to your holding period. Fundamentals driven areas tend to reward patient holders more than short term flippers chasing narrative driven price spikes.

Frequently asked questions

Where exactly are Bendemeer, Kolam Ayer and Whampoa?

They form a central fringe cluster within the Kallang planning area, sitting between Toa Payoh to the north, Novena to the west and the Kallang River corridor to the south east. Boon Keng and Bendemeer MRT stations on the Downtown Line serve the newer precincts, with Novena and Toa Payoh MRT within reach on the eastern edge of the cluster. It is a short ride into the CBD and a similarly short reach into Kallang and the eastern waterfront.

Is Bendemeer a mature or non mature estate?

Bendemeer is one of the newer HDB precincts built on redeveloped older Whampoa and Kallang land, so it carries fresh leases and modern designs while sitting inside an otherwise mature, established part of the central region. That combination, new flats inside an old, well served neighbourhood, is the core of its property case.

How does this cluster compare to the Kallang River district?

They overlap geographically but are read differently by the market. Kallang River gets more attention for its waterfront redevelopment, sports and lifestyle precinct framing and newer condo launches near the stadium. Bendemeer, Kolam Ayer and Whampoa are quieter, more HDB heavy, and priced more on fundamentals like MRT access and lease age than on a waterfront narrative. For a buyer, that often means less of a premium for the same central fringe convenience.

Is this a good area for a first HDB purchase?

For many first time buyers it is a strong option precisely because it is under covered. You get central fringe travel times to the CBD and Novena, Downtown Line access, and if you can secure a newer BTO or a relatively young resale flat, a long remaining lease, often without paying the attention premium that better marketed central fringe estates command.

Weighing a central fringe purchase?

Whether this cluster or a more marketed neighbour fits your plan depends on your holding horizon, financing and what your portfolio already owns. A Property Portfolio Analysis puts the numbers on the table so you are buying access, not attention.
