Dunearn House location, schools and MRT: the verified facts

By Winfred Quek · 10 minute read · Published 3 July 2026

By Winfred Quek, Associate Marketing Consultant · CEA R073319H · Crestbrick Pte Ltd (L31010886H) · Published 3 July 2026

Quick answer: Dunearn House sits on Dunearn Road (addresses 760–770) in the Swiss Club subzone of D11, Bukit Timah Planning Area, CCR. Sixth Avenue MRT (Downtown Line, DT7) is approximately a 4-minute walk from the Luxury Collection blocks and approximately a 7-minute walk from the Pinnacle Collection blocks. Future Turf City MRT (Cross Island Line Phase 2, CR14) is estimated to open around 2032 at approximately a 7-minute walk. Verified school proximities from the research brief: Methodist Girls’ School Primary (~1,096m straight-line, borderline 1km ballot zone); Raffles Girls’ Primary (~1.8km by road); Pei Hwa Presbyterian Primary (~2.4km). Nanyang Primary School is approximately 3.3km by road and is NOT a 1km school for this development despite claims in some marketing materials.

School distances from the research brief sourced from singaporecondominium.sg, darrenong.sg. Verify all distances directly with MOE School Finder before relying on proximity for school registration.

Location claims for new launches are often written to impress rather than to inform. Walk times get rounded down, school distances get cited in straight-line metres when MOE registration zones use different measurements, and future MRT stations get presented as if they open next month. This guide covers what the verified sources actually say about Dunearn House’s location, and is clear about what is a confirmed fact and what is an estimate. If you are buying partly for school access, the school section of this article may be the most important thing you read before the showflat visit.

The Swiss Club subzone: what it is and why the 33-year gap matters

Dunearn House occupies five addresses on Dunearn Road (760, 762, 766, 768, 770) in the Swiss Club subzone of the Bukit Timah Planning Area. This subzone is classified as part of the Core Central Region (CCR). The boundary sits between the Sixth Avenue residential estate and the Turf City area, which housed the Singapore Turf Club and the Swiss Club before the master plan was gazetted.

The reason this subzone matters for buyers is the supply history. The last non-landed private residential development to launch here was in the early 1990s, approximately 33 years ago. That is not a marketing claim. It is a verifiable fact about the absence of new private housing supply in this specific subzone across three decades. Sixth Avenue MRT (DT7) provides Downtown Line access. Downtown Line stations of note from Sixth Avenue: Botanic Gardens (CC interchange) in one stop south; King Albert Park, Bukit Timah and Beauty World northward; City Hall, Bugis and Bayfront reachable within 12–15 stops. The line is direct and does not require a transfer for CBD commutes.

The Turf City MRT station on the Cross Island Line Phase 2 is approximately a 7-minute walk from Dunearn House according to the research brief (darrenong.sg and decouplingexpertise.sg). The CRL is a structural catalyst not yet in the Dunearn House launch price. When it opens (estimated 2032, subject to LTA), it will add a second line, improving connectivity to Jurong Lake District and the eastern corridor via Bright Hill. This is a genuine long-term access upgrade, but buyers should not present it to themselves as a benefit available at VP in 2030.

Schools: what the brief actually verifies

This section is the most careful in this article because school proximity claims circulate widely for Dunearn House and some of them are wrong. I only report distances that appear in the research brief with sourcing. Every buyer must verify their specific unit’s distance with MOE School Finder and the official Primary 1 registration exercise materials before treating school access as a purchase driver.

Critical school zone note: MOE Phase 2B and 2C Primary 1 ballots use straight-line (Haversine) distance from the registered home address to the school, measured as of 30 June in the year of registration. Road distance is not the relevant metric. However, the 1km ballot band is measured from the child’s registered home address, which is your unit’s postal code, not the development centroid. Distances vary by stack. One block in the development may be within 1km straight-line while another is outside. Verify your specific unit’s distance with MOE School Finder after the showflat visit. Do not rely on generic development-level distances for a school-driven purchase.

The Methodist Girls’ School Primary borderline situation

Multiple sources in the research brief cite MGS Primary at approximately 1,096m straight-line from Dunearn House. That is borderline. At 1,096m, the school is technically beyond the 1km straight-line band for Phase 2B priority, but some stacks within the development may be within 1km and others outside, depending on the specific unit’s location on the site. The 1.7km figure cited by singaporecondominium.sg is a road distance, which is not the MOE-relevant measurement. The honest position is: check your specific unit against MGS Primary on MOE School Finder. Do not assume a 1km placement. Do not rule it out either. This is a buyer-specific verification item, not a development-level yes or no.

The Nanyang Primary error in marketing materials

Several marketing sites claim Nanyang Primary School is within 1km of Dunearn House. This appears to be an error, likely a conflation of Nanyang Primary School (actual primary school, approximately 3.3km by road on Nanyang Road in the Jurong area) with Nanyang Girls’ High School (a secondary school on Bukit Timah Road, approximately 1km away). These are different institutions. Do not cite Nanyang Primary as a 1km school for Dunearn House on the basis of marketing material. The research brief flags this explicitly as a probable error.

The Turf City masterplan: what gets built and when

The Turf City masterplan is the long-duration case for the precinct. The key facts from the research brief: 176 hectares, planned for 15,000–20,000 new homes over 20–30 years. The development will include HDB flats (the first new HDB supply in the Bukit Timah area in approximately 40 years), private residential units, retail, schools, healthcare facilities, park connector networks and heritage conservation of 22 structures including the Grandstand.

For buyers planning to move into Dunearn House at VP in 2030, the honest picture is this: the CRL Turf City MRT station will not yet be open; the retail and commercial component of the masterplan will be in early stages; and significant construction activity for infrastructure, the MRT station and adjacent residential projects will be visible from the development. The masterplan is genuinely transformative for the precinct over 20–30 years, but buyers who need a fully formed neighbourhood in 2030–2032 will need to be comfortable with a construction environment.

The second GLS site (Plot 2), approximately 330 units, is expected to launch in 2H 2027 and complete around 2031–2032. From VP to approximately 2035, Dunearn House residents will live adjacent to at least one active construction site. That is a lived-environment consideration, not just an investment variable.

What the location means for different buyer types

For school-zone buyers

MGS Primary at a borderline 1,096m straight-line is the headline. Raffles Girls’ Primary at approximately 1.8km by road is a Phase 2C distance (outside 1km). If school zone is the primary purchase driver, verify your specific unit’s straight-line distance to the target school using MOE School Finder before committing to a ballot. For a broader guide to using school catchment in a property purchase strategy, see school catchment and property strategy in Singapore.

For MRT-access buyers

Sixth Avenue MRT (DT7) is operational today and gives direct Downtown Line access to Botanic Gardens, City Hall, Bugis and Bayfront. The future CRL Turf City MRT adds a second line by approximately 2032. For buyers who drive, Dunearn Road and Bukit Timah Expressway access gives reasonable connectivity to Orchard and the CBD. For buyers who rely entirely on MRT, the 4–7 minute walk to Sixth Avenue is the operative reality for at least the first 5–6 years after VP.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Dunearn House from Sixth Avenue MRT?

Approximately a 4-minute walk from the closer 10-storey Luxury Collection blocks (2BR and 3BR at 766, 768 and 770 Dunearn Road) and approximately a 7-minute walk from the 19-storey Pinnacle Collection blocks (4BR at 760 and 762 Dunearn Road). Both figures reflect easy walkability; the difference depends on which block you are in.

When does the Turf City MRT open?

The Turf City MRT (Cross Island Line Phase 2, CR14) is estimated to open around 2032. It is approximately a 7-minute walk from Dunearn House. The station is not yet operational and the opening date is subject to LTA confirmation. It adds a second line to the precinct but is not available at VP in 2030.

Is Methodist Girls’ School Primary within 1km of Dunearn House?

The straight-line distance from Dunearn House to MGS Primary is approximately 1,096m according to multiple research sources, placing it borderline within the 1km Phase 2B ballot band. Road distance is approximately 1.7km (not the MOE metric). Buyers relying on MGS Primary proximity must verify their specific unit’s straight-line distance with MOE School Finder before using this as a purchase rationale.

Is Nanyang Primary within 1km of Dunearn House?

No. Nanyang Primary School is approximately 3.3km by road. Claims that it is within 1km appear to confuse it with Nanyang Girls’ High School (a secondary school). Do not rely on this claim without verifying directly with MOE.
