HLE vs HFE Letter: Is There Still a Difference

Published: 7 September 2026 · By Winfred Quek, Crestbrick Pte Ltd

No, not anymore. The HDB Loan Eligibility, or HLE, letter no longer exists. HDB replaced it with the HDB Flat Eligibility, or HFE, letter, which covers flat eligibility, CPF grants and loan eligibility together in one letter, instead of assessing only the loan. An old forum thread mentioning the HLE is describing a retired step.

Safety: what changed and when

HDB announced the new HDB Flat Eligibility letter on 28 April 2023, and it took effect for flat applications from 9 May 2023. Before that date, a buyer applied separately for an HLE letter mainly to check housing loan eligibility, then dealt with flat eligibility and grant questions through other channels along the way. The HFE letter folded all of that into a single upfront assessment, so a buyer knows their flat eligibility, grant amount and loan eligibility before they start looking seriously at flats, rather than discovering a problem partway through.

If you have not applied for an HDB flat since before mid 2023, the process you remember is not the process that exists today. There is no HLE letter to apply for anymore. Every applicant now goes through the HFE letter, whether they are buying a Build To Order flat, a resale flat, or applying for the Sale of Balance Flats exercise.

The short version: HLE checked your loan alone. HFE checks your flat eligibility, your grants and your loan together, in one letter, before you commit to a specific flat.

Safety: what the HFE letter actually covers now

The HFE letter is broader than its predecessor in three specific ways.

Area assessed Old HLE letter Current HFE letter
Flat purchase eligibility Not covered directly Covered, including citizenship and family nucleus conditions
CPF Housing Grants Not covered directly Estimated grant amount included
Loan eligibility Covered, HDB loan only Covered, with an indication of both HDB loan and bank loan options

In practice this means a single application now tells a buyer whether they even qualify to buy the type of flat they want, how much grant support they can expect, and roughly how much they can borrow. That is a meaningfully different starting point from the old process, where a clean HLE approval did not guarantee anything about flat eligibility or grants.

Timing: validity and when to apply

The HFE letter's validity period was extended from six months to nine months, a change HDB made in November 2023 to give buyers more breathing room during their flat search. Apply for the HFE letter before you start seriously viewing resale flats or before you take part in a Build To Order or Sale of Balance Flats exercise, since you generally need a valid HFE letter in hand at key steps such as submitting a resale application or booking a flat.

If your circumstances change materially during the validity period, such as a change in income, family nucleus or citizenship status, HDB may review and revise your HFE letter. It is worth checking HDB's own guidance on when a review is triggered rather than assuming an approved letter is fixed for its full nine months regardless of what changes in your situation.

Do not let it lapse mid search: If your HFE letter expires before you complete your purchase, you will need to reapply. Track the expiry date against your flat hunting timeline so it does not catch you out right before you are ready to commit.

Timing: what this means if you are starting today

If you are only now beginning your HDB purchase journey and picked up the term HLE from an older article, a relative's experience, or a forum thread, drop it. Apply directly for the HFE letter through HDB's website as your first concrete step, before you start comparing specific flats or resale listings. It will tell you, in one document, whether you are eligible for the flat type you want, what grants you may receive, and what your loan options look like, which is a far more complete starting point than the old loan only letter ever gave buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I still need to apply for an HLE letter before buying an HDB flat?

No. The HLE letter has been retired. Since 9 May 2023, all buyers apply for the HDB Flat Eligibility, or HFE, letter instead, which covers flat eligibility, grants and loan eligibility in one application.

How long is an HFE letter valid for?

The HFE letter is valid for nine months. HDB extended the validity period from six months to nine months in November 2023 to give buyers more time to complete their flat search.

What is different about the HFE letter compared to the old HLE letter?

The old HLE letter mainly checked eligibility for an HDB housing loan. The HFE letter is broader, covering flat purchase eligibility including citizenship and family nucleus conditions, an estimate of CPF Housing Grants, and loan eligibility for both an HDB loan and a bank loan, all in a single letter.
