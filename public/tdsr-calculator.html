How the Numbers Work

TDSR
55% of gross monthly income
Total Debt Servicing Ratio. All monthly debt obligations (including the new mortgage, stress-tested) must not exceed 55% of gross income. This is a hard MAS limit for bank loans.
MSR
30% (HDB & EC only)
Mortgage Servicing Ratio. For HDB and EC bank loans, the mortgage instalment alone must not exceed 30% of gross income. A tighter constraint than TDSR for these property types.
Stress Test Rate
4.0% p.a.
MAS requires banks to stress test affordability at a minimum floor rate of 4.0% p.a. regardless of the actual prevailing loan rate.
Reference Rate (instalment estimate)
3.5% p.a.
The estimated monthly instalment shown uses 3.5% p.a. as a reference (current market ballpark). The max loan calculation uses the 4.0% stress test rate.
LTV (Loan-to-Value)
75% / 45% / 35%
For bank loans: 75% LTV for 1st property, 45% for 2nd, 35% for 3rd+. The down payment floor (5% cash + remainder via CPF) applies for 1st property.
Down Payment Breakdown
1st property: 25% min
At least 5% must be in cash (OTP exercise). The remaining 20% can come from CPF OA. For 2nd property and above, 25% cash downpayment is required (no CPF for down payment on 2nd+).

Frequently asked questions

What does the TDSR calculator actually compute?

It calculates your TDSR ceiling at 55 percent of gross income, subtracts existing debts to find your headroom, then derives the maximum loan that fits using the 4 percent stress rate and your tenure. It also applies the LTV limit for your property count and runs the 30 percent MSR check when the purchase is an HDB flat or EC.

Why does it use 4 percent for the loan but 3.5 percent for the instalment?

The maximum loan is sized against the MAS 4 percent stress rate floor, because that is the rate banks must underwrite affordability at. The estimated monthly instalment is shown at a 3.5 percent reference rate as a current market ballpark, giving you a closer sense of likely real payments while the loan ceiling stays conservatively stress tested.

How do the LTV limits change with property count?

The tool applies 75 percent LTV for a first housing loan, 45 percent for a second and 35 percent for a third or more, in line with MAS limits. A higher property count means a larger cash and CPF down payment and a smaller loan, so your binding constraint can shift from TDSR to the LTV cap.

When does MSR apply instead of just TDSR?

MSR applies on top of TDSR only for HDB flats and Executive Condominiums bought with a bank loan. It caps the housing instalment alone at 30 percent of gross income, which is usually stricter than the 55 percent TDSR limit. For these property types the calculator shows both checks, and the tighter one governs your maximum loan.

How much cash do I need for the down payment?

For a first property the down payment is at least 25 percent, of which a minimum of 5 percent must be cash paid at the option stage, with the remaining 20 percent payable from CPF Ordinary Account or cash. For a second or subsequent property the cash component is larger. The calculator estimates the cash and CPF split for you.
