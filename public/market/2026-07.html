HDB resale market recap: July 2026

Quick answer: 2,654 HDB resale transactions were recorded across 26 towns in July 2026. The island wide median resale price was S$630,000 (+0.8% versus June 2026), with a median of S$6,407 per square metre. Figures below are computed directly from data.gov.sg HDB resale transaction records.

Observations

Island wide, the median HDB resale price rose 0.8% to S$630,000 in July 2026, across 2,654 transactions in 26 towns, against S$625,000 in June 2026.

Marine Parade recorded the largest median price rise among towns with at least 5 transactions in both months, up 67.3% to S$950,000 across 17 transactions, from S$568,000 in June 2026.

Jurong East recorded the largest median price fall among towns with at least 5 transactions in both months, down 16.5% to S$459,000 across 46 transactions, from S$550,000 in June 2026.

Observations are descriptive only, computed directly from the transaction counts and medians shown on this page. They are not predictions and should not be read as advice.

Note on method: each town's median price is taken across whatever mix of flat types (2 room to executive) actually transacted that month. A town with few transactions can show a large swing simply because the mix shifted, for example more 5 room flats changing hands one month than the next, rather than because like for like prices moved. Treat single month, low volume swings with that in mind.
