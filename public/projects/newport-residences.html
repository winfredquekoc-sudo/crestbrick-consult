What the review found

Newport Residences is a genuinely scarce structural proposition: freehold tenure inside the CBD, where most land has been released under 99 year leasehold Government Land Sales terms for decades. The review frames the case as long horizon and capital preservation rather than yield, since psf pricing sits above nearby resale CBD stock, units start from the 23rd floor, and the developer has not disclosed an expected rental yield. Structural tailwinds cited include the URA's CBD Incentive Scheme and the Greater Southern Waterfront masterplan. CDL is one of Singapore's largest, most established listed developers, which the review scores as a genuine safety strength.
