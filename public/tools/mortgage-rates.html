How to read this

  • Fixed 2yr = locked rate for 24 months, then reverts to floating (usually SORA+spread).
  • SORA 3M = current 3-month Singapore Overnight Rate Average, the benchmark most floating packages price off.
  • Aggregator sites often show promo rates for new launches or restrictive quantum, always verify with the bank directly before committing.
