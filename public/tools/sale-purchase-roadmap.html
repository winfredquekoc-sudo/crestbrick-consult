Frequently asked questions

What routes does the Sale & Purchase Roadmap cover?

Selling an HDB only, selling private only, buying an HDB resale only, buying a private resale only, or four combined moves: upgrading (sell HDB, buy private), moving HDB to HDB, moving private to private, and right sizing (sell private, buy HDB resale).

What happens to ABSD if I buy before selling my existing home?

You pay it upfront in cash: 20% for a Singapore Citizen's second property, 30% for a PR, 60% for a foreigner. A married couple with at least one Singapore Citizen can claim it back only if the existing home is sold, meaning the buyer has exercised their OTP, within 6 months of the date you exercised the purchase OTP. Singles and non married co owners get no remission.

What is the safest sequence to avoid ABSD altogether?

Sell first, then buy. You never touch ABSD and know your exact proceeds before committing, but you need to line up interim housing, for example a Temporary Extension of Stay of up to 3 months, or renting.

What is the current Seller's Stamp Duty schedule for property bought after 4 July 2025?

SSD applies within 4 years: 16% in year 1, 12% in year 2, 8% in year 3, and 4% in year 4. Property bought before 4 July 2025 uses the older 3 year window at 12%, 8% and 4%.

Has the HDB wait out rule changed for buying a resale flat after selling private property?

Since 28 July 2026 there is no wait out period for buying a non subsidised resale flat without an HDB housing loan. A 30 month wait out still applies if you take an HDB housing loan, claim CPF housing grants, or buy a subsidised flat or a new EC. If you still own private property when the resale completes, you must dispose of it within 6 months.
