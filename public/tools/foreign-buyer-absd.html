Since 27 Apr 2023, foreign buyers pay 60% ABSD on every Singapore residential purchase, on top of BSD. That's an extra S$1.2M on a S$2M property. This page tells you the real cost, and the legitimate alternatives.

Alternatives if you're a foreigner

1. Marry an SC/PR (joint purchase as occupier). ABSD on the household is determined by the highest-tier profile. SC + Foreigner = SC's 0% on 1st property (but only if structure is right, get this wrong and full 60% applies).

2. Buy under a Singapore corporate vehicle (Pte Ltd). Trust + corporate rate is 65% (yes, higher), but this only works for specific commercial scenarios. Generally NOT a workaround for residential.

3. Sengkang/Tengah landed (PR exception). Some FTAs exempt nationals (US, Switzerland, Norway, Liechtenstein, Iceland) from ABSD entirely on residential. If you're from one of these countries, you pay SC rates.

4. Wait for PR. PR (1st property) ABSD is 5%. The 55pp delta = a meaningful reason to apply.

5. Commercial/industrial property. No ABSD. But yields are different + tenant risk profile differs.
