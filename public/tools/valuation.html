Frequently asked questions

How does the instant HDB valuation estimate work?

The tool groups the last six full months of HDB resale transactions from data.gov.sg by town, flat type and storey band, then works out the median price per square metre for each group. It multiplies that median, and the surrounding p25 to p75 range, by the floor area you enter to give an indicative price and range for your flat.

Why is the result a range instead of one number?

Flats in the same town, flat type and storey band still differ by block, facing, renovation and exact floor, so transacted prices per square metre naturally spread out. The range shown runs from the 25th to the 75th percentile of that spread, which is a more honest picture than a single average.

How accurate is this compared to a formal valuation?

This is an indicative estimate built purely from public resale transaction data. It does not see your exact block, unit condition, renovation or remaining lease, so treat it as a starting point, not a formal valuation. Winfred Quek prepares a detailed, personally reviewed valuation report for your exact unit within 24 hours on request.

What happens if my town or flat type combination shows no data?

Where a town, flat type and storey band combination had fewer than five transactions in the last six months, the tool holds back an estimate rather than showing an unreliable figure. Try a different storey band, or WhatsApp Winfred directly for a personally researched read on your flat.
