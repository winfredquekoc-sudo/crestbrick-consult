Free Money, Timing & Safety Self-Assessment

Property Progression Score

Answer 14 quick questions across Money, Timing and Safety. Get your score out of 100 and a point by point breakdown instantly. No sign up, no gate.

01 Money — can you afford it: equity, CPF, cash, income, TDSR headroom

1. How would you describe the equity in your current property (value minus outstanding loan)?

2. What is your combined CPF Ordinary Account and Special Account balance available for property or investment?

3. How many months of expenses does your cash savings buffer, outside CPF, cover?

4. Beyond property and CPF, do you hold other liquid assets (stocks, bonds, unit trusts, fixed deposits)?

5. How would you describe your household income stability?

6. Roughly how much headroom do you have under the TDSR 55% cap?

7. Do you carry other regular commitments (car loan, personal loan, credit card balances, family support)?

8. How would you describe your monthly budget after all commitments, including dependents?

02 Timing — current property, timeline, upgrade intent

9. What best describes your current property situation?

10. What is your timeline for your next property move, if any?

11. How clear is your progression pathway (for example HDB to EC to private, or hold versus upgrade versus right size)?

03 Safety — rate exposure, lock in, insurance, contingency

12. Where do you stand on your mortgage rate and lock in period?

13. How would you describe your insurance coverage tied to the property (mortgage reducing term, life, critical illness)?

14. If mortgage rates rose by 1 to 2 percentage points, how would your household be affected?
