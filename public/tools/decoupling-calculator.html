How to read these results

WORTH DECOUPLING

Net benefit above S$15,000. The ABSD savings comfortably outweigh the transfer costs. Decoupling is financially compelling.

MARGINAL

Net benefit between S$1 and S$15,000. The numbers work, but only just. Consider other factors: loan impact, CPF, timeline.

NOT WORTH IT

Transfer costs exceed ABSD savings. Other strategies (e.g. one spouse absorbs ABSD, or delaying the second purchase) may be better.
