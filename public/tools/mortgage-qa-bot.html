Frequently asked questions

What kinds of mortgage questions can this tool answer?

It handles common Singapore home loan topics: the difference between TDSR and MSR, how SORA pegged packages work, when refinancing makes sense, how lock in penalties are calculated and the fixed versus floating trade off. It draws on current SORA and bank rate context plus the playbook Winfred Quek uses with clients.

Are the answers personalised financial advice?

No. The tool gives general educational answers based on prevailing rules and market data, not tailored financial or mortgage advice. Your actual loan outcome depends on your income, existing debts, property count and the bank's own credit assessment. For a decision specific to your numbers, message Winfred Quek directly through the WhatsApp link.

How does the tool explain TDSR versus MSR?

It explains that TDSR caps your total monthly debt, including the new mortgage, at 55 percent of gross income and applies to all property types. MSR is a tighter 30 percent cap on housing loan instalments alone and applies only to HDB flats and Executive Condominiums. For HDB and EC, both checks must be satisfied.

Can it tell me whether I should refinance now?

It can walk you through the factors that matter, such as your current rate versus prevailing packages, whether you are still inside a lock in period and how soon repricing or refinancing is worthwhile. It gives general guidance rather than a verdict on your specific loan. For a tailored call, reach Winfred Quek on WhatsApp.

What happens if the tool cannot answer my question?

If a question falls outside its scope or needs your personal figures, the tool points you to message Winfred Quek directly on WhatsApp for a human answer. Edge cases like complex restructuring, decoupling interactions or unusual income structures are usually better handled in a direct conversation than by an automated tool.
