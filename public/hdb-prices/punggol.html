Punggol HDB resale prices 2026

Quick answer: In Punggol, HDB resale flats typically sold for $673,543 across all flat types, weighted by transaction volume, from February 2026 to July 2026. Prices ranged from $391,688 for a 2 Room flat to $836,944 for a Executive flat, based on 825 resale transactions. Source: data.gov.sg HDB Resale Flat Prices.

By Winfred Quek · CEA R073319H · Data updated 2026-08-05

“Typical price” is the transaction count weighted average of the median resale price recorded in each floor band (01 to 06, 07 to 12, 13 and above) for that group. It is not a pooled median across individual transactions, the source data only provides a median per floor band, not a full transaction list.

What the numbers show

  • The 4 Room flat type was the most transacted in Punggol from February 2026 to July 2026, with 416 resale transactions.
  • Typical resale prices in Punggol ranged from $391,688 for a 2 Room flat to $836,944 for a Executive flat over the same period.
  • 2 Room flats recorded the highest median price per square metre in Punggol at $8,566 psm, against $6,513 psm for Executive flats.

Frequently asked questions

What is the typical HDB resale price in Punggol?

Across all flat types in Punggol, the transaction count weighted typical resale price was $673,543 from February 2026 to July 2026, based on 825 resale transactions recorded on data.gov.sg.

Which HDB flat type sells most often in Punggol?

The 4 Room flat type had the most resale transactions in Punggol from February 2026 to July 2026, with 416 recorded transactions and a typical price of $679,880.

What is the price per square metre for a 4 Room flat in Punggol?

A 4 Room flat in Punggol had a typical price of $7,320 psm from February 2026 to July 2026, the transaction count weighted average across the floor bands with sufficient data (01 to 06, 07 to 12, 13 and above).
