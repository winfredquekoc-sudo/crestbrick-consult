What's in the report

Three things you need before you list, not after.

Recent transacted prices in your stack and development, not a district-wide average. Floor, facing, and stack all move the number.

A range you can actually defend at negotiation, anchored to what buyers are paying right now, not what you'd like to hear.

Whether now genuinely makes sense: your equity and CPF position, your SSD and market-cycle timing, and how exposed you'd be to rate or income shocks either way.
