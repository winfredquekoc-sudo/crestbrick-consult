Good Class Bungalow

For Singapore Citizens acquiring or selling within the 39 GCB Areas. S$15M+ entry, 1,400 sqm minimum land, restricted ownership. Discreet engagement; minimal public listings.

The market mechanics

  • ~2,800 GCB plots across 39 designated areas (Nassim, Cluny, Bin Tong, Eden Hall, Tanglin, etc)
  • SC-only ownership; PR + foreigner exception by special LDAU approval (rare, slow)
  • Min 1,400 sqm land, two storeys, 35% plot ratio max
  • Transactions ~50/year market-wide; ~3-5 per quarter "active"
  • Discreet, most transactions never appear on PG/99.co

How I work GCB

  • Off-market network, most viable transactions never list publicly
  • NDA standard from first conversation
  • Co-broke with established GCB specialists where appropriate
  • Tax + estate-planning coordinated with your existing advisors
  • Land-cost vs total-house-cost framework, many GCB buyers conflate these
